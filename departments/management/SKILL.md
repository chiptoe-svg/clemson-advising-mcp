# Management — Department Advising

**Programs this department owns in the catalog:** "Management, BS".

**No department advising policy has been recorded in this document yet.** That
is a statement about this document, not about the department: rules may exist
that simply have not been written down here. What IS already covered:

- **Course-level slot rules are data, not prose.** Faculty-approved additions
  and denials for requirement slots ride on `get-requirement-rules` as
  `advisor_courses` / `advisor_denies`, served live. Read them there.
- **The advising method** — degree-audit walk, counting rules, prerequisite
  checks, planning — is the `advising-method` skill and applies to this
  department unchanged.
- **The shared freshman year** is the `pre-business-advising` skill.

When this department records policy — internship expectations, approval
workflows, scheduling lore, declared-major rules beyond the catalog's — it
belongs HERE, so every advising agent fetches one authoritative copy. Until
then, direct department-policy questions to the department's human advisors
rather than guessing.
