Entrepreneurship Minor

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Entrepreneurship requires ELE 3010 and 12 additional credits selected from ECON 3210, ELE 3020, ELE 3030, ELE 3210, ELE 4010, ELE 4020, ELE 4030, ELE 4990, MGT 4220, MGT 4400, and MGT 4970.

 

NOTE: This minor is not open to Management majors.
