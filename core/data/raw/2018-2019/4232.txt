Anthropology Minor

(18 credits minimum)

A minor in Anthropology requires ANTH 2010 and at least six credits selected from ANTH 3010, ANTH 3310, ANTH 3510 or LANG 3710. Nine additional credits may be selected from any other Anthropology courses, but at least three credits must be taken at the 4000 level. No more than three credits of ANTH 4960 may be counted toward the minor.
