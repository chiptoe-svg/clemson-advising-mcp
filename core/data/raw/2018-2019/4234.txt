Art Minor

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Art requires ART 1030, ART 1510 or ART 1520; AAH 1010, AAH 1020 or ART 2100; at least six credits of ART or AAH courses at the 2000 level or above; and at least three credits of ART or AAH courses at the 3000 or 4000 level.

 

NOTE: Nine credits at the 3000 or 4000 level are not required for a minor in Art, as approved by the University Undergraduate Curriculum Committee 2010.
