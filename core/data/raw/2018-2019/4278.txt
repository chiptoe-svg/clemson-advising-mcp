Middle Eastern Studies Minor

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Middle Eastern Studies requires 15 credits as follows: three credits from Group I, three credits from Group II, and nine additional credits selected from any courses listed in Group I or II or another approved course. Middle Eastern Studies minors are encouraged to learn languages native to the region. On a case-by-case bases, students with intermediate level or higher proficiency in a Middle Eastern language may earn credit for their language skills. Contact the director of Middle Eastern Studies for more information.

Group I - Religion and Culture: ENGL 3540, REL 3030, REL 3060, REL 3150, REL 3510 
Group II - History and Politics: HIST 3510, HIST 3520, HIST 3960, HIST 3970, HIST 4720, HIST 4880, POSC 4760
