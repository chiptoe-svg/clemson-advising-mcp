Japanese Studies Minor

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Japanese Studies requires 15 credits of 3000- and 4000-level JAPN courses, including at least one 4000-level literature course. JAPN 4010, JAPN 4170 and JAPN 4990 may not be used to satisfy requirements for the Japanese Studies minor.

 

NOTE: Language and International Trade majors are not eligible to receive a minor in the same modern language as their concentration area language.
