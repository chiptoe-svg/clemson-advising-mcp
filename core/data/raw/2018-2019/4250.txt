Economics Minor

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Economics requires ECON 3140, ECON 3150, and nine additional credits from economics courses numbered 3000 or higher. Please note that ECON 3140 and ECON 3150 have ECON 2110 and ECON 2120 as prerequisites. Some 3000-level elective classes may have additional prerequisites. See course descriptions for detailed prerequisite information.
