Packaging Science Minor

(19 credits minimum)

A minor in Packaging Sciences requires PKSC 1020, PKSC 2020, PKSC 2040 and PKSC 2060; and at least nine credits selected from FDSC 4010, FDSC 4020, FOR 4410, FOR 4420, GC 4060, PKSC 3200, PKSC 3680, PKSC 4010, PKSC 4040, PKSC 4160, PKSC 4200, PKSC 4300, PKSC 4400, PKSC 4540 and PKSC 4640.
