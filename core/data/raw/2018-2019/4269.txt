Horticulture Minor

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Horticulture requires HORT 1010 and 12 additional credits of horticulture courses (excluding HORT 4080 and HORT 4710), nine credits of which must be at the 3000 level or higher. HORT 2710 is highly recommended.

 

NOTE: This minor is not open to Turfgrass majors.
