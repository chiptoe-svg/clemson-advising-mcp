Screenwriting Minor

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Screenwriting requires 15 credits in ENGL above the sophomore level as follows: ENGL 3480, ENGL 3570 and ENGL 4480 (six credits); and one of the following: ENGL 4500, ENGL 4510, ENGL 4520, ENGL 4530 or THEA 3470.
