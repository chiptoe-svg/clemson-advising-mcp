German Studies Minor

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in German Studies requires 15 credits of 3000- and 4000-level GER courses, including at least one 3000- or 4000-level literature course and one 3000- or 4000-level film or culture course.

 

NOTE: Language and International Trade majors are not eligible to receive a minor in the same modern language as their concentration area language.
