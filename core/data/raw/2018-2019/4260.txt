French Studies Minor

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in French Studies requires 15 credits of 3000- and 4000-level FR courses, including FR 3050 and at least one 4000-level literature course (select from FR 4000, FR 4100, FR 4120, FR 4200, or FR 4990). FR 4380 and FR 4390 may not be used to satisfy requirements for the French Studies minor.

 

NOTE: Language and International Trade majors are not eligible to receive a minor in the same modern language as their concentration area language.
