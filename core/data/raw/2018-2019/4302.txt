Sustainability Minor

(18 credits minimum)

A minor in Sustainability requires 18 credits, including CU 2010 and 15 additional credits selected from AGM 3010, AGRB 3570, AGRB 4570*, ARCH 4250, ARCH 4710*, ARCH 4720*, BE 4240, BE 4400, BE 4640, BIOL 2040*, BIOL 3130, BIOL 4410, BIOL 4860, BT 2200, BT 2400, CE 4120, CE 4360, CE 4370, CE 4400, CHE 4500, COMM 3070*, ECE 4200, ECE 4570, ECE 4610, ECE 4710, ECON 3190, EES 4860, ENGL 4340*, ENR 1010, ENR 3120*, ENR 4130, ENR 4290*, ENR 4500*, ENSP 1250, ENSP 2000, ENSP 2010, ENSP 3150, ENSP 4000*, ENSP 4720, ENT 3000, ETOX 4000, ETOX 4210, ETOX 4370, ETOX 4460, ETOX 4470, ETOX 4850, FOR 4230, FOR 4340, FOR 4510, FOR 4650, GEOL 1120, GEOL 1140, GEOL 1200, GEOL 1250, GEOL 2700*, GEOL 3000, GEOL 4090*, HIST 1240*, HIST 3920*, HON 2060 (when the course covers sustainable energy innovation or experimental forest topics), HORT 1010, HORT 3080, HORT 3090, HORT 4560*, HORT 4610*, LARC 4230*, ME 4200, ME 4260, MSE 4330, PES 3150, PES 3350, PES 4080, PES 4220, PES 4230, PES 4450*, PES 4900, PHIL 3260*, PHIL 3400*, PHIL 3450*, PHSC 1070, PHYS 2450, PHYS 4200, PKSC 3680*, POSC 4160*, PRTM 4300*, RS 4010*, SOC 4030*, SOC 4330*, SOC 4590*, SOC 4710*, STS 2150*, WFB 3130, WFB 4180 and WFB 4300. At least nine credits must be selected from 3000- or 4000-level courses. At least three and no more than nine credits must be from courses addressing the social dimension of sustainability. These courses are identified by an asterisk in the list above. Creative Inquiry, study abroad, independent research, capstone project, or other courses that focus on sustainability issues may be submitted through CU 2010 instructors for approval. 

For more information about this minor, please contact David Freedman.
