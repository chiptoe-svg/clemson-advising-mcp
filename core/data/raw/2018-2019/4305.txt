Turfgrass Minor

(16 credits minimum)

A minor in Turfgrass requires HORT 2120, HORT 4120, PES 2020, and two of the following: AGM 4020, HORT 4330, PLPA 4060.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Horticulture majors.
