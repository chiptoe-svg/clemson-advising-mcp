Russian Area Studies Minor

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Russian Area Studies requires 15 credits, three of which must be in Russian language courses at the 2000 level or above. The remaining 12 credits are distributed as follows:

Group I: Three credits selected from RUSS 3070, RUSS 3400, RUSS 3600, RUSS 3610, RUSS 3980 and RUSS 4600 
Group II: Three credits selected from HIST 3850, HIST 3860, HIST 3870 and HIST 4940 
Group III: Three credits of POSC 4710 
Group IV: Three additional credits selected from any of the courses listed above.
