Legal Studies Minor

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Legal Studies requires 15 credits at the 3000 or 4000 level selected from any LAW courses, or COMM 4300, COMM 4310, ECON 4020, ENR 4290, HIST 3190, HIST 3280, HIST 3290, HIST 4960, HLTH 4780, JUST 4680, PHIL 3210, PHIL 3430, POSC 4360, POSC 4370, POSC 4380 or POSC 4470.
