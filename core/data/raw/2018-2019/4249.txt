East Asian Studies Minor

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in East Asian Studies requires 15 credits, at least six of which must be at the 4000 level, distributed as follows: three credits from Group I; six additional credits selected from either Group I or from Group II; and six credits from Group III:

Group I: CHIN 4180, HIST 3340, JAPN 4170 or POSC 3720 

Group II: HIST 3300, HIST 3330, PHIL 3120, PHIL 3130, POSC 4770, REL 3140, or any other approved courses

Group III: EAS 1230, JAPN 4010, JAPN 4990, any Chinese or Japanese language course, or any other approved courses

Courses in Groups II and III must represent a combination of Chinese and Japanese courses.
