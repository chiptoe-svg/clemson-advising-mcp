Geography Minor

(18 credits minimum)

The Geography minor consists of three credits of geography at the 1000 level and 15 credits of geography at the 3000 or 4000 level. At least one 4000-level geography course must be taken. One of the following courses may be taken as part of the 15-credit, upper-level requirements, but may not be substituted for the required 4000-level geography course: BIOL 4420, SOC 4710.
