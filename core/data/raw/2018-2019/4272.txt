Italian Studies Minor

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Italian Studies requires 15 credits of 3000- and 4000-level ITAL courses, including at least one 4000-level literature course.

 

NOTE: Language and International Trade majors are not eligible to receive a minor in the same modern language as their concentration area language.
