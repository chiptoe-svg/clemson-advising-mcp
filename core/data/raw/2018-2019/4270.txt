Human Resource Management Minor

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Human Resource Management requires 15 credits, including MGT 3070, MGT 4000, MGT 4310 and MGT 4350, and three additional credits selected from MGT 4160 or MGT 4250.

 

NOTE: This minor is not open to Management majors.
