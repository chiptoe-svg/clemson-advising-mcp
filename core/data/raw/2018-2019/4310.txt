Youth Development Studies Minor

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Youth Development Studies requires YDP 3000 or YDP 3050; YDP 3100, YDP 3150 or YDP 3200; and YDP 3300, YDP 3350 or YDP 3400; and six additional credits selected from YDP courses. YDP courses are offered online in an accelerated seven-week format. Students meet weekly through a synchronous Web conference for discussion and interaction.
