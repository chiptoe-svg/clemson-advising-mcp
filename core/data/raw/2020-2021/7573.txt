Criminal Justice Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Criminal Justice requires JUST 2880 and 12 additional credits of JUST courses, including at least nine credits at the 3000 level or higher. No more than three credits of JUST 4860 may be applied toward the minor.
