Economics Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Economics requires ECON 3060 or ECON 3140; and ECON 3020 or ECON 3150; and nine additional credits from economics courses numbered 3000 or higher. Note that ECON 2110 and ECON 2120 are prerequisites to completion of the minor. Some 3000- and 4000-level elective classes may have additional prerequisites. See course descriptions for detailed prerequisite information.
