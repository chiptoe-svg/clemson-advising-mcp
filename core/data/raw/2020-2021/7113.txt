Nuclear Engineering and Radiological Sciences Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Nuclear Engineering and Radiological Sciences (NERS) requires EES 4090, and four additional courses selected from EES 4100, EES 4110, EES 4120, EES 4140, EES 4800, ME 4260, ME 4280, or PHYS 4520. A minimum of 15 credits is required.
