English Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in English requires 15 credits in English above the sophomore level, arranged as follows:

Critical Writing About Literature: ENGL 3100 
British: Three credits selected from ENGL 3960, ENGL 3970, ENGL 4070, ENGL 4080, ENGL 4100, ENGL 4140, ENGL 4150, ENGL 4160, ENGL 4170, ENGL 4180, ENGL 4330 or ENGL 4440 
American: Three credits selected from ENGL 3980, ENGL 3990, ENGL 4200, ENGL 4210, ENGL 4250, ENGL 4260, ENGL 4550, or ENGL 4820.
Electives: Six additional credits above the sophomore level, including at least three from the 4000 level

NOTE: This minor is not open to Secondary Education - English Teaching Area majors.
