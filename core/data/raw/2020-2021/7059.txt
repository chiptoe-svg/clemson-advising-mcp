Agricultural Business Management Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Agricultural Business Management requires AGRB 3020, AGRB 3080 and AGRB 3190, and at least two courses selected from AGRB 3090, AGRB 3510, AGRB 4020, AGRB 4080, AGRB 4090, AGRB 4520, AGRB 4560 and AGRB 4600.
