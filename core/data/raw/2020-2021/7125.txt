Religious Studies Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Religious Studies requires 15 credits as follows: REL 3000 and 12 additional credits, nine of which must be at the 3000 level or higher. PHIL 3030, POSC 4070 and SOC 4320 may be included.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Religious Studies majors.
