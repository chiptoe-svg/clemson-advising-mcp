Natural Resource Economics Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Natural Resources Economics requires AGRB 3570 and AGRB 4570 and three additional courses selected from AGRB 3520, AGRB 4090, AGRB 4120, AGRB 4210, AGRB 4520, AGRB 4750 and ECON 3190.
