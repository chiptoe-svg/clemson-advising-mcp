Communication Studies Minor

 

Minor Description

(16 credits minimum)

A minor in Communication Studies requires completion of one of the following options:

General: COMM 2010 (with a grade of C or better), COMM 2020, COMM 2090, COMM 3310, and COMM 3550.
Sports Communication: COMM 2010 (with a grade of C or better), COMM 2020, COMM 2090, COMM 3240, and COMM 3280.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Communication majors or Sports Communication majors.
