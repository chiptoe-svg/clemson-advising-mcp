Materials Science and Engineering Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Materials Science and Engineering requires MSE 2100, and 12 additional credits selected from BE 4840, CE 2060, CE 3510, CE 4520, CE 4530, CH 4010, CH 4020, CH 4540, CH 4650, CHE 3190, EES 4090, EES 4840, ENGR 14101, ME 2040, ME 4400, ME 4410, MSE 3100, MSE 3190, MSE 3260, MSE 3270, MSE 3450, MSE 4050, MSE 4070, MSE 4120, MSE 4130, MSE 4150, MSE 4160, MSE 4170, MSE 4220, MSE 4240, MSE 4280, MSE 4320, MSE 4340, MSE 4520, MSE 4530, MSE 4540, MSE 4570, MSE 4580, MSE 4610, MSE 4650, MSE 4900, PHYS 4450, PHYS 4460 and PKSC 4160. At least nine of the 12 additional credits must be at the 3000 or 4000 level and at least six of the credits must be MSE courses.

1 One of the following combinations may be substituted for ENGR 1410: (1) ENGR 1070 and ENGR 1080 and ENGR 1090; or (2) CHE 1300 and ENGR 1070; or (3) ENGR 1640.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Materials Science and Engineering majors.
