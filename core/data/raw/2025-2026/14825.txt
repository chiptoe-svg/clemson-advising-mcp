Digital Production Arts Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Digital Production Arts requires DPA 3070 and completion of one of the following options:

Group I (for Architecture, Art, or Visual Arts majors): DPA 4000* and DPA 4010* and six credits selected from CPSC 4040, CPSC 4050, CPSC 4160, DPA 4440, DPA 4810, DPA 4820, DPA 4830, ENGL 3570, PKSC 2200, PKSC 3200, THEA 2880, THEA 4870, THEA 4970.
Group II (for Computer Science, Computer Engineering, and Computer Information Systems majors): DPA 4020* and DPA 4030* and six credits selected from AAH 1010, ART 2100, ART 2130, ART 3060, ART 4210, DPA 4440, DPA 4810, DPA 4820, DPA 4830, ENGL 3570, GC 3400, PKSC 2200, PKSC 3200, THEA 2880, THEA 4870, THEA 4970.
Group III (for all other majors): DPA 4000*, DPA 4010*, DPA 4020*, and three credits selected from AAH 1010, ART 2100, CPSC 4040, CPSC 4050, CPSC 4160, DPA 4440, DPA 4810, DPA 4820, DPA 4830, ENGL 3570, PKSC 2200.

*Note that DPA 4000/DPA 4010 and DPA 4020/DPA 4030 are two-semester course sequences. Students are recommended to allow at least three semesters for completion.
