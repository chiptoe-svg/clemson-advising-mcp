Plant Pathology Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Plant Pathology requires PLPA 3100 and 12 credits selected from the following: BIOL 4250, BIOL 4260, PLPA 4020 or MICR 3050, or any 3000- or 4000-level PLPA courses.
