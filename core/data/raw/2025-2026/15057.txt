Landscape Architecture Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Landscape Architecture requires LARC 1150, LARC 1160, and LARC 4230, and six additional credits selected from LARC 2140, LARC 2150, LARC 3190, LARC 3210, LARC 3810, LARC 3990, LARC 4430, LARC 4900, and LARC 4970. Courses may be offered in Clemson, Charleston or another off-campus location. Some courses are offered during summer sessions.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Landscape Architecture majors.
