Chemistry Minor

Minor Description

(23 credits minimum)

A minor in Chemistry requires CH 1010, CH 1020 and 15 additional credits selected from the following: BCHM 3010, BCHM 3050, BCHM 4060, BCHM 4230, BIOE 3020, any CH course (except CH 1040, CH 1050, CH 1060, CH 4500, or CH 4710), CHE 3070, EES 4850, ETOX 4210, ETOX 4300, FDSC 4010, FDSC 4020, FDSC 4030, GEOL 3180, MSE 4050, MSE 4150, PHYS 4520, PHYS 4810, or PKSC 4160. At least nine of the 15 additional credits must be at the 3000 or 4000 level. Only three credits of CH 3990, CH 4430, CH 4440, or CH 4990 may be counted toward the minor in Chemistry. Note: Credit will only be given for one of each of the following course sets:  BCHM 3010 or BCHM 3050 or CH 3600; CH 3300 or CH 3310; CH 3150 or CH 3170; CH 2010 or CH 2230; CH 2020 or CH 2270 or CH 2290.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Chemistry majors or Science Teaching - Chemistry Teaching Area majors.
