Biochemistry Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Biochemistry requires three credits of GEN 3000 or GEN 3020; three credits of BCHM 3010 or BCHM 3050; and nine credits of 4000-level BCHM courses.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Biochemistry majors.
