Artificial Intelligence Minor

Minor Description

(20 credits minimum)

A minor in Artificial Intelligence requires a minimum of 20 credits as follows:

Programming Fundamentals-eight credits selected from one of the following combinations:
CPSC 1010 and CPSC 1020 
CPSC 1050 and CPSC 1070 
CPSC 1060 and CPSC 1070 
Statistical Foundations-three credits selected from IE 3610, MATH 3020, STAT 2300, or STAT 3090.
Supporting Requirements-nine credits selected from CPSC 4300, CPSC 4420, CPSC 4430, or HCC 4600. Note that CPSC 4300 has a prerequisite of CPSC 2120 (4 credits).

NOTE: This minor is not open to Computer Science BA majors.
