Physics Minor

Minor Description

(18 credits minimum)

A minor in Physics requires PHYS 1220, PHYS 2210 and PHYS 2220 and nine additional credits selected from ASTR 3020, ASTR 3030, PHYS 3110, PHYS 3120, PHYS 3150, PHYS 3210, PHYS 3220, PHYS 3250, PHYS 3260, PHYS 3550, PHYS 4170, PHYS 4200, PHYS 4320, PHYS 4410, PHYS 4420, PHYS 4450, PHYS 4460, PHYS 4520, PHYS 4550, PHYS 4560, PHYS 4650 and PHYS 4750.

NOTE: This minor is not open to the following majors: Physics; Science Teaching - Physical Sciences Teaching Area; or Science Teaching - Physics Teaching Area.
