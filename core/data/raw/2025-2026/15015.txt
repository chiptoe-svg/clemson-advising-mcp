Brand Communications Minor

 

 

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Brand Communications requires GC 3700 and GC 3760; and nine additional credits selected from COMM 3230, COMM 3500, COMM 3570, GC 3450, GC 3600, GC 3610, GC 3620, GC 3710, GC 3720, GC 3730, GC 3740, GC 3750, GC 3780, or GC 3790.
