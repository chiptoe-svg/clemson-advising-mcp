Theatre Minor

 

Minor Description

(20 credits minimum)

A minor in Theatre requires 20 credits arranged as follows:

Three credits of dramatic literature selected from ENGL 4100, ENGL 4110, THEA 3470, THEA 4290 or THEA 4300 
Three credits of theatre history selected from THEA 3150, THEA 3160, THEA 3170 or THEA 3180 
Six credits in a sequence selected from THEA 2780/THEA 4790; or THEA 3470/THEA 4470; or THEA 3670/THEA 4670; or THEA 3720/THEA 4720; or THEA 3760/THEA 4760; or one of THEA 2880 or THEA 3770 plus one of the following: THEA 4770, THEA 4870 or THEA 4970 
Six additional credits of THEA courses at the 3000 or 4000 level
Two credits of THEA 2790 

NOTE: This minor is not open to Performing Arts majors whose concentration area is Theatre.
