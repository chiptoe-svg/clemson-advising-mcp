Women’s Leadership Minor

Minor Description

(18 credits minimum)

A minor in Women’s Leadership requires either WS 1030 or WS 3010; both WS 2300 and WS 2400; either WS 3900 or WS 4010; and six additional credits selected from AAH 3050, ANTH 4230, COMM 3080, COMM 3150, COMM 3830, COMM 3800, ECON 4350, ENGL 3530, ENGL 3800, ENGL 3860, ENGL 4360, ENGL 4420, ENGL 4530, ENGL 4820, ENGL 4830, FR 4100, HIST 3160, HIST 3180, HIST 3530, HLTH 3100, PHIL 3280, PHIL 3490, POSC 4800, PRTM 3250, PSYC 3060, PSYC 4080, SOC 3100, SOC 3110, SOC 4600, SOC 4610, SOC 4840, SPAN 4030, THEA 3170, THEA 3180, WS 3490, WS 4230, WS 4360, WS 4590, WS 4900, and WS 4950. Special topics courses in various departments may also meet this requirement. Students should consult the program’s website for a list of other qualifying courses in a specific term, and should be aware that a course substitution form must be submitted in order for such a course to count towards the completion of minor and graduation requirements.   

NOTE: This minor is not open to Women’s Leadership majors.
