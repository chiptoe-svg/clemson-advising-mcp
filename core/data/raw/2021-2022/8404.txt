Public Policy Minor

Minor Description

(18 credits minimum)

A minor in Public Policy requires POSC 1010, POSC 3210, POSC 3410 and POSC 4210, plus six credits in courses dealing with specific policy domains selected from ACCT 4040, AGRB 3520, AGRB 4110, AGRB 4120, AGRB 4520, AGRB 4570, AGRB 3610, AGRB 4750, AS 4090, AS 4100, CRP 4010, CRP 4030, ECON 3010, ECON 3020, ECON 3030, ECON 3090, ECON 3150, ECON 3190, ECON 3400, ECON 3600, ECON 4020, ECON 4100, ECON 4110, ECON 4190, ECON 4200, ECON 4220, ECON 4230, ECON 4240, ECON 4250, ECON 4260, ECON 4270, ECON 4280, ECON 4290, ECON 4350, ECON 4400, EDF 4060, ENR 3120, ENR 4160, ENR 4290, ENR 4500, ENSP 4000, FDSC 3010, FOR 3040, FOR 4150, FOR 4250, FOR 4260, FOR 4310, HIST 3270, HIST 3920, HIST 3950, HLTH 3610, HLTH 4310, HLTH 4780, MGT 3050, NURS 3510, PES 4450, PHIL 3450, PHIL 3460, POSC 3020, POSC 3630, POSC 4030, POSC 4050, POSC 4160, POSC 4230, POSC 4240, POSC 4300, POSC 4360, PRTM 3010, PRTM 3200, RS 4010, SOC 3600, SOC 4010, SOC 4030, SOC 4140, SOC 4710 and WFB 4300.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Political Science majors.
