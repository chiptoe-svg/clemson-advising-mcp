Adult/Extension Education Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Adult/Extension Education requires AGED 4030 and AGED 4400, and nine additional credits selected from AGED 4070, AGED 4280, AGED 4820 and PRTM 3080.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Agricultural Education majors.
