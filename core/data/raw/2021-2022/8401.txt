Political Science Minor

Minor Description

(18 credits minimum)

A minor in Political Science requires POSC 1010 or POSC 1020 or POSC 1030 or POSC 1040 and 15 additional credits at the 3000 or 4000 level, nine of which must be selected from three different fields of political science as follows:

American Politics: POSC 4030, POSC 4050, POSC 4160, POSC 4360, POSC 4420 
Comparative Politics: POSC 3710, POSC 3720, POSC 4660, POSC 4710, POSC 4760, POSC 4770, POSC 4780 
International Relations: POSC 3610, POSC 3620, POSC 3630, POSC 3750, POSC 4470, POSC 4480 
Political Theory: POSC 4490, POSC 4500, POSC 4530, POSC 4550 
Public Policy and Public Administration: POSC 3020, POSC 3210, POSC 4210, POSC 4230, POSC 4240, POSC 4300 

No more than a total of three credits from POSC 3050, POSC 3100, POSC 3110, POSC 3120, POSC 3130, POSC 4090 and POSC 4100 may be applied to the requirements for a Political Science minor.
