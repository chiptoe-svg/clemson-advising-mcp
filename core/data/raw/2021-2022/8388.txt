Microbiology Minor

 

Minor Description

(16 credits minimum)

A minor in Microbiology requires at least 16 credits as follows:

Required courses: MICR 3050; and either BCHM 3010 or BCHM 3050 

At least three credits from each of the following three sections:

Section I: MICR 4000, MICR 4120, MICR 4160 
Section II: BIOL 4250, MICR 4020, MICR 4030, MICR 4050, MICR 4560 
Section III: MICR 4070, MICR 4130, MICR 4910, MICR 4930, MICR 4940, MICR 4950  

NOTE: This minor is not open to Microbiology majors.
