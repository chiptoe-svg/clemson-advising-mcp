Equine Industry Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Equine Industry requires AVS 1500 and AVS 1510; three credits selected from any 3000- or 4000-level AVS courses; and eight additional credits selected from AVS 2040, AVS 2080, AVS 3090, AVS 3850, AVS 4120, AVS 4160, AVS 4170, AVS 4560, or AVS 4570.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Animal and Veterinary Sciences majors in any concentration.
