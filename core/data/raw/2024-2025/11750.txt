Business Administration Minor

Minor Description

(21 credits minimum)

A minor in Business Administration requires ACCT 2010, ECON 2110, ECON 2120, FIN 3060, LAW 3220, MGT 2010 and MKT 3010. Please note that ECON 2120 requires ECON 2110 as a prerequisite. Also, FIN 3060 has prerequisites of ACCT 2010 and a statistics course. See FIN 3060 course description for courses that currently fulfill the statistics requirement. Additionally, these statistics courses may have prerequisites of their own. MKT 3010 requires one of ECON 2000, ECON 2110, ECON 2120 or any 2000-level AGRB class as a prerequisite.

NOTE: This minor is not open to students in the following majors: Accounting, BS Economics, Financial Management, Management, and Marketing.
