Wilbur O. and Ann Powers College of Business Leadership Certificate

Program Description

As one of eight Signature Programs in the Wilbur O. and Ann Powers College of Business, the objective of the Leadership Signature Program, and the associated 11-credit Leadership Certificate, is to cultivate mission-driven leaders charged with making a positive, global impact. 

The Leadership Certificate is open to all business students and consists of eight credits of experiential learning gained in a nominal, five-semester Leadership Practicum sequence, and three credits in leadership-focused elective courses.   

Completion of the certificate requires the following:

Eight credits of Leadership Practicum courses: BUS 2700 (2 credits), BUS 3700 (2 or 4 credits), and BUS 4700 (2 or 4 credits).
Three additional credits selected from ACCT 4100, the AS 2090/AS 2100 sequence (4 credits), AS 3090, AS 3100, ECON 2050, ECON 3500, ELE 4020, LAW 3400, MGT 3070, MGT 3080, MGT 3500, MGT 3810, MGT 3900, MGT 4000, MGT 4050, MGT 4080, MGT 4110, MGT 4270, the ML 1010/ML 1020 sequence (4 credits), PSYC 3680, PSYC 3690, PSYC 3770, SOC 4500, NPL 3000, POSC 4580, WS 2300, and WS 2400.
