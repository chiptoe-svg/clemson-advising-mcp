Forest Products Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Forest Products requires 15 credits selected from ACCT 2010, FNR 4900 (if the course is in a forest products field), FOR 3410, FOR 4080, FOR 4410, FOR 4420, FOR 4440, FOR 4470 or MKT 3010.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Forest Resource Management majors or Forest Resource Management majors with an Emphasis Area in Land Surveying.
