Pathways in the Humanities and Social Sciences Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

The Pathways in the Humanities and Social Sciences Minor requires 15 credits selected from the categories below.

Living Together (three credits) - HUM 1010 or three credits of alternate coursework approved by the Pathways director.
Learning in Common (nine credits) - HUM 3010, HUM 3020, HUM 3060, or alternate coursework approved by the Pathways director totaling nine credits. Alternate courses must be selected from courses with different subjects and at least two of the courses must be at or above the 3000 level.
Transforming Our World (three credits) - HUM 3090 or three credits of alternate coursework approved by the Pathways director at or above the 3000 level.

NOTE: Alternate coursework requires a course substitution.
