Artificial Intelligence Minor

Minor Description

(24 credits minimum)

A minor in Artificial Intelligence requires a minimum of 24 credits as follows:

Programming Fundamentals-eight credits selected from one of the following combinations:
CPSC 1010 and CPSC 1020 
CPSC 1050 and CPSC 1070 
CPSC 1060 and CPSC 1070 
Statistical Foundations-three credits selected from MATH 3020, STAT 2300, or STAT 3090 
Supporting Requirements (13 Credits)-CPSC 2120, CPSC 4300, CPSC 4420, and CPSC 4430 

NOTE: This minor is not open to Computer Science BA majors.
