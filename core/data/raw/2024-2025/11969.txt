Electrification of Transportation Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Electrification of Transportation requires ECE 4710 and an additional 12 credits selected from AUE 4600, CE 3110, CE 4100, CE 4120, CHE 4160, ECE 4160, ECE 4190, ECE 4310, ECE 4320, ECE 4420, ECE 4490, ECE 4700, ECE 4730, ECE 4980, ME 4170, ME 4250, ME 4510, ME 4520, and ME 4600. A maximum of three credits of ECE 4980 may be counted towards completion of the minor.
