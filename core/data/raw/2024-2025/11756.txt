Creative Writing Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Creative Writing requires 15 credits, completed according to one of the following concentration options:

Fiction: ENGL 3450, ENGL 4230, and ENGL 4450; and six additional credits selected from ENGL 3560, ENGL 4190, ENGL 4260, ENGL 4280, ENGL 4650, and ENGL 4830.

Poetry: ENGL 3460, ENGL 4220, and ENGL 4460; and six additional credits selected from ENGL 4190, ENGL 4260, ENGL 4280, ENGL 4310, ENGL 4650, and ENGL 4830.

Playwriting: ENGL 3470/THEA 3470 and ENGL 4470/THEA 4470; and nine additional credits selected from ENGL 4190, ENGL 4260, ENGL 4280, ENGL 4300, ENGL 4650, ENGL 4830, and THEA 3470.

Screenwriting: ENGL 3480, ENGL 3570 and ENGL 4480; and six additional credits selected from ENGL 4500, ENGL 4510, ENGL 4520, ENGL 4530, and THEA 3470.
