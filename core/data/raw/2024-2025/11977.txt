Orthopaedic Medical Device Product Specialist Certificate

Program Description

The Orthopaedic Medical Device Product Specialist certificate provides students with core competencies within various aspects of the orthopaedic medical device industry. Focus is on managing a product throughout its life cycle, including product development and performance relevant to clinical use, and communication of its commercial value. The certificate is comprised of four courses (12 credits) as follows:

MKT 3250 Professional Selling 
MKT 4360 - Selling in Healthcare 
One Orthopaedics course selected from the following:
BIOE 3120 Fundamentals of Orthopaedic Device Science and Engineering 
BIOE 4120 Orthopaedic Engineering and Pathology 
One course selected from the following:
BCHM 3050 Essential Elements of Biochemistry 
BIOE 3100 Engineering Analysis of Physiological Processes 
BIOL 2220 Human Anatomy and Physiology I 
BIOL 3150 Functional Human Anatomy 
HLTH 2030 Overview of Health Care Systems 
HLTH 2600 Medical Terminology and Communication 
HLTH 3800 Epidemiology 
IE 4300 Human Factors Engineering in Healthcare 
MKT 3010 Principles of Marketing
