Precision Agriculture Minor

Minor Description

(18 credits minimum)

A minor in Precision Agriculture requires AGM 2060 and AGM 4100; and either FOR 4340 or GEOL 4210; and at least nine credits selected from AGRB 3020 or AGRB 4020; ENT 4070, FOR 4330, PLPA 4020, PES 4210, PES 4220, PES 4230, PES 4260, PES 4330, PES 4460 or PES 4520.
