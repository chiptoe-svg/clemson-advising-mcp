Biological Sciences Minor

Minor Description

(20 credits minimum)

A minor in Biological Sciences requires either BIOL 1030/BIOL 1050 or BIOL 1100; and either BIOL 1040/BIOL 1060 or BIOL 1110; plus 12 additional credits selected from BIOL courses at the 3000 level or above, BCHM 3010, BCHM 3050, GEN 3000 or GEN 3020, or MICR 3050/MICR 3060. Only four credits of BCHM 3010, BCHM 3050, BIOL 3940, BIOL 4910, BIOL 4920, BIOL 4930, BIOL 4940, BIOL 4950, BIOL 4980, GEN 3000, GEN 3020, and/or MICR 3050/MICR 3060 may count toward the minor.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Biological Sciences majors or to Science Teaching - Biological Sciences Teaching Area majors.
