Engineering Leadership Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum),

A minor in Engineering Leadership requires ECAS 2910, ECAS 3910, and nine additional credits selected from ACCT 2010, ECON 2000, ECON 2050, ECON 2110, ELE 3010, ELE 4020, ESED 3100, ESED 3200, ESED 3300, HIST 3210, HIST 4910, LAW 3220, LAW 3400, MGT 2010, MGT 3030, MGT 3070, MGT 3080, MGT 3810, MGT 3900, MGT 4000, MGT 4050, MGT 4110, MKT 3010, MKT 3240, MKT 4240, NPL 3000, POSC 4580, PSYC 2010, PSYC 3680, PSYC 3690, PSYC 3770, SOC 4500, WS 2300, and WS 2400. At least six of the additional credits must be at the 3000 level or higher.
