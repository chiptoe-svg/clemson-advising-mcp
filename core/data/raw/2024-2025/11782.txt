Italian Studies Minor

Minor Description

(18 credits minimum)

A minor in Italian Studies requires ITAL 2010 and ITAL 2020 and 12 credits of 3000- and 4000-level ITAL courses.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Language and International Business majors whose target language is Italian or to Modern Languages majors whose emphasis area is Italian.
