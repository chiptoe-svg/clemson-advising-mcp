Construction Science and Management Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Construction Science and Management requires either CSM 2030 or CSM 2050 and 12 additional credits selected from CSM 2040, CSM 3510, CSM 3520, and CSM 4530.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Construction Science and Management majors.
