Materials Science and Engineering Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Materials Science and Engineering requires MSE 2100, and 12 additional credits selected from BIOE 3020, CH 4010, CH 4020, CH 4650, CHE 3190, ENGR 1410, ME 2040, MSE 3100, MSE 3190, MSE 3260, MSE 3270, MSE 3450, MSE 4020, MSE 4070, MSE 4120, MSE 4130, MSE 4150, MSE 4220, MSE 4240, MSE 4280, MSE 4320, MSE 4330, MSE 4530, MSE 4570, MSE 4580, MSE 4610, MSE 4900, PHYS 4450, and PHYS 4460. At least nine of the 12 additional credits must be at the 3000 or 4000 level and at least six of the credits must be MSE courses.
