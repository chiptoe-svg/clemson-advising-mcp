Political and Legal Theory Minor

Minor Description

(18 credits minimum)

A minor in Political and Legal Theory requires POSC 1030; at least three credits selected from Group I; at least three credits selected from Group II; and nine additional credits selected from either group.

Group I: POSC 4370, POSC 4380 

Group II: POSC 4490, POSC 4500 (may be repeated if different topics are covered), POSC 4530, POSC 4550 

NOTE: This minor is not open to Political Science majors.
