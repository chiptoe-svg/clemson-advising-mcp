Computer Science Minor

Minor Description

(16 credits minimum)

A minor in Computer Science requires CPSC 2120* and 12 additional credits in computer science, at least nine of which must be at the 3000 level or higher.

*Note that before enrolling in CPSC 2120, students must have completed a two-semester introductory computer science course sequence (CPSC 1010/CPSC 1020 or CPSC 1110/CPSC 1020 or CPSC 1060/CPSC 1070).

NOTE: This minor is not open to Computer Information Systems majors.
