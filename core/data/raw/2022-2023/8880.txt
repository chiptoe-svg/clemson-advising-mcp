Athletic Leadership Minor

Minor Description

(17 credits minimum)

A minor in Athletic Leadership requires 17 credits arranged as follows: AL 3490, AL 3500, AL 3530, AL 3610, AL 3620, AL 3760 and one of the following: AL 3710, AL 3720, AL 3730, AL 3740, AL 3750 or AL 3770. Students must complete a coaching internship or athletic administrative internship (AL 4000) with the approval of the Athletic Leadership Coordinator.
