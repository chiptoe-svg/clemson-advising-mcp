Wilbur O. and Ann Powers College of Business Leadership Certificate

Program Description

As one of eight Signature Programs in the Wilbur O. and Ann Powers College of Business, the objective of the Leadership Signature Program, and the associated 12-credit Leadership Certificate, is to cultivate mission-driven leaders charged with making a positive, global impact. 

The Leadership Certificate is open to all business students and consists of six credits of experiential learning gained in a nominal, three-year Leadership Practicum, and six credits in leadership-focused elective courses.   

Completion of the certificate requires the following:

Six credits of Leadership Practicum courses, one credit each semester, two per year, of BUS 2700, BUS 3700, and BUS 4700 in the sophomore, junior, and senior years, respectively.
Six additional credits selected from the AS 2090/AS 2100 sequence (4 credits), AS 3090, AS 3100, ECON 2050, ECON 3500, ELE 4020, MGT 3070, MGT 3500, MGT 3810, MGT 3900, MGT 4000, MGT 4050, MGT 4080, MGT 4150, MGT 4270, the ML 1010/ML 1020 sequence (4 credits), PSYC 3680, PSYC 3690, PSYC 3770, SOC 4500, NPL 3000, POSC 4580, WS 2300, and WS 2400.
