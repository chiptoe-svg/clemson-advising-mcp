Communication Studies Minor

 

Minor Description

(16 credits minimum)

A minor in Communication Studies requires completion of one of the following options:

General: COMM 2010 (with a C or better) and 12 additional credits in communication studies at the 3000 level or higher
Sports Communication: COMM 2010 (with a C or better) and 12 additional credits in sports communication selected from COMM 3240, COMM 3270, COMM 3280, COMM 4250, and COMM 4270.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Communication majors or Sports Communication majors.
