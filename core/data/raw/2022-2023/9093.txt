Renewable Energy Certificate

Program Description

The Renewable Energy Certificate consists of nine credits. Students must earn a grade of C or higher in all courses in order to be awarded the certificate. The following courses are required:

ECE 4200 - Renewable Energy Penetration on the Power Grid 3 Credits
ECE (ME) 4570 - Fundamentals of Wind Power 3 Credits
ECE 4610 - Fundamentals of Solar Energy 3 Credits
