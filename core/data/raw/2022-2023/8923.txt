Military Leadership Minor

Minor Description

(19 credits minimum)

A minor in Military Leadership requires at least 19 credits, including ML 3010, ML 3020, ML 4010, ML 4020 and one of the following: HIST 3900, HIST 3980, ML 3900 or POSC 4580. Completion of Leadership Laboratory and participation in cadet activities are mandatory. (ML 1000 and 2000 levels may be taken concurrently in the sophomore year.)
