Camp Leadership Certificate

Program Description

The Camp Leadership Certificate is a 12-credit online certificate offered through the Department of Parks, Recreation and Tourism Management. It focuses on leadership and management issues relevant to those working in a variety of camp settings, both day and residential. The following courses are required:

PRTM 3520 - Camp Organization and Administration 3 Credits
PRTM 3530 - Foundations of Camp Counseling 3 Credits
PRTM 3540 - Youth Development in Camp 3 Credits
PRTM 3550 - Trends and Issues in Camp Management 3 Credits
