Agricultural Education Teacher Certificate

Program Description

The Agricultural Education Teacher Certificate provides the opportunity for students or working professionals to complete the requirements to be recommended for a teacher certificate to teach secondary agricultural education, while enhancing their technical skills and content and pedagogical knowledge through the process of teaching and learning.

Candidates seeking teacher certification in the area of Agricultural Education must be pursuing or hold an earned Bachelor of Science Degree in a specialized field of agriculture.

Program Requirements
First Year
Fall Semester
AGED 3030 - Mechanical Technology for Agriculture Education 3 Credits
AGED 4030 - Principles of Adult/Extension Education 3 Credits
AGED 2010 - Introduction to Agricultural Education 3 Credits
EDSP 3700 - Introduction to Special Education 3 Credits
Credit Hours: 12
Spring Semester
AGED 1000 - Orientation and Field Experience 1 Credits
AGED 2030 - Teaching Agriscience 3 Credits
EDF 3020 - Educational Psychology 3 Credits
Optional Agriculture (AFNR) Content Elective 3 Credits 1
Credits Hours: 9-12
Second Year
Fall Semester
AGED 4230 - Curriculum 3 Credits
AGED 4000 - Supervised Field Experience II 1 Credits
AGED 4010 - Instructional Methods in Agricultural Education 3 Credits
EDLT 4980 - Content Area Reading and Writing for Middle and Secondary Teachers 3 Credits
Credit Hours: 12
Spring Semester
AGED 4060 - Directed Teaching 12 Credits
AGED 4250 - Teaching Agricultural Mechanics 2 Credits
Credit Hours: 15
Total Credits: 48 (or 51 with optional elective course)
Footnotes

Other appropriate AFNR/Agriculture Sciences content courses may be approved.
