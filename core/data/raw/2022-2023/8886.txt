Chinese Studies Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Chinese Studies requires 15 credits selected from 3000- and 4000-level CHIN courses, including CHIN 3050 and CHIN 3060, and least one 4000-level literature course.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Language and International Business majors or Language and International Health majors whose target language is Chinese, or to Modern Languages majors whose emphasis area is Chinese.
