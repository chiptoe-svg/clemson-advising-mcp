Power Systems Engineering Certificate

Program Description

The Power Systems Engineering Certificate consists of nine credits. Students must earn a grade of C or higher in all courses in order to be awarded the certificate. The following courses are required:

ECE 3600 - Electric Power Engineering 3 Credits
ECE 4180 - Power System Analysis 3 Credits
ECE 4190 - Electric Machines and Drives 3 Credits
