Aerospace Studies Minor

Minor Description

(24 credits minimum)

A minor in Aerospace Studies requires AS 1090, AS 1100, AS 2090, AS 2100, AS 3090, AS 3100, AS 4090 and AS 4100. Completion of AS Leadership Laboratory and participation in cadet activities are mandatory. Students must compete for an allocation and be accepted into the Professional Officer Course before enrolling in AS 3090.
