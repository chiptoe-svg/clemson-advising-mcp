Pan African Studies Minor

 

Minor Description

(18 credits minimum)

A minor in Pan African Studies requires 18 credits, including HIST 3110 or HIST 3120; and PAS 3010; and 12 additional credits arranged as follows:

Group I: Three credits selected from GEOG 3300, HIST 3370, HIST 3380, HIST 3390, HIST 4380, PAS 1010 or PAS 4980 
Group II: Three credits selected from ENGL 4820, ENGL 4830, POSC 3810, SOC 4600 or THEA 3170 
Group III: Three credits in the social sciences selected from HIST 3140, HIST 4930, POSC 4230, POSC 4540, PSYC 3520, SOC 3110, SOC 3310 or SOC 4620 
Group IV: Three credits in the humanities selected from COMM 3690, ENGL 3530, ENGL 4590, MUSC 3120, PAS 3010, REL 3100, THEA 3180 or WS 3010
