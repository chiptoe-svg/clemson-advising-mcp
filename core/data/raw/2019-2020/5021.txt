Management Information Systems Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Management Information Systems requires 15 credits as follows:

ACCT 3220 or MGT 3180 
MGT 4110 
MGT 4520 
Two of the following: MGT 3120, MGT 4540, MGT 4550, MGT 4560 

NOTE: This minor is not open to Management majors.
