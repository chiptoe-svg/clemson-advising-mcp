Agricultural Mechanization and Business Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Agricultural Mechanization and Business requires six credit hours selected from AGM 2050, AGM 2060, AGM 2200, AGM 2210, AGM 3010 and AGED 3030; and nine credits selected from AGM 4020, AGM 4050, AGM 4060, AGM 4100, AGM 4520, AGM 4600 and AGM 4720.
