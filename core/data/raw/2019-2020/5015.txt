Horticulture Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Horticulture requires HORT 1010 and 12 additional credits of horticulture courses at the 2000 level or higher, nine credits of which must be at the 3000 level or higher. HORT 2710 is highly recommended. Students may count only three credits of HORT 4080 and HORT 2020 may not be used to fulfill the minor requirements.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Turfgrass majors.
