Art Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Art requires ART 1510; one of AAH 1010, AAH 1020 or ART 2100; at least six credits of ART or AAH courses at the 2000 level or above; and at least three credits of ART or AAH courses at the 3000 or 4000 level. Students must declare an Art minor before they complete 60 credits. Students with more than 60 credits are not permitted to declare an Art minor as the minor program requires a minimum of four semesters to complete.

NOTE: Nine credits at the 3000 or 4000 level are not required for a minor in Art, as approved by the University Undergraduate Curriculum Committee 2010.
