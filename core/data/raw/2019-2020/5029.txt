Nuclear Engineering and Radiological Sciences Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Nuclear Engineering and Radiological Sciences (NERS) requires 15 credits: EES 4090, EES 4100, EES 4120, ME 4260, and one additional course selected from EES 4110, EES 4140, EES 4800, ME 4280 or PHYS 4520.
