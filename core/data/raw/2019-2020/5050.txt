Travel and Tourism Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Travel and Tourism requires PRTM 3010, PRTM 3420, and nine additional credits selected from PRTM 3430, PRTM 3440, PRTM 3490, PRTM 3920, PRTM 3980, PRTM 4300, PRTM 4410, PRTM 4440, PRTM 4450, PRTM 4460, PRTM 4470 and PRTM 4980.
