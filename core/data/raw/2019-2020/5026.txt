Music Minor

Minor Description

(18 credits minimum)

A minor in Music requires the following:

MUSC 1420, MUSC 1430, MUSC 1510, MUSC 1520, MUSC 2510 and MUSC 2520 
MUSC 4150 or MUSC 4160 
Four semesters of ensemble, totaling four credits, selected from MUSC 3230, MUSC 3610, MUSC 3620, MUSC 3630, MUSC 3640, MUSC 3690, MUSC 3700, MUSC 3710 and MUSC 3720 
One three-hour MUSC course at the 3000 or 4000 level

NOTE: All four semesters of applied music and large ensemble must be on the student’s primary instrument.
