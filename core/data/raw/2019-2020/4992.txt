Creative Writing Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Creative Writing requires 15 credits, consisting of the following: three credits selected from ENGL 3450, ENGL 3460, ENGL 3470 or ENGL 3480; six credits selected from ENGL 4450, ENGL 4460, ENGL 4470 or ENGL 4480; three credits selected from ENGL 4220 or ENGL 4230; and three credits selected from ENGL 3560, ENGL 4190, ENGL 4260, ENGL 4280, ENGL 4310, ENGL 4320, ENGL 4650 or ENGL 4830.
