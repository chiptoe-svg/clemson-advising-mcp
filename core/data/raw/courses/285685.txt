MATH 2060 - Calculus of Several Variables
4 Credits (4 Contact Hours)
Topics include real valued functions of several variables, multiple integration, differential calculus of functions of several variables, vector field theory. Includes Honors sections. Preq: MATH 1080 with a C or better or MATH 1110 with a C or better.
