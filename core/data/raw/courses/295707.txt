SCI 1200 - Fundamentals of Science
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
This interdisciplinary course is designed to prepare students for advanced study in the sciences by developing essential study, problem-solving, analytical, soft, and research skills. Through hands-on activities, case studies, and collaborative projects, students explore complex and multifaceted problems in STEM as a mechanism to employ and understand key foundational skills necessary for success in STEM. The course emphasizes critical thinking, scientific literacy, and ethical decision-making while integrating foundational concepts from biology, chemistry, physics, and mathematics. Preq or concurrent enrollment: Any of the following courses: BIOL 1030 or BIOL 1100 or CH 1010 or CH 1020 or CH 2230 or MATH 1030 or MATH 1040 or MATH 1050 or MATH 1060 or MATH 1080 or PHYS 1220 or PHYS 2070 or PHYS 2080 or PHYS 2210. Coreq: SCI 1201.
