PSYC 3680 - Organizational Psychology
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Study of individual and organizational factors that affect job performance and well-being in modern organizations. Sample topics include leadership, employee motivation, job performance, counterproductive workplace behavior, work stress and burnout, job attitudes and perceptions, team dynamics, and organizational change management. Preq: PSYC 2010.
