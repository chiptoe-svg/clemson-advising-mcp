MUSC 3690 - Symphony Orchestra
1 Credit (3 Contact Hours)
College-community orchestra devoted to performing works from the standard repertoire. Public performances are given periodically in addition to the minimum rehearsal time. Audition required. This course may be repeated for credit with a maximum of 16 hours ensemble credit allowable toward a degree.
