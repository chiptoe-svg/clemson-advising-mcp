JAPN 4040 - Cultural Studies in Japan
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Study of Japanese cultural topics on site in Japan through lectures, field trips, small student group reconnaissance excursions, and reporting sessions. All activities are conducted in Japanese. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits. Preq: JAPN 2020.
