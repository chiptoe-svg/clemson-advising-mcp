HORT 4550 - Just Fruits
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Students explore the origins, biology, culture, and production of major temperate zone fruits: apples, berries, and cherries to pawpaws, peaches, and pomegranates, the familiar to the forbidden. They discover principles, practices, and technologies employed to grow, protect, and harvest the fruits that feed us from commercial orchards, organic farms, and backyards. Preq: HORT 1010.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
