MICR 1010 - Microbes and Human Affairs
1 Credits (1 Contact Hours)
Introduces Microbiology majors to University career and library services, evaluation of computer program proficiency, Web page development, Microbiology emphasis areas, and Microbiology faculty. Students initiate their own Web-based student portfolios, which showcase their skills and experiences (e.g., resumes, accomplishments, and work samples) during their undergraduate programs. Preq or concurrent enrollment: BIOL 1030 and BIOL 1050; or BIOL 1100.
