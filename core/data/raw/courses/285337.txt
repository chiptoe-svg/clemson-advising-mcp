HON 2090 - Border Crossings: Experiences in World Cultures
1-3 Credits (1-3 Contact Hours)
Readings and studies that heighten understanding of world cultures and societies. Taken in conjunction with international educational experiences approved by the Clemson University Honors College. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits, with a maximum of 12 credits per study abroad experience. Preq: Membership in the Clemson University Honors College
