AGRB 2020 - Agricultural Economics
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Analytical survey of the various subdivisions of agricultural economics, including farm organization, enterprise, land economics, marketing, farm prices, governmental farm policies, and the relation of agriculture to the national and international economy. Preq: MATH 1020 with a grade of C or higher or a score of 620 or higher on the SAT Math section or a score of 26 or higher on the ACT Math section or a score of 60 or higher on the Clemson Mathematics Placement Test (CMPT).
