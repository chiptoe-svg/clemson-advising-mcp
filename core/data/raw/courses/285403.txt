IE 3840 - Engineering Economic Analysis
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Basic principles and techniques of economic analysis of engineering projects. Consideration of time, value of money, short- and long-term investments, replacement analysis, depreciation methods, cost allocation, and measures of cost effectiveness. Preq: MATH 1080 with a C or higher.
