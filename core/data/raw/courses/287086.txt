ITAL 3160 - Italian for International Business I
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Spoken and written Italian common to the Italian-speaking world of business and industry, emphasizing business practices and writing and translating business letters and professional reports. Cross-cultural references provide opportunity for comparative and contrastive analyses of American and Italian cultural patterns in a business setting. Enrollment is not limited to Language and International Business students. Preq: ITAL 2020.
