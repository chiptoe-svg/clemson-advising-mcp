POSC 3050 - Creative Inquiry in Political Science
1-3 Credits (1-3 Contact Hours)
Engages students in faculty-led research projects. No more than three hours from POSC 3050, POSC 3100, POSC 3110, POSC 3120, POSC 3130, POSC 4090, POSC 4100 may be applied toward a Political Science major, minor, or a Global Politics minor. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits. Preq: Consent of instructor.
