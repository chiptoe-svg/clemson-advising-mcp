HIST 2150 - Introduction to Digital History
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduces digital methods for historical research and communication. Includes online research strategies, basic digital tools for historical research, simple ways of writing web pages, and discussion of new forms of public participation in history and how they may change the discipline.
