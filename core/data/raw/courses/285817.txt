MGT 4520 - Business Analysis
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Follows the traditional systems development life cycle (SDLC), although alternative methodologies are also discussed. Focuses on earlier phases of the SDLC, from IS planning through specification of structured requirements and on the methods, techniques, and tools used to determine information requirements and their unambiguous documentation. Preq: MGT 3500; or MGT 2010 and ACCT 3220; or MGT 2010 and CPSC 2150 and CPSC 2310.
