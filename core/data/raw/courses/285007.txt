GC 1020 - Introduction to Digital Graphics
2 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Students learn to develop and produce the basic components needed for self-promotion. Emphasis is placed on strategies relevant to students marketing themselves utilizing current business practices. Students are introduced to various Adobe Creative Cloud software applications and industry standard techniques for producing print and digital media. Includes Honors sections.
