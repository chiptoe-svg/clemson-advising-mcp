ECON 3990 - Internship in Economics
1-3 Credits (1-3 Contact Hours)
Faculty-supervised economics internship designed to give students learning opportunities that support their classroom experiences. Requires a minimum of six weeks. This course may not be used to satisfy Major Requirement I or II requirements for Economics majors. To be taken Pass/No Pass only. May be repeated for a maximum of three credits. Preq: Junior standing.
