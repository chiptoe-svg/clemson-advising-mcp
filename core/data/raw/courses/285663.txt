LS 3890 - Intermediate Tennis
1 Credit (3 Contact Hours)
Develops skills necessary to play at a competitive level of tennis. Students learn mechanically sound tennis skills, court positioning, court movement, proper shot selection, and strategic insight into the game. Preq: LS 1890.
