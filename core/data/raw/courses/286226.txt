PHYS 3260 - Experiments in Modern Physics
3 Credits (1 Contact Hour)
Experiments in fields of modern physics, including atomic and nuclei physics, solid state physics, optics/lasers, magnetism, spectroscopy, and materials characterization. Measurement of fundamental constants, repetition of crucial, Nobel prize winning experiments of modern physics. Includes Honors sections. Preq: PHYS 2220. Coreq: PHYS 3261.
