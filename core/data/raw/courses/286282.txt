PLPA 2130 - Fungi and Civilization
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Overview of how fungi affect the lives of humans, both currently and historically. Addresses the diversity of fungi and the tremendous roles fungi play on the planet with respect to the biological, social and ethical consequences. The general nature of this course makes it beneficial to all students.
