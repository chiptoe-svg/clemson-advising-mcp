LS 1350 - Women’s Riflery
1 Credit (3 Contact Hours)
Introduces students to the basics of rifle shooting and safety. Students learn basic shooting skills and are exposed to more advanced topics, such as reloading, external ballistics and long range shooting. This course is designed to give women a comfortable environment to learn the skills necessary to participate in the sport of riflery.
