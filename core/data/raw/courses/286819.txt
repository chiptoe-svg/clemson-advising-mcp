CSM 3060 - Construction Technology
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Students examine the transformative impact of emerging technologies on the construction industry, exploring advancements in digital, innovative, integrated, and emerging construction technologies. Students learn how these technologies foster collaboration, integration, and a sustainable built environment and explore various construction approaches that enhance project efficiency and productivity. Students gain hands-on experience with construction software for planning activities. Various pedagogical methods, including class discussions, case studies, and a final presentation, facilitate learning. Preq: Construction Science and Management major; and CSM 2040 and MGT 2180. Coreq: CSM 3061.
