ART 3050 - Intermediate Drawing
3 Credits (6 Contact Hours)
Presents drawing problems and processes directed toward the production of thematically driven projects. Exploration of diverse drawing media. Emphasizes further development of drawing skills, relationships of materials/techniques to subject matter, and the study of contemporary issues in drawing. Preq: ART 2060.
