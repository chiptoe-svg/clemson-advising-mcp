PSYC 3240 - Physiological Psychology
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Study of human neuroanatomy with emphasis on the function of the nervous and endocrine systems. Discusses the biological basis of behavior in its normal and abnormal dimensions. Preq: PSYC 2010.
