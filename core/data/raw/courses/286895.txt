ENTR 2010 - Selected Topics in Entrepreneurship
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Examination of selected topics in entrepreneurship based on developments in the field and faculty interests. Emphasis is placed on the strategic formulation and application of topics to support entrepreneurial thinking. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits, but only if different topics are covered.
