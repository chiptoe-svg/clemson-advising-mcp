EDML 4280 - Teaching Middle Level Social Studies
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Development of instructional practices and materials for teaching middle grades social studies and familiarization with middle grades social studies curriculum materials. Includes field experiences in local schools. Preq: EDML 3240 or EDML 3280; and admission to the professional level.  Coreq: EDML 4281.
