CSM 4550 - Building High Performance Teams
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Focuses on the study of the delivery of projects and how teams can affect the successful completion of the venture. Topics include management of human resources, understanding the needs and processes of team participants, identifying team-related problems, and methods of avoiding and settling disputes. Preq: Junior standing and Construction Science and Management or Architecture major.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
