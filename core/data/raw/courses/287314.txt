JUST 4130 - Race and Crime
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Provides a critical examination of the interplay between race and crime in the American justice system. This course examines historical and contemporary racial justice struggles, specifically as they pertain to interactions with police, courts, corrections, and the juvenile justice system. Preq: JUST 2880 or SOC 2880.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
