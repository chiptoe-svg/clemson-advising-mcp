PRTM 4100 - Methods of Recreation Research II
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Continuation of PRTM 4090; includes supervised execution and reporting of results of research proposal developed in PRTM 4090 and the application of inferential statistics to research. Students must have a 2.0 cumulative grade-point average to enroll in this course. Includes Honors sections. Preq: PRTM 4090 and consent of instructor.
