LARC 2510 - Landscape Architecture Design Fundamentals
6 Credits (1 Contact Hours)
Compositional skills introduced in LARC 1510 and LARC 1520 are applied to design in the landscape. Through research, design assignments and discussions, students derive and apply design principles to place, study the processes of design and develop an understanding of how design principles, plant materials and structures are used in the landscape. Preq: LARC 1520. Coreq: LARC 2511.
