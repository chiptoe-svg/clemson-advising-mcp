MATH 4400 - Linear Programming
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to linear programming covering the simplex algorithm, duality, sensitivity analysis, network models, formulation of models, and the use of simplex codes to solve, interpret, and analyze problems. Includes Honors sections. Preq: MATH 2060 and MATH 3110.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
