PLPA 3020 - Plant Pathology Research
1-6 Credits (3-18 Contact Hours)
Research experience in a plant pathology project for undergraduates who understand basic concepts of research. Students develop research objectives, procedures, and collect data. A written report includes interpretation of results. Includes Honors sections. To be taken Pass/ No Pass only. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits. Preq: Consent of instructor.
