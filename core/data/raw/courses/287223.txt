EDHC 3210 - Practical Research
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This research methodology courses is embedded in cross-disciplinary contexts. Students are taken through the process of identifying a problem, choosing a research design, collecting relevant data, analyzing and interpreting data, drawing conclusions, and communicating those conclusions to a wide audience.
