PRTM 3900 - Independent Study in Parks, Recreation and Tourism Management
1-6 Credits (1-6 Contact Hours)
Comprehensive studies and investigation of special topics not covered in other courses. Emphasizes field studies, community service, and independent readings. Students must have a 2.0 cumulative grade-point average to enroll in this course. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits. Preq: Junior standing and consent of instructor.
