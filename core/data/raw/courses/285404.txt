IE 3860 - Production Planning and Control
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Fundamentals of forecasting demand, scheduling production, and controlling the movement and storage of material associated with production are studied. State-of-the-art manufacturing techniques are discussed. Preq: Both IE 2140 and IE 3800; or MATH 4400.
