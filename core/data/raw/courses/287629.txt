MUSC 3830 - The Music Entrepreneur
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This survey of basic arts business concepts includes financial management, marketing, business organization, and legal considerations, and provides the foundational knowledge students need to identify, develop, and execute their entrepreneurial ideas. Preq: MUSC 3810 and MUSC 3820.
