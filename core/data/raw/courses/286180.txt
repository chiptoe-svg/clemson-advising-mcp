PHIL 3510 - Philosophy of Emotion
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Considers a range of classic and contemporary readings on the nature and function of emotion. Topics include cognitive, physiological, and constructionist approaches to understanding emotion, emotion and reason, emotion and morality, and select individual emotions.
