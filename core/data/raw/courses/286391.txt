PRTM 3040 - Challenge Course Facilitation
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Develops knowledge and skill in planning, directing, and evaluating group performance in an adventure challenge course environment; emphasis is placed on low and high ropes elements, processing, assessment, safety, and course management. Students must have a 2.0 cumulative grade-point average to enroll in this course. Coreq: PRTM 3041.
