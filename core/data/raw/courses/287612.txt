CSM 4990 - Creative Inquiry in Building Affordable Housing II
3 Credits (6 Contact Hours)
Students learn about affordable housing construction through hands-on experience and community service. They volunteer at Habitat for Humanity sites, gaining skills in permitting and construction management. The course emphasizes project management, teamwork, and problem-solving to prepare students to positively impact communities in need. Preq or concurrent enrollment: CSM 3990.
