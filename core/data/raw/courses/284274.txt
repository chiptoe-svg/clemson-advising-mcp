CSM 2050 - Materials and Methods: Concrete/Steel Construction
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Descriptive study of materials and methods of construction, focusing on nomenclature, building materials, and assembly of building systems consisting primarily of steel and concrete, in addition to roofing assemblies and interior and exterior commercial finishes. Preq: CSM 2030; and Construction Science and Management major.
