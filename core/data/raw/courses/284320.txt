DANC 1600 - Ballet Dance I
1 Credit (2 Contact Hours)
Introduces basic principles and fundamentals of classical ballet, with emphasis on good technique, center work, and across the floor work. Performing Arts fee is assessed. May be repeated for a maximum of eight credits.
