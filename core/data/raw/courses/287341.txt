BUS 3010 - Business Ethics
1 Credit (1 Contact Hour)
This course provides College of Business students with an ethical framework as they prepare to embark on their professional careers. The course examines the role of business in society and explores how business allows people to use their knowledge, skills, and capacities in the service of others. Preq: BUS 1010 and BUS 2010.
