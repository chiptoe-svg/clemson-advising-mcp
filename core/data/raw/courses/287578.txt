POSC 3140 - United Nations: Case Studies and Simulations
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course concerns the purpose and history of the United Nations, the problems it has faced, and the ways it has adapted over the course of its 80-year existence to resolve these problems. Academic coursework on the United Nations is paired with case studies that illustrate the dynamic between international players, NGOs, and nation-states. Students attend a Model UN competition. Preq: Sophomore standing.
