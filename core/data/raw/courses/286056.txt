NURS 4120 - Nursing Care of Women and Their Families
5 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Emphasizes biological, psychological, and sociocultural concepts; identification of appropriate nursing strategies to enhance individual capacity to achieve or maintain wellness in the family, home, community, and hospital environment. Preq: NURS 3030 and NURS 3050 and NURS 3110 and NURS 3230 and NURS 3300, each with a C or better. Coreq: NURS 4010 and NURS 4110 and NURS 4121.
