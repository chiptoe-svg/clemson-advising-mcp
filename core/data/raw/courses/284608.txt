EES 3040 - Wastewater Treatment Systems
2 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Study of fundamental principles, rational design considerations, and operational procedures of the unit operations and processes employed in wastewater treatment. Both physiochemical and biological treatment techniques are discussed. Introduces the integration of unit operations and processes into wastewater treatment systems. Preq: EES 2020 with a C or better. Coreq: EES 3030 and EES 3050.
