MUSC 3680 - String Orchestra
1 Credit (3 Contact Hours)
This orchestra studies and performs diverse music from the string orchestra repertoire. Public performances are given periodically in addition to the scheduled rehearsal time. No audition is required, but previous experience on a string instrument is required. May be repeated for a maximum of 10 credits.
