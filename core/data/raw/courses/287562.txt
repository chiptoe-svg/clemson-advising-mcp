FDSC 4230 - Food Safety Certifications
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Food safety training in nationally-recognized programs (ServSafe, HACCP, and FSMA). Covers food safety concepts associated with proper food handling and sanitation throughout the flow of food, hazard analysis, and risk-based preventive controls for human food regulation. Preq: Junior standing and Food Science and Human Nutrition major or Packaging Science major.
