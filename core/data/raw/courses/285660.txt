LS 3470 - Advanced Alpine Skiing
1 Credit (3 Contact Hours)
Advanced downhill snow skiing instruction in such techniques as mogul skiing, check turns, free-style, and racing. There is an additional fee for course. Taught over Christmas break. Credit is awarded for spring semester. (Contact Department of Parks, Recreation and Tourism Management in October.) Preq: LS 1470.
