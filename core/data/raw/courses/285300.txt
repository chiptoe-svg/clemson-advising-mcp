HLTH 3400 - Health Promotion Program Planning
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Students develop skills to conduct community health needs assessments and to plan and evaluate theoretically grounded health promotion intervention programs for diverse populations. Best practices for specific health behavior change interventions are identified. Preq: HLTH 2400 and HLTH 2980.
