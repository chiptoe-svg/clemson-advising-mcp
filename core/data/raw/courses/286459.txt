PRTM 4210 - Recreation Financial Resources Management
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Analysis of recreation financial resources management. Deals with revenue sources and their allocation. Students must have a 2.0 cumulative grade-point average to enroll in this course. Includes Honors sections. Preq: PRTM 2200.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
