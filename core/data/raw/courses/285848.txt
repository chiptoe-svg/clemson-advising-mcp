MICR 4100 - Soil Microbiology
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Role of microorganisms in the decomposition of organic substances, transformation of nitrogen and mineral substances in the soil; interrelationships between higher plants and microorganisms; importance of microorganisms in soil fertility. Includes Honors sections. Preq: MICR 3050; and MICR 4040 or PES 4900.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
