PRTM 4740 - Advanced Recreation Resources Management
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Advanced topics in recreation resource management focusing on management strategies and techniques for addressing common resource and social problems in recreation resource management. Case studies and problem analysis are emphasized. Students must have a 2.0 cumulative grade-point average to enroll in this course. Includes Honors sections. Preq: PRTM 2700 and senior standing.
