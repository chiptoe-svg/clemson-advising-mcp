HIST 4900 - Senior Seminar
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Seminar in current research themes in history. Students conduct directed research on a particular topic and learn research, writing, and oral presentation techniques. Seminar topics vary from section to section and from semester to semester. Preq: History major or Secondary Education: Social Studies major; and Senior standing and HIST 2990 with a C or better.
