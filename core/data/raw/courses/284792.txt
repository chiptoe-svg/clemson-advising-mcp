ENSP 2000 - Introduction to Environmental Science
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Basic principles of environmental science, including ecology, energy, resources, waste management, and air, water, and soil pollution. Consideration of issues, specific cases, investigative approaches, and remedial actions. Preq or concurrent enrollment: Any course that satisfies the Natural Science with a Lab General Education requirement.
