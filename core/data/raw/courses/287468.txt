GC 3780 - Brand Agency Practicum
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Students work with external brand partners (clients) to solve problems for the brand and brand management team. Students work with intern leaders and mentors to gain skills and effectiveness. May be repeated for a maximum of nine credits. Preq: GC 3700. Coreq: GC 3781.
