EDSP 4980 - Directed Teaching in Special Education
9 Credits (27 Contact Hours)
Comprehensive course providing a full-time, semester-long experience for preservice special education teachers who plan to teach individuals with mild/moderate disabilities. Generally the last course in the program; provides teaching experience under the supervision of University and school personnel. Meets Read to Succeed state requirements. Offered spring semester only. Preq: EDSP 4960.
