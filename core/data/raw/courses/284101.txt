CHE 2300 - Fluids/Heat Transfer
4 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
General principles of chemical engineering and study of fluid flow, fluid transportation, and heat transmission. Special emphasis is placed on theory and its practical application to design. Preq: CHE 2110. Preq or concurrent enrollment: CHE 2200 and MATH 2080. Coreq: CHE 2301.
