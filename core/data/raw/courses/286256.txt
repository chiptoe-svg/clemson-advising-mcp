PKSC 2200 - Product/Package Design and Prototyping
4 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Overview of structural and graphic development tools for product and packaging design. Focus on digital creation, photo rendering, wide-format plotting/proofing, rapid prototyping, visualization and real-time 2d/3d design. Course utilizes online lectures and hands-on laboratory experience at The Sonoco Institute. Preq: PKSC 1020 and Packaging Science major. Coreq: PKSC 2201.
