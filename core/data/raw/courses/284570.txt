EDSC 4470 - Teaching Internship in Secondary Science
9 Credits (37 Contact Hours)
Supervised teaching internship in assigned secondary public school science classroom. Meets part of requirement for South Carolina science teaching certification. Taught spring semester only. Preq: EDSC 4270 and admission to the professional level. Coreq: EDSC 4570.
