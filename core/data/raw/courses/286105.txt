GBS 1000 - Introduction to Global Black Studies
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course introduces students to the varied peoples and cultures of the African diaspora, including the historical, colonial, and contemporary political-economic contexts, as well as the religious, migratory, and other cultural practices.
