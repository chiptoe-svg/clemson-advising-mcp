ECON 4600 - Environmental Law and Economics

3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)

As an introduction to environmental law and economics, this course examines the nature of environmental conflicts and the various mechanisms for resolving those conflicts (e.g., voluntary exchange, common law remedies, and prescriptive regulation). Through reading, writing, and discussion, students evaluate a variety of environmental policies from the distinct and often conflicting perspectives of ethics, efficacy, and efficiency. Preq: ECON 3140.
 

This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
