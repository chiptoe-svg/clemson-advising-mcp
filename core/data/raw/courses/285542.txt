LARC 3810 - Landscape Architecture Professional Practice
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Seminar pertaining to the practice of landscape architecture and professional procedures. Topics include the professional relationship between the landscape architect and client, including ethical, legal and business issues, as well as portfolio development. Preq: Junior standing and Landscape Architecture major.
