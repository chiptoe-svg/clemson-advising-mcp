CPSC 4240 - System Administration and Security
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Covers topics related to the administration and security of computer systems. Primary emphasis is on the administration and security of contemporary operating systems. Preq: One of the following courses with a grade of C or higher: CPSC 3220 or CPSC 3600 or ECE 3220 or ECE 4380.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
