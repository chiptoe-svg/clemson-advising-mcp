POSC 3760 - Democratization
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Analysis of the process by which countries move from authoritarian forms of rule to democratic rule. This course covers the steps involved in the democratization process, as well as how and why the democratization process can go wrong. Preq: Sophomore standing.
