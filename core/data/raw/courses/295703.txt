DPA 4100 - Digital Lighting
3 Credits (6 Contact Hours)
A study of the physics of light and color, and an examination of the controllable qualities of light and the vital role that lighting plays in animation and storytelling. Students observe light in the world around them, gather reference materials that communicate design ideas, develop a lighting approach, and light digital scenes accordingly. Animation software is used for projects, but the course is not designed to teach a specific program. Preq: DPA 3070.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
