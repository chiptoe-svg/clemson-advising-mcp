HLTH 4800 - Community Health Promotion
4 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Focuses on the participatory approach in the planning and implementation of community health programs. Emphasizes professional ethics, needs assessment, coalition building, proposal writing, and implementation of special events in the community. Preq: HLTH 2400 and HLTH 2980. Coreq: HLTH 4801.
