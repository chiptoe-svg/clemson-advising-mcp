AI 4820 - Special Topics in Artificial Intelligence
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
A dynamic, topic-driven exploration of emerging developments in artificial intelligence. Focus areas change each semester to reflect current trends and breakthroughs. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits, but only if different topics are covered. Preq: CPSC 1070 with a grade of C or higher; and one of IE 3610 or MATH 3020 or STAT 3090 or STAT 3300, with a grade of C or higher.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
