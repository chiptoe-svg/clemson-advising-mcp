EDML 4540 - Middle Level Language Arts Capstone Seminar
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Capstone seminar accompanying supervised middle level language arts teaching internship. Satisfies part of the requirement for South Carolina middle level language arts certification. Preq: EDML 4240 and admission to the professional level. Coreq: EDML 4440.
