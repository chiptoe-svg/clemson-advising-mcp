HIST 3280 - United States Legal History to 1890
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Survey of the American legal system in its historical perspective from Colonial times to 1890. Emphasizes the relationship between law and society, the way in which the practice of law changed American society, and the way in which social development affected both the theory and practice of the law.
