JAPN 4910 - Senior Seminar in Japanese Literature
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Close readings of various works of premodern and modern Japanese literature. Includes study of important authors and their representative works in prose and poetry. Familiarizes students with the cultural and linguistic nuances of literature in the original language. All readings and activities are in Japanese. Preq: JAPN 3060.
