SPAN 3180 - Spanish Through Culture
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Topic-generated conversation course in Spanish through a broad array of artistic manifestations in the Hispanic World emphasizing conversation and short written exercises. Focuses on one Hispanic culture topic which provides a basis for class discussion and short written compositions in Spanish. Preq: At least three credits of 3000-level SPAN coursework.
