CE 3410 - Introduction to Fluid Mechanics
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to fluid mechanics, including hydrostatics and fluid flow. This course covers the principles of hydrostatics, mass, momentum, and energy conservation, and dimensional analysis. These tools are then applied to solve engineering problems in external flows, pressurized conduit flows, and open channel flows. Preq: CE 2010 with a grade of C or better. Preq or concurrent enrollment: MATH 2060 with a grade of C or better.
