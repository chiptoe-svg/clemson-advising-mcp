MGT 3020 - Personal Leadership
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course focuses on developing essential personal leadership skills, such as communication, emotional intelligence, teamwork, and problem-solving, to enhance both personal and professional success. Through practical exercises and real-world applications, participants learn to navigate interpersonal dynamics, foster collaboration, and improve leadership abilities. Preq: BUS 2010.
