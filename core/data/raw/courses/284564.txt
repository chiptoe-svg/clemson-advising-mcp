EDSC 4270 - Teaching Secondary Science
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Development of instructional practices and materials for teaching secondary school science (biological, earth, and physical sciences); familiarization with secondary science curriculum materials; includes field experiences in local schools. Includes Honors sections. Taught fall semester only. Preq: Second semester Junior standing, admission to the professional level, ED 1050 and ED 3010 and EDF 3020 and EDF 3350; at least 18 hours of science coursework and a minimum grade-point average of 2.75. Preq or concurrent enrollment: EDLT 4980. Coreq: EDSC 4271.
