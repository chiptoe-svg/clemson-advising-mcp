EDSC 3260 - Practicum in Secondary Mathematics
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Pre-service secondary mathematics teachers gain both content and pedagogical knowledge by observing and reflecting upon the classroom practices of selected in-service high school mathematics teachers. Preq: Admission to the professional level. Coreq: EDSC 3261.
