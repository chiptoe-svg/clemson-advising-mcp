HORT 3030 - Woody Landscape Plant Identification and Use
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Woody, ornamental plants and their aesthetic and functional uses in landscape developments. Study covers habit of growth, ultimate size, texture effect, period of bloom, color, and cultural requirements. Coreq: HORT 3031.
