MKT 3250 - Professional Selling
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduces contemporary theories about selling to organizational buyers in the context of long-term relationships as a component of the marketing mix. Role playing, video-taped presentations, and other techniques are employed to enhance interpersonal communication skills. Preq: MKT 3010.
