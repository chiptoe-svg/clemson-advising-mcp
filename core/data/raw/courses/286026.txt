NURS 3100 - Health Assessment
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Introduces concepts of health, wellness, and illness. Focuses on physical, psychosocial, and cultural assessment for the well adult client with variations across the lifespan. Includes interviewing techniques. Preq: BIOL 2220 with a C or better. Coreq: NURS 3101 and NURS 3120 and NURS 3400.
