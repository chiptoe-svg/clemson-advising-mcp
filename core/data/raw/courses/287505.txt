POSC 4410 - Applied Political Science Research
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course is a general introduction to common data science tools and applications. Topics covered include both software training, theoretic backgrounds in statistical model, as well as heavy emphasis on understanding model interpretation and critique. Preq: POSC 3410.
