JUST 3140 - Victimology
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course examines patterns of crime and victimization and their distribution in terms of demographic characteristics and geographic areas, as well as offender-victim interaction, theories of victimization, and prevention of victimization. Preq: JUST 2890 or SOC 2890.
