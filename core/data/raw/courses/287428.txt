AUE 2010 - Human Factors and Fundamentals of Vehicle Design
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to vehicle design from the end user’s perspective (e.g. market segments, user studies, cognition, perception, ethics). These principles, which apply widely across engineering disciplines, are exemplified in the automotive context. Preq or concurrent enrollment: ENGR 2080.
