MUSC 4050 - Instrumental and Vocal Arranging
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Advanced study of the properties of instruments and voices and their combination in various small and large ensembles. Emphasis is placed on applying this knowledge to the creation of instrumental and vocal arrangements. Preq: MUSC 1800 and MUSC 1420. Coreq: MUSC 4051.
