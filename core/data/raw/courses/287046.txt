EDML 4240 - Teaching Middle Level Language Arts
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Development of instructional practices and materials appropriate for middle level language arts and familiarization with middle level language arts curriculum materials. Includes field experiences in local schools in preparation for student teaching. Preq: EDML 3240 or EDML 3280; and admission to the professional level.  Coreq: EDML 4241.
