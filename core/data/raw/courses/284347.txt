ECE 2720 - Computer Organization
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introductory course in computer organization and architecture. Topics include CPUs, memory, I/O, processor families, buses, peripherals, microarchitectures, and instruction sets. Includes Honors sections. Preq: ECE 2010 and ECE 2210, each with a grade of C or higher.
