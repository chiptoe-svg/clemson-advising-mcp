EES 4500 - Professional Seminar
1 Credit (1 Contact Hour)
Covers various topics related to skills and techniques for evaluating career opportunities, seeking and obtaining environmental engineering employment, career development, professional registration, professional ethics, and other factors necessary for achieving success in a professional career. Course enables students to make decisions that will help them succeed in their careers. Preq or concurrent enrollment: EES 3030 and EES 3040 and EES 3050.
