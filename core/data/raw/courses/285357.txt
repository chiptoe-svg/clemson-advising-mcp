HORT 2120 - Introduction to Turfgrass Culture
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Studies of the introductory principles associated with the art and science of turfgrass culture. Develops an understanding of the history and evolution of turfgrasses and turfgrass culture. Explores career potentials in turfgrass management. Explains the basic scientific principles and techniques associated with the propagation and establishment of fine turfgrasses.
