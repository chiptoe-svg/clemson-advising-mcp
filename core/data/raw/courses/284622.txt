EES 4300 - Air Pollution Engineering
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introductory course in air pollution and its control. Topics include air pollutants and effects, sources, dispersion models, engineering controls, and air-quality legislation. Preq: BE 4100 or EES 2020 or EES 4010.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
