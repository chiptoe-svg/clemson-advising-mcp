MGT 3030 - Global Business
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Provides a survey of the global environment, institutional differences, and concepts in doing business internationally. Topics include foreign direct investment, international trade, and the role of businesses and their stakeholders in the evolving global economic integration. Preq: MGT 2010.
