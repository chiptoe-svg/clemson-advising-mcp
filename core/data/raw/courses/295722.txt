EDHC 4890 - Human Capital Education and Development Internship Preparation
1 Credit (1 Contact Hour)
Prepares students for their required internship in Human Capital Education and Development while building foundational career development skills, including job search strategies, professional communication, ethical decision making, critical thinking, and problem solving. Students also explore workplace readiness, workforce leadership and development, create a personalized career plan, and gain an understanding of the capstone proposal process. Preq: Sophomore standing.
