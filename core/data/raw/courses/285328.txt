HLTH 4300 - Health Promotion of the Aged
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Focuses on analysis and evaluation of health issues and health problems of the aged. Emphasizes concepts of positive health behaviors. Health majors are given enrollment priority. Preq: HLTH 2980.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
