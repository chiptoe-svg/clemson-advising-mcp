EDHC 3100 - Systems and Organizational Learning Improvement
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to systems and organizational improvement focused on human capital education and development topics. This course focuses on systems design and change with a focus on theory and models, leadership, data analytics, communication, networking, and evaluation. Preq: Sophomore standing.
