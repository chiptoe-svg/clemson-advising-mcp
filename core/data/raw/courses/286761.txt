WFB 4180 - Fisheries Management and Conservation
4 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
History, theory and practice of fisheries management. Synthesis of fish population dynamics and manipulation, habitat management, and human management to achieve fisheries management objectives. Analysis of fisheries data to inform management. Coreq: WFB 4181.
