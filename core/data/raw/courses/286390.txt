PRTM 3010 - Recreation and Society
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Investigation of the role of recreation in a technological and work-oriented society. Particular emphasis is on recreation behavior, resources, and programming in public and private organizations that serve the public wants. Not open to Parks, Recreation and Tourism Management majors; may not be substituted or otherwise used to meet Parks, Recreation and Tourism Management area requirements. Students must have a 2.0 cumulative grade-point average to enroll in this course.
