PES 4760 - Sustainable Food Systems Towards Global Food Security
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Covers the concept of food security linking to different agriculture production systems, human nutrition and food policies. Crop production systems include modern farming systems, small holder farming systems, and organic production systems to increase global nutritious food production. Preq: Junior standing.
