CCPD 1670 - Intrapersonal and Interpersonal Dynamics of Leadership
1 Credit (1 Contact Hour)
This course examines leadership from a number of perspectives. Many college students have taken on leadership responsibilities during college, but may not fully realize the leadership skills they have developed or feel comfortable or capable of applying these in their future careers. Additionally, they need to better understand the complex intrapersonal and interpersonal dynamics related to leadership, especially in workplace settings. Preq: Consent of instructor.
