PRTM 3830 - Golf Shop Operations
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Provides students with the knowledge and skills necessary to succeed as managers of golf shops. Particular emphasis is on fundamental business planning, development of policies and procedures, merchandising, inventory control, pricing, and customer service. Students must have a 2.0 cumulative grade-point average to enroll in this course. Preq: PRTM 2810 and PRTM 2820 and PRTM 2950.
