HORT 4710 - Advanced Turfgrass Internship
0-6 Credits (1-6 Contact Hours)
Preplanned work experience under competent supervision in approved agency dealing with horticultural endeavors. Gives advanced students on-the-job learning opportunities to apply acquired knowledge and skills. Monthly reports and final departmental seminar required. Undergraduates may accumulate a maximum of six credits for participation in HORT 2710 and/or HORT 4710. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits. Preq: HORT 2710 and junior standing.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
