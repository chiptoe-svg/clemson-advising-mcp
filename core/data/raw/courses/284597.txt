EDSP 4950 - Communication and Collaboration in Special Education
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Focuses on effective communication skills for preservice special education teachers to encourage collaboration among relevant stakeholders and improve outcomes for individuals with disabilities. Meets Read to Succeed state requirements. Preq: Sophomore standing. Preq or concurrent enrollment: EDSP 4980. Coreq: EDSP 4810.
