CE 3420 - Applied Hydraulics and Hydrology
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Study of hydrologic cycle, including precipitation, evapotranspiration, infiltration, and runoff. Includes hydrograph analysis, gradually varied flow in open channel flow, design of stable channels, flood routing, groundwater hydraulics, flood frequency analysis, and hydrologic design. Preq: CE 3410 with a grade of C or better. Preq or concurrent enrollment: CE 3430.
