ENSP 2010 - Exploring Environmental Science
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to basic principles of environmental science, including climate variation, atmospheric and water pollution, and global challenges. The course emphasizes the practical applications through hands-on activities, educational field trips and projects. Credit toward a degree will be given for only one of ENSP 2000 and ENSP 2010. Preq or concurrent enrollment: Any course that satisfies the General Education Natural Science with a Lab requirement.
