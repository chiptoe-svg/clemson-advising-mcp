POSC 4880 - Global Politics Simulations
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course immerses students in global politics through applied simulations mirroring historic and current events. Students assume roles as political figures, marginalized communities, or institutional representatives, balancing overarching goals (negotiating treaties, crafting accords) with individual aims (maintaining popularity, winning elections). This allows students to test political science theories in evolving situations. Scenarios may include humanitarian intervention, economic statecraft, nuclear disarmament, global health crises, and conflict resolution. Preq: Sophomore standing.
