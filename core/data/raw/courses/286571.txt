SOC 3100 - Modern Intimate Relationships
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course provides an examination of singlehood, dating, intimate relations, mate selection, living together, and marital relations in the U.S. from a sociological perspective. Includes Honors sections. Preq: Sophomore standing.
