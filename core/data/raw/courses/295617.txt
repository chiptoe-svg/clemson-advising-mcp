SPAN 3120 - Spanish Grammar and Composition for Heritage Speakers
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Supports heritage speakers of Spanish, who have already mastered their speaking abilities, but need to improve their linguistic/grammar skills. Students must interview before enrolling in the course. Preq: Consent of instructor.
