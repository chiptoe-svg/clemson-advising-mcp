PRTM 3800 - Community Recreation in South Carolina
3 Credits (1 Contact Hours)
Students study indoor and outdoor recreation facilities, governmental jurisdiction, funding, programming, management, and staffing at community recreation agencies throughout South Carolina during a hands-on five-day field trip. Students must have a 2.0 cumulative grade-point average to enroll in this course. Coreq: PRTM 3801.
