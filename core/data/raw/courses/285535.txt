LARC 4540 - Urban Design Studio
6 Credits (1 Contact Hours)
Landscape architectural design in the urban context. Students study urban issues and offer design solutions for urban areas. The course includes a reading and theory component, as well as an opportunity to collaborate with architecture students. Students attend an international field trip during Spring Break. Preq: Senior standing. Coreq: LARC 4541.
