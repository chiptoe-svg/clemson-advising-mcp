EDML 4580 - Middle Level Social Studies Capstone Seminar
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Capstone seminar accompanying supervised middle level social studies teaching internship. Satisfies part of the requirement for South Carolina middle level social studies certification. Preq: EDML 4280 and admission to the professional level. Coreq: EDML 4480.
