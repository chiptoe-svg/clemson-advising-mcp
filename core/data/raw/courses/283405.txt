AGRB 3090 - Economics of Agricultural Marketing
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
General course in marketing agricultural commodities with particular emphasis upon food products. Analyzes efficiency criteria, consumer behavior, market organizations and institutions, and marketing functions. Includes Honors sections. Preq: AGRB 2020 with a grade of C or higher or ECON 2000 with a grade of C or higher or ECON 2110 with a grade of C or higher.
