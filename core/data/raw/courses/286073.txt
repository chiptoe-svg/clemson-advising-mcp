NUTR 2100 - Nutrition and Physical Activity
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Topics include role of carbohydrates, fats, and proteins on energy utilization during exercise; altering body composition and improving fitness with diet and physical activity; importance of fluid intake on performance; effectiveness of dietary supplements and ergogenic aids; and choosing a diet appropriate for individual physical activity levels. Preq: BIOL 1200; and one of BIOL 1220 or BIOL 1230.
