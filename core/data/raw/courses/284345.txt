ECE 2620 - Electric Circuits II
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Continuation of the study of electric circuits, including sinusoidal steady state circuits, magnetically-coupled circuits, power in sinusoidal steady state circuits, three-phase circuits, transient analysis using Laplace transforms, frequency response, two-port parameters, and ideal operational amplifier circuits. Includes Honors sections. Preq: ECE 2020 and MATH 2060 and PHYS 2210, each with a grade of C or higher.
