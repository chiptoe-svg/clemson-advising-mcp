FOR 2210 - Forest Biology
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Study of woody plant form and function, wood properties, general physiology and forest biomes of North America. Presented as a companion course to dendrology lab. Preq: BIOL 1030 and BIOL 1050; or BIOL 1100 and BIOL 1110. Coreq: FOR 2050.
