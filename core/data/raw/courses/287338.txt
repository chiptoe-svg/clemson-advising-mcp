AGM 3050 - Agricultural Safety
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Students develop the knowledge, awareness, and attitude necessary to become effective supervisors, managers, employers, employees, or educators of safety topics in the agricultural industry. They learn how to develop and implement strategies to promote a safe work environment and become familiar with various regulations and standards.
