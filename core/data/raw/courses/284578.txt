EDSC 4580 - Secondary Social Studies Capstone Seminar
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Capstone seminar accompanying supervised high school social studies teaching internship. Satisfies part of requirement for South Carolina secondary certification. Offered spring semester only. Preq: EDSC 4280 and admission to the professional level.  Coreq: EDSC 4480 and EDSC 4581.
