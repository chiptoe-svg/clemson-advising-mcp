MUSC 1120 - Intermediate Class Guitar
1 Credit (2 Contact Hours)
Continued work on guitar skills, including finger-style, strumming, pick playing, ensemble playing, and soloing. Also includes music theory for guitarists such as keys, scales, and chord building, as well as discussions of the impact of various players and composers on the medium. Applied music fee is assessed. Preq: MUSC 1110.
