PRTM 4950 - Professional Golf Management Seminar IV
1 Credits (1 Contact Hours)
Covers golf shop merchandising and inventory management and supervising and delegating. Emphasizes topics covered in the PGA/PGM Training Program Level III checkpoint. Students must have a 2.0 cumulative grade-point average to enroll in this course. Preq: PRTM 2830 and PRTM 3830.
