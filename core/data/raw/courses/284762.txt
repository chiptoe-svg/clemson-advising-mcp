ENGR 1510 - Engineering Skills
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Provides students a solid foundation of skills for solving engineering problems. Students demonstrate problem solving techniques with dimensions and units, use modeling techniques, and interpret a variety of experimental results. Preq: Membership in the General Engineering Learning Community. Coreq: ENGR 1511.
