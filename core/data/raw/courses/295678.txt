POSC 3420 - Data Visualization and Storytelling in Political Science
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course introduces the basics of data as evidence and data communication, with particular attention given to presentation skills. The course explores data through visualization and summary statistics using a variety of tools. Students learn how stories can be told through data and best practices in communicating data, and become able to produce their own data projects. Preq: Sophomore standing.
