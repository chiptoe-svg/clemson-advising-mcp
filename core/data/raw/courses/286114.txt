PES 2020 - Soils
4 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduces world land resources, soil formation, classification, and mineralogy. Emphasizes basic chemical and physical properties of soil. Also discusses soil microorganisms, plant nutrients, and fertilization. Soil properties are related to growth. Preq: CH 1010 or CH 1020 or GEOL 1010, with a C or higher. Coreq: PES 2021.
