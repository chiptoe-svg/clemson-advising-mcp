BE 4740 - Biosystems Engineering Design Project Management
1 Credit (1 Contact Hour)
Study of biological systems design using hydrology principles, fluid mechanics, bioprocessing, heat/mass transfer, instrumentation, mechanical unit operations, and structural principles for project design, scheduling, and cost estimation. Topics also include engineering ethics, professional development, written and oral communication, and job skills. Senior portfolios are also developed. Preq: Senior standing in Biosystems Engineering. Coreq: BE 4750.
