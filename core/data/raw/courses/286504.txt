PSYC 4840 - Practicum in Clinical Psychology
3 Credits (1 Contact Hour)
Students apply classroom theory in solving individual and community problems through interaction with community agencies and other professional groups in the mental health area. Students have limited but well-controlled contact with actual clinical problems as they occur in the community environment. Preq: PSYC 3100 and PSYC 3830. Coreq: PSYC 4841.
