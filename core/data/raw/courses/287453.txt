HP 3010 - Researching Historic Properties
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduces the professional research methods and strategies used in historic preservation studies of the built environment. This course is designed to provide students with the tools to uncover and showcase a building’s story in order to preserve its role and relevancy in today’s society. Preq: HP 2010.
