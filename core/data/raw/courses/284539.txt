EDLT 4600 - Foundations of Reading: Assessment and Instruction
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Provides preservice teachers with an understanding of foundations and methods of reading assessment and instruction (oral language, phonemic awareness, phonics, vocabulary, fluency and comprehension) in the elementary setting in grades 2-6. Designed to meet Read to Succeed state requirements. Preq or concurrent enrollment: ED 3010.
