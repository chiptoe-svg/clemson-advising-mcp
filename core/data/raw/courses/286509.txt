PSYC 4980 - Team-Based Research
1-4 Credits (1-4 Contact Hours)
Students conduct psychological research and learn about phases of the research process with a team of their peers under the direction of a faculty member. The collaborative nature of psychological research is emphasized. Includes Honors sections. May be repeated for a maximum of 18 credits. Preq: Consent of instructor.
