NURS 4030 - Medical-Surgical III: Complex Nursing of Adults
5 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Focuses on the biological, psychological, philosophical, and sociocultural influences on complex health problems related to acute and traumatic conditions. Emphasizes the concepts of circulation, oxygenation, homeostasis, and compensation in acutely ill adults. Preq: NURS 4010 and NURS 4110 and NURS 4120, each with a C or better. Coreq: NURS 4031 and NURS 4100 and NURS 4150.
