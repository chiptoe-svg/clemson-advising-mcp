PRTM 4231 - Adaptive Sports and Recreation Laboratory
0 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Non-credit laboratory to accompany PRTM 4230. This lab course provides students enrolled in PRTM 4230 with the opportunity to engage in hands on skill development associated with the provision of a variety of adaptive sports and recreation activities. Coreq: PRTM 4230.
