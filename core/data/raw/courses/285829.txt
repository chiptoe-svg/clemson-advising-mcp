MICR 4500 - Advanced Microbiology Laboratory I
2 Credits (1 Contact Hours)
Application of knowledge and techniques learned in the Introductory Microbiology Lab with new topics on microbial ecology, diversity and physiology. Experiments in soil, marine and environmental microbiology will be conducted. Preq: MICR 4040. Coreq: MICR 4501.
