PHYS 2070 - General Physics I
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introductory course for students who are not majoring in physical science or engineering. Covers such topics as mechanics, waves, fluids, and thermal physics. Credit for a degree will be given for only one of PHYS 1220, PHYS 2000, or PHYS 2070. Preq: MATH 1020 or MATH 1040 or MATH 1050 or MATH 1060 or MATH 1070 or a score of 620 or higher on the SAT Math section or a score of 26 or higher on the ACT Math section or preq or concurrent enrollment in PHYS 1020.
