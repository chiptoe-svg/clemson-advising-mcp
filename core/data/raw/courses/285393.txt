IE 2100 - Design and Analysis of Work Systems
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Introduction to the tools and techniques used to design and analyze work systems for human use, including process improvement, workplace design and an introduction to the field of human factors and ergonomics. Preq: ENGL 1030 with a grade of C or higher and PHYS 1220 with a grade of C or higher. Coreq: IE 2101.
