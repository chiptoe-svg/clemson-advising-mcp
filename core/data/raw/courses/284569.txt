EDSC 4460 - Teaching Internship in Secondary Mathematics
9 Credits (27 Contact Hours)
Prospective secondary mathematics teachers apply teaching and learning theories in a field-based setting through an internship in a secondary public school classroom. Taught spring semester only. Preq: EDSC 4260 and admission to the professional level.  Coreq: EDSC 4560.
