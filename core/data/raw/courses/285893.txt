ML 1010 - Leadership Fundamentals I
2 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Study of leadership focused at the individual level. Students learn effective communicating skills, ethical decision making, small group management, and mental and physical conditioning. Skills are applied in a variety of challenging training events during laboratory, including rappelling, water survival, land navigation, and team athletics. Coreq: ML 1011.
