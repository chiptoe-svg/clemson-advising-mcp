MUSC 4300 - Conducting
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Study of choral and instrumental conducting. Emphasis is on manual conducting techniques, attitudes, philosophies, and responsibilities necessary for the preparation, planning, and execution of artistic conducting. Preq: MUSC 1420.
