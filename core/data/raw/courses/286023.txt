NURS 3050 - Psychosocial Nursing
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Lifespan approach to examine psychosocial, developmental, family, and cultural factors that influence individuals from diverse populations and their families in the promotion, maintenance, and restoration of health. The use of the nursing process, critical thinking, therapeutic communication, and psychosocial nursing interventions is explored. Coreq: NURS 3030.
