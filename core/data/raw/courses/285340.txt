HON 2200 - Studies in Social Science
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Discipline specific social science seminar including a disciplinary introduction (anthropology, economics, history, political science, psychology or sociology) and a detailed examination of specific theories and methods within that discipline. May be repeated for a maximum of 12 credits, but only if different disciplines are covered. Preq: Membership in the Clemson University Honors College.
