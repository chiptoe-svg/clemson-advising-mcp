GLCH 1510 - Perspectives and Pathways Through a Pandemic
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course examines the Covid-19 pandemic from various perspectives. Students explore essential topics relevant to the prevention and control of Covid-19 through the reading of scholarly articles, periodicals, commentaries, social media posts, and other sources. Other topics include a historical overview of global pandemics and what has been learned through various societal responses; an examination of disease prevention and control strategies; an examination of cultures and populations most at risk; and potential health, economic, societal and individual impacts of the pandemic. Across all topics, special emphasis is placed on identifying top ethical dilemmas as experienced by individuals, groups, and global communities during the Covid-19 pandemic. Students also have the opportunity to reflect upon their own experiences of the Covid-19 pandemic.
