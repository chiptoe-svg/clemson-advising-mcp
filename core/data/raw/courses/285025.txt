GC 4510 - Special Projects in Graphic Communications
1-6 Credits (1-6 Contact Hours)
Advanced projects covering theory and/or practices going beyond the scope of regular coursework. Written project approval is required before registering. Includes Honors sections. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits with advisor’s approval. Preq: Junior standing and completion of three graphic communications courses.
