JAPN 4300 - Language in Japanese Society
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Explores the dynamic relationship between language and social structure in Japan. Examines everyday interactions and media discourse from linguistic perspectives. Course materials include Japanese examples with English translations. Instruction is in English. Preq: Junior standing.
