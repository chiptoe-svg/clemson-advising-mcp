PHIL 3750 - Minds and Machines
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Examines controversial questions in artificial intelligence and the Computational Theory of Mind. Topics may include: “Can machines think?” “What’s involved in being able to think?” “Can machines reason, understand, be conscious, be self-aware, learn, be creative, have emotions, and use natural language?” Focus is on manmade computers and the mind as computer.
