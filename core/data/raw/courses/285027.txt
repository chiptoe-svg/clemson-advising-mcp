GC 4800 - Senior Seminar in Graphic Communications
1 Credit (1 Contact Hour)
A study of the current trends and issues in the graphic communications industry. The class centers on group discussions dealing with relevant topics facing the graphic communications professional today, and makes extensive use of visiting expertise. Students draw upon their academic and internship experiences and library research to facilitate discussion. Must be taken during the student’s last two semesters on campus. Preq: Senior standing.
