PRTM 4900 - Senior Independent Study
1-6 Credits (1-6 Contact Hours)
In cooperation with and under supervision of a faculty member, students develop and execute a field study or community project. Students must have a 2.0 cumulative grade-point average to enroll in this course. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits. Preq: Senior standing and consent of instructor.
