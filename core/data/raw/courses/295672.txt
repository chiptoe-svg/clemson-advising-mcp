CE 2070 - Structural Mechanics Laboratory
1 Credit (3 Contact Hours)
Laboratory investigation class that examines the deformation of materials under various loading conditions including axial loading, bending, torsion, and buckling. Preq or concurrent enrollment: CE 2060 with a grade of C or better.
