HIST 1720 - The West and the World I
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Examines the history of the West from early times until 1648. After a comparative evaluation of the emergence of civilization around the globe, course concentrates on the history of the peoples of Europe up to the age of European exploration and overseas expansion. Includes Honors sections.
