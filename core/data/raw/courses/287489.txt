HON 2990 - Academic Development
1 Credit (1 Contact Hour)
Introduces Honors students to the college experience, student development, leadership identity, professional competencies, and related topics, including emphasis on the unique opportunities and challenges facing high-achieving students. To be taken Pass/No Pass only. May be repeated for a maximum of two credits. Preq: Membership in the Clemson University Honors College.
