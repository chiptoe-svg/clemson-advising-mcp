MSE 4340 - Fundamentals of Additive Manufacturing
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduces basic Additive Manufacturing (AM) principles, history, and general process. Topics include fundamentals of AM technologies, process selection guidelines, post-processing, direct digital manufacturing and progress of AM technologies. Credit will be given for only one of MSE 4340 or ME 4560. Preq: MSE 2100.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
