PES 4920 - Senior Honors Research
3 Credits (1 Contact Hours)
Continuation of PES 4910. Senior division honors research in an agricultural sciences curriculum. Upon termination of the research project, students submit formal written reports and make final oral presentations of results to the College Honors Program Committee. Professor-student discussions of additional topics are arranged. Coreq: PES 4921.
