EDHC 4330 - Advanced Information Technology and the Future of Work
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Provides students with an understanding of the issues and contributions of technology and the future of work at the human-technology frontier. Examples of these relationships are taken from political, economic, social, geographic, historical contexts, and from analyses of contemporary technological interventions in a globalized society. Preq: Sophomore standing.
