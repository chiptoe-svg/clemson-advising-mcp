LS 1330 - Women’s Shotgun Shooting
1 Credit (3 Contact Hours)
Introduces basic shotgun shooting skills and firearm safety. Topics include gun fitting, skeet and trap shooting, and gun and range safety. Course is designed to provide women a comfortable environment in which to learn the necessary skills to participate in shotgun shooting.
