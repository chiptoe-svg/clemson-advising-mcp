MGT 3510 - Business Analytics for Competitive Advantage
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course focuses on problem solving using statistics to analyze business data. Case studies are used to address business problems in data management, model fitting, model interpretation, and diagnostics. Model approaches include selected topics from multivariate analysis, exploratory data analysis, and linear models. Preq: MGT 3100.
