WFB 4160 - Fisheries Techniques
4 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Application of field and laboratory methods in fisheries sciences and research. Experience with fisheries equipment and techniques, including capture, handling and processing of fishes. Analysis of fisheries data. Preq: WFB 3000. Coreq: WFB 4161.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
