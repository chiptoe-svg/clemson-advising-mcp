WFB 3500 - Principles of Fish and Wildlife Biology
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to principles of fisheries and wildlife biology on which sound management practices are based. Interrelationships of vertebrate and invertebrate biology, habitat, and population dynamics are covered. Preq: One of the following combinations: BIOL 1030 and BIOL 1050 and BIOL 1040 and BIOL 1060; OR BIOL 1100 and BIOL 1110.
