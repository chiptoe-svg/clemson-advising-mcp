LANG 2500 - Introduction to World Languages
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to fundamental questions concerning the nature and use of human language throughout the world. Emphasizes the definition, genesis and theoretical characterization of language, as well as its role in social and political discourses. All readings and discussions are in English.
