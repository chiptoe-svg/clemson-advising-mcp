HON 2060 - Controversies and Innovations
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Honors seminar that examines historical, contemporary, and/or future challenges through interdisciplinary lenses. Students analyze contested issues; compare various cultural, political, and ethical perspectives; and assess how controversies and innovations arise and evolve. May be repeated for a maximum 12 credits, but only if different topics are covered. Preq: Membership in the Clemson University Honors College.
