PHYS 4810 - Physics of Surfaces
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction for advanced undergraduates to the physics and chemical physics of solid surfaces and to the interaction of atoms and molecules with those surfaces. Preq: PHYS 3120 and PHYS 3220 and PHYS 3250 and PHYS 3260 and PHYS 4410.
