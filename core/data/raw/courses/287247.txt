BIOL 3110 - Introduction to Toxicology
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course provides a first introduction to the field of toxicology and covers the basic concepts of toxicology, followed by typical examples of toxicological issues in medical and pharmaceutical fields, food toxicology and pesticides, occupational exposures, natural toxins, environmental toxicology, and risk assessment. Special attention is given to the interactions between the science of toxicology and societal implications. Preq: BIOL 1040 and BIOL 1060; or BIOL 1110. Preq or concurrent enrollment: CH 2010 or CH 2230.
