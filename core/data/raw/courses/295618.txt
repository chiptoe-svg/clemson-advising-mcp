RED 4100 - Commercial Real Estate
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
An advanced survey to acquaint students with the theories, practices and principles of real estate. Topics include urban economics, real estate law, governmental controls, real estate valuation, market research, investment analysis, and the development process. Through coursework, students are exposed to the collaborative and interpersonal skills required to succeed in the real estate industry. Preq: Junior standing.
