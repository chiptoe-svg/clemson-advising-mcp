PSYC 3100 - Advanced Experimental Psychology
4 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Continuation of PSYC 3090. Focus is on techniques of empirical research (experiments, quasi-experiments, non-experimental research, etc.) that are widely used in psychology. Students design and carry out their own empirical research projects normally involving data collected from human participants. Extensive practice in the writing of reports is included. Preq: Psychology major; and PSYC 2010; and PSYC 2030 with a grade of C or higher; and PSYC 3090. Coreq: PSYC 3101.
