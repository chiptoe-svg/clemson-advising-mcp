CPSC 1900 - Teaching Assistant Fundamentals for Computer Science
1 Credit (1 Contact Hour)
This course is required for all new undergraduate teaching assistants, lab assistants, and/or graders for School of Computing undergraduate courses. Students learn policies, procedures, best practices, and pedagogy associated with their positions. Example topics include question redirection, privacy, grading using rubrics, writing grading scripts, lab management, and debugging techniques. To be taken Pass/No Pass only. Preq: Consent of department.
