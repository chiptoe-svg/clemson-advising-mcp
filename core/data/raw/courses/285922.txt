MSE 4610 - Polymer Fiber Engineering
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Familiarizes students with the physical properties of textile and high performance fibers and how these properties influence process and end-use performance; method of measuring those properties; and how those properties are related to structural features of the fiber. Includes Honors sections. Preq: MSE 4150.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
