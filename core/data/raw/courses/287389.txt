HON 1960 - First-Year Seminar: Global Challenges
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
First-year honors students consider current or enduring challenges at the local, regional, national, and/or global level. The course allows students to develop critical thinking skills, evaluate varying perspectives, and consider ethical dilemmas and decision-making. Topics vary. Preq: Membership in the Clemson University Honors College.
