LS 1250 - Budget Travel
1 Credit (3 Contact Hours)
Teaches the necessary skills to travel internationally on a budget. Students learn how to get the best airfares, research destinations, and build an itinerary. Packing, security, local transportation, and culture/reverse-culture shock are also discussed.
