AL 3810 - Introduction to Coaching Athletes with Learning Disabilities
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
All aspiring athletic coaches and administrators teach and coach some student-athletes with learning disabilities or other learning needs. This course introduces students to the characteristics of the student-athlete with disabilities and introduces strategies that enable maximal success in their sport. This course includes the characteristics and prevalence of learning disabilities as well as the implications for athletic participation and coaching strategies for enhanced athletic performance. Preq: Sophomore standing.
