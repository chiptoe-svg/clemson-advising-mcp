MUSC 3360 - Percussion Ensemble
1 Credit (2 Contact Hours)
Ensembles: study and performance of percussion ensemble literature. One two-hour class meeting each week, for which a minimum of two hours of individual practice is required. Audition required. Preq or concurrent enrollment: MUSC 3310 or MUSC 3610 or MUSC 3620 or MUSC 3630 or MUSC 3640 or MUSC 3690.
