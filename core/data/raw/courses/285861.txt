MICR 4300 - Soil Microbiology Laboratory
1 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Examines microbes residing in the soil and their effects on the soil substratum and resident plant communities. Topics include biogeochemistry, microbial isolation, microcosm development, and characterization of soil microbial communities. Preq or concurrent enrollment: MICR 4100.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
