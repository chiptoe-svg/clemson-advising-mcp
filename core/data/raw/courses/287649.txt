EDLT 4720 - Cultural and Linguistic Diversity in PK-12 Education
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Examination of multiple components of culture, including sociocultural identity markers with emphasis on language and ethnicity. Focus on how cultural and linguistic diversity impact student identity and how this identity is tied to student learning and academic achievement. Preq: Sophomore standing.
