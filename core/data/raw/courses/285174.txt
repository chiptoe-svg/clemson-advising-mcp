GW 4020 - Great Works of Science
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Understanding of science in terms of its history and its approach to problem-solving through study of selected great works. Emphasis is on developing students’ abilities to reflect on the problems and methodologies encountered in the scientific method. Includes Honors sections.
