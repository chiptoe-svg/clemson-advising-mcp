MATH 4830 - Research Experience for Visiting Undergraduates
0 Credits (30 Contact Hours)
Research students carry out several weeks (the number varies by program) of mentored research under the direction of faculty in the mathematical and statistical sciences, and participate in professional development workshops to develop their research careers. Products include a presentation and written report. May be repeated for a maximum of 20 credits. Preq: Acceptance into summer research program in Mathematical Sciences.
