PRTM 3640 - Programming and Trends in Intercollegiate Athletics
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Current trends in intercollegiate athletics in the United States and abroad are identified and examined. Topics include programs, programming, organizational structure, education, policies, funding, governance and communication as they relate to intercollegiate athletics.
