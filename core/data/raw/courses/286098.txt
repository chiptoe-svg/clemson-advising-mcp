PA 4010 - Capstone Project
2 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Capstone course for Performing Arts majors. Students research, prepare, execute and assess a substantial project for the community as approved by a faculty committee. Students, with faculty guidance, manage all aspects of the project. Preq: PA 3010. Preq or concurrent enrollment: PA 1500.
