BIOE 4500 - Special Topics in Bioengineering
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Comprehensive study of a topic of current interest in the field of biomedical engineering under the direct supervision and guidance of a faculty member. May be repeated for a maximum of 12 credits, but only if different topics are covered. Preq: Consent of instructor.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
