MUSC 3450 - Chamber Choir
1 Credit (3 Contact Hours)
Ensemble devoted to music training of ensemble members through reading and rehearsal of appropriate music. Public performances are given periodically in addition to the minimum rehearsal time. An audition is required. May be repeated for a maximum of 10 credits.
