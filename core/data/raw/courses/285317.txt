HLTH 4850 - Introduction to GIS for Public Health
4 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduces students to the theory and application of geographic information systems (GIS) in the context of public health and epidemiology addressing both spatial information and methods and technology used to solve spatial inquiry in health and disease prevention. Coreq: HLTH 4851.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
