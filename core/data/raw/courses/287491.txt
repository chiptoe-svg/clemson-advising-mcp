ENGL 3520 - Disability and Literature
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
A study of literary works and cultural artifacts written about disability as well as written by disabled people, emphasizing how representations of physical and cognitive difference reinforce but also challenge stereotypical or stigmatized images of disability. Preq: ENGL 2020 or ENGL 2120 or ENGL 2130 or ENGL 2140 or ENGL 2150 or  ENGL 2160.
