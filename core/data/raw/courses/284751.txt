ENGR 1060 - Engineering Discipline and Skills II
1 Credit (1 Contact Hour)
Continuation of topics introduced in ENGR 1050. Students create and utilize spreadsheets for analysis, employ modeling techniques, and interpret the validity of experimental results. Various forms of technical communication are emphasized. Credit toward a degree will be given only for ENGR 1020 or the combination of ENGR 1050 and ENGR 1060. Includes Honors sections. Preq: ENGR 1050 with a grade of C or better.
