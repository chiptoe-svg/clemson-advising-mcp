MUSC 2920 - Class Jazz Improvisation
1 Credit (1 Contact Hour)
An exploration of improvisation styles, including blues, jazz, folk, rock, and some world music, with an emphasis on creating melodies over chord changes. Melodic elements studied include scales, modes, arpeggios, rhythm, intervals, range, dynamics, timbre, and repetition. May be repeated for a maximum of eight credits.
