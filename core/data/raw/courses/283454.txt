ANTH 3310 - Archaeology
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Overview of anthropological archaeology, emphasizing the scientific analyses, methodologies and theoretical approaches used by anthropological archaeologists to study the human past. Students learn about the origins and development of complex societies and explore individual case studies. Preq: Sophomore standing.
