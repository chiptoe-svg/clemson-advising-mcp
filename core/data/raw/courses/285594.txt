LS 1470 - Alpine Skiing
1 Credit (3 Contact Hours)
Basic downhill snow skiing instruction including equipment selection, safety, and maintenance; parallel turns; edging; carved and linked turns; wedeling; and safety and etiquette. There is an additional fee for this course. Taught during Christmas recess. (Contact the Department of Parks, Recreation and Tourism Management in October.)
