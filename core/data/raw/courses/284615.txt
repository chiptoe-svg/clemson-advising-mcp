EES 4750 - Capstone Design Project
3 Credits (1 Contact Hour)
Students apply creativity and their engineering knowledge to solve open-ended environmental engineering problems. Problems are formulated and solutions are evaluated by faculty and practicing engineers. Oral and written communication skills are developed through presentations, correspondence and project reports. Preq: CE 3410 with a C or higher; and EES 3030 and EES 3040 and EES 3050; and at least three of the following courses: EES 4300, EES 4370, EES 4800, EES 4840, EES 4850, EES 4860; and an engineering grade point average of 2.0 or better. Coreq: EES 4751.
