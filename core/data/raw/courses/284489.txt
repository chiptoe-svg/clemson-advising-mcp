EDEC 2200 - Family, School, and Community Relationships
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Historical trends, theoretical models, and strategies of effective family/school/community relationships are examined. Special emphasis is placed on multicultural issues and on programs that support collaborative interaction with families that benefit children. Meets Read to Succeed requirements. Preq: EDEC 2000.
