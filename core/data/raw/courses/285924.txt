MSE 4910 - Undergraduate Research
2 Credits (4 Contact Hours)
Investigation of a typical materials science and engineering research problem under the direct supervision of a faculty member. After completing the research, students prepare a formal written and oral report. Includes Honors sections. Preq: MSE 3910.
