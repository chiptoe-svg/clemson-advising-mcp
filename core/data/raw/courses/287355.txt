DANC 3120 - Dance for Musical Theatre I
1 Credit (2 Contact Hours)
Introduces students to the basic elements of dance for the stage. Provides opportunities for the development of dance choreography skills and explores concepts of musical theatre staging. Performing Arts fee is assessed. Preq: DANC 2400 or DANC 3300.
