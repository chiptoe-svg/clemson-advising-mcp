CHE 4180 - Plastic Recycling Technologies
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course addresses the major global problem of plastic waste. Recycling is one way to mitigate the plastic waste problem, and various technologies are discussed. Current technical, legal, business, environmental impacts, and financial challenges for recycling of plastics also are addressed. Preq: CH 1010 and MATH 1080.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements
