MATH 3650 - Numerical Methods for Engineers
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Application of undergraduate mathematics and basic engineering principles with emphasis on numerical methods, computer programming and the use of mathematical software packages in the solution of engineering problems. Preq: ENGR 1090 with a C or better or ENGR 1410 with a C or better; and MATH 2080, each with a C or better.
