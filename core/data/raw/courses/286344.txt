POSC 4780 - Latin American Politics
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Survey of prominent trends in Latin American politics, with a focus on major countries in the region and major issues affecting the region. Relations between Latin America and the United States and other prominent countries are also considered. Preq: Sophomore standing.
