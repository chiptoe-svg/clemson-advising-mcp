EDHC 4400 - Leadership and Innovation in Human Capital Education and Development Studio
1 Credit (1 Contact Hour)
A short, high-impact experiential learning studio focused on leadership and innovation in human capital education and development. Students engage in field-based learning with industry experts and complete a hands-on project addressing a real-world workforce challenge. May be repeated for a maximum of three credits. Preq: Consent of instructor.
