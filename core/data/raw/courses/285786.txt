MGT 3050 - Economics of Transportation
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Topics include history and structure of transportation systems in the United States, the nature of transportation costs and rates, transportation systems as factors in industrial location, transportation policy, and the role of transportation in national security. Preq: Junior standing.
