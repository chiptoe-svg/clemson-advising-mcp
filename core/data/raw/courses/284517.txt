EDEL 4580 - Health Education Methods and Content for the Classroom Teacher
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Study of the content, methodology, and resource materials necessary for teaching comprehensive health education in public schools. Emphasizes the National Health Education Standards and the health behaviors of youth that are allied with the Coordinated School Health Program. Preq: Minimum grade-point average of 2.0.
