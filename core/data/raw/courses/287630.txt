MUSC 3970 - Instrumental Seminar
1 Credit (1 Contact Hour)
Performing arts majors with a concentration in music focus on improving their performance skills through the application of physical technique, historical performance practice, and score analysis. May be repeated for a maximum of eight credits. Preq or concurrent enrollment: MUSC 1530 and MUSC 1540 and MUSC 2430 and MUSC 2540 and MUSC 3530 and MUSC 3540 and MUSC 4530 and MUSC 4540.
