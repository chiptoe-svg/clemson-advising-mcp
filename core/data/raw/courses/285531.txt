LARC 1580 - Digital Representation I
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Introduces students to the use of computer technology in the landscape architectural design process. Covers the basics of computer applications used in the industry for conceptualizing, drafting, modeling, and graphic communications. Preq: Landscape Architecture major. Coreq: LARC 1581.
