ENGL 3370 - Creative Inquiry: English
1-3 Credits (1-3 Contact Hours)
Students pursue scholarly activities individually or in teams under the direction of a faculty member. Creative Inquiry projects may be interdisciplinary. Arrangements with faculty must be established prior to registration. May be repeated for a maximum of nine credits. Preq: Consent of instructor.
