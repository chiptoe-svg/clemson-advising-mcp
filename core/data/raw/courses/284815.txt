ENT 4090 - Urban Entomology Laboratory
1 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Identification of household and structural pests common to the urban environment. Students also gain hands-on experience in termite and general pest control. Includes Honors sections. Preq: BIOL 1030 and BIOL 1040; or BIOL 1100 and BIOL 1110; or ENT 3010. Preq or concurrent enrollment: ENT 4040.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
