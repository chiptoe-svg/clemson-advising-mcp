EDHC 4900 - Directed Research in Human Capital Education and Development
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Faculty mentored disciplinary and multidisciplinary individual and group research projects with the goal of developing the students’ ability to discover, analyze, evaluate, and communicate findings from human capital data and authentic research investigations. Includes Honors sections. Preq: EDHC 3210.
