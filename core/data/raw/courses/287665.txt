ED 4180 - Place-Based Education
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Students contextualize curricula within place by theorizing, designing, delivering, and assessing curricular projects that are interdisciplinary in nature. A special emphasis is placed on developing instructional methodologies incorporating the outdoors and local community. Preq: Sophomore standing.
