PRTM 3920 - Special Event Management
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Students acquire an in-depth knowledge about the field of special event management. Planning techniques, strategies, and requirements for planning, implementing, and evaluating community events are included. Emphasizes ordinances, planning, funding, and marketing. Students must have a 2.0 cumulative grade-point average to enroll in this course.
