ENTR 4020 - Venture Planning
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Students develop a business plan focused on creating an organization capable of effectively exploiting a viable opportunity. Topics include organization strategy and design, start-up capital, operations and sourcing issues, leadership, team building, and management of rapid growth. Preq: ENTR 3010.
