EDSP 4940 - Teaching Reading to Students with Mild Disabilities
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Emphasizes the knowledge and skills necessary for teaching reading to students with mild disabilities. Meets Read to Succeed state requirements. Offered fall semester only. Preq: EDSP 4910; and admission to the professional level. Preq or concurrent enrollment: EDSP 4920 and EDSP 4970.
