CU 1500 - Special Topics in Experiential Learning
0 Credits (1 Contact Hour)
This course explores personal and/or professional development topics using experiential learning pedagogy. These topics include, but are not limited to, leadership, service learning and community engagement, and cultural enrichment. To be taken Pass/No Pass only. May be repeated eight times.
