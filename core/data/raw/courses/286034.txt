NURS 3230 - Gerontology Nursing
2 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Introduction of theories of aging. Focuses on complex health care issues of aging and chronic care, including promotion, maintenance, and restoration of health of the elderly. Scientific concepts address physiological, psychological, and sociological issues of aging and chronic illness. Includes Honors sections. Preq: NURS 3100 and NURS 3120.
