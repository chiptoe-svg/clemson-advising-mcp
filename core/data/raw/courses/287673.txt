CPSC 3020 - Systems and Networks: From OS to the Internet
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Study of computer system design for supporting multiple users and networked applications. Includes introductions to operating system concepts, parallel programming, and computer system security. Preq: CPSC 2020 with a grade of C or higher; or both ECE 2220 and ECE 2720, each with a grade of C or higher.
