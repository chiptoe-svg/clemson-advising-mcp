ASL 3000 - Fingerspelling and Numbers in American Sign Language
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Advanced study of the manual alphabet (fingerspelling) and the numerical system in American Sign Language, with extensive practice in both expressive and receptive skills. Preq or concurrent enrollment: ASL 3010.
