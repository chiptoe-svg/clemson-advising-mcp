ENT 4200 - Systematics and Biodiversity
4 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduces systematic biology and the methods by which biologists recognize species, reconstruct the history of life, and use phylogenetic trees to study ecological and evolutionary processes. Preq: BIOL 1100 and BIOL 1110. Coreq: ENT 4201.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
