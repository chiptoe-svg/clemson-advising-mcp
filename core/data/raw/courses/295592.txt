HON 3910 - Honors in Action
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Honors students are provided an immersive, hands-on experience in addressing real-world issues within local, regional, national, or global communities. Students identify pressing problems, analyzing them through various academic and practical lenses, and develop effective solutions, which foster critical thinking and impactful community engagement. May be repeated for a maximum of 12 credits. Preq: Membership in the Clemson University Honors College.
