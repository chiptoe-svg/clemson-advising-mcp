EDEL 4520 - Elementary Methods in Mathematics Teaching
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Special emphasis is given to the development of understanding, skills, and attitudes in the elementary curriculum with focus on strategies, techniques, and materials for teaching elementary mathematics. Includes Honors sections. Preq: MATH 1150 and MATH 1160 and MATH 2160; and admission to the professional level. Coreq: EDEL 4521.
