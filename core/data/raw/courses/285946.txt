MUSC 1510 - Applied Music
1 Credit (1 Contact Hour)
Individual study in performance medium (piano, voice, strings, woodwinds, brass, percussion, guitar, organ, or carillon). One 30-minute lesson each week, for which a minimum of four hours practice is required. Applied music fee is assessed. May be repeated for credit with departmental approval of differing performance media. Preq: Consent of instructor, based upon a qualifying audition.
