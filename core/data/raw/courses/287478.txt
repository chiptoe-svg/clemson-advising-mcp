HIST 4850 - Studies in Military History
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Selected topics and problems in the history of warfare, with concentration in modern history, from the 18th century onward. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits with departmental consent. Preq: Sophomore standing.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
