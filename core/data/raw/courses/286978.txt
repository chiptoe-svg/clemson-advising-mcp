ENGL 2160 - African American Literature
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This introductory course traces the development of a distinct African American literary tradition from the colonial period to the present through an examination of varied themes and movements and texts by authors of African descent. Includes Honors sections. Preq: ENGL 1030.
