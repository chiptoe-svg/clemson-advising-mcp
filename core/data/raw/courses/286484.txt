PSYC 3640 - Industrial Psychology
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Application of theories and methods of applied psychology to support organizational effectiveness and workforce development. Topics include job and task analysis, employee recruiting strategies, design and validation of pre-employment selection testing, performance evaluation methods, and training program development and evaluation. Preq: PSYC 2010.
