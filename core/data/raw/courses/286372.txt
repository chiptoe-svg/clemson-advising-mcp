PRTM 2220 - Program and Event Planning in Parks, Recreation and Tourism
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Introduces concepts, principles, and skills necessary to plan, implement, and evaluate leisure and recreation programs and events. Topics include assessing needs, developing goals and objectives, selecting programs, events, and resources, marketing, venues, implementation, evaluation, group dynamics and leadership techniques. Preq or concurrent enrollment: PRTM 2000. Coreq: PRTM 2221.
