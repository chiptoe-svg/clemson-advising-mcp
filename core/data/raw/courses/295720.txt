BIOE 4540 - Global Biomedical Ethics
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course explores global biomedical ethics. Course content includes diverse philosophical and cultural perspectives regarding health, disease, equitable access to healthcare, ethical issues in global clinical trials, public health ethics, and global genetic technologies. Students gain critical analysis of social justice and international collaboration addressing global health issues. Preq: Junior standing.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
