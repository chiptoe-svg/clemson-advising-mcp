MICR 4930 - Senior Seminar
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Capstone course engaging students in analysis and discussion of publications from the technical and non-technical literature in biological sciences and from current topics of biology appearing in other media. Emphasis is placed on ethical issues that arise as a result of biological research. Credit toward a degree will be given for only one of BIOL 4930 or MICR 4930. This course is not open to students who have received credit for BIOL 4930. Preq: Senior standing; and COMM 1500 or COMM 2500 or HON 2230; and MICR 3070.
