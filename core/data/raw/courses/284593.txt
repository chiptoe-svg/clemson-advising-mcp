EDSP 4910 - Educational Assessment of Individuals with Disabilities
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Introduction to assessment process (verification) in special education. Includes procedural safeguards; data collections via informal and standardized procedures; issues in assessment; psychometric properties of standardized tests; and administration, scoring, and interpretation of selected instruments. Meets Read to Succeed state requirements. Offered spring semester only. Preq: Admission to the professional level. Coreq: EDSP 4911.
