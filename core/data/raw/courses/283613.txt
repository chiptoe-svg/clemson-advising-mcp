ASL 4010 - Linguistics of American Sign Language
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Designed for advanced-level students in American Sign Language. Primary goal is to further develop students’ understanding and knowledge of American Sign Language by incorporating in-depth analysis of American Sign Language’s historical development, linguistic structures, syntax, grammar, and pragmatics. Preq or concurrent enrollment: ASL 3010.
