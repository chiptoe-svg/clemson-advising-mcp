IE 4820 - Systems Modeling
4 Credits (4 Contact Hours)
The purpose, theory, and techniques of modeling systems with dynamic events. Students learn a powerful analytical process to use in the analysis and improvement of systems in several industries, including transportation, logistics, manufacturing and service systems. Incorporates professional simulation software as a tool in evaluating the system performance. Preq: IE 3610 and IE 3810; or MATH 3020 and MATH 4400 and MATH 4410.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
