PHIL 1000 - Introduction to the Philosophy Major
1 Credit (1 Contact Hour)
This class is for students beginning in the philosophy major. Students learn about the discipline of philosophy, its various subfields, and what career paths are open to someone majoring in philosophy. They meet the philosophy department’s faculty members and learn about their respective research areas, as well as begin to practice philosophical argumentation alongside their fellow students in the major. To be taken Pass/No Pass only.
