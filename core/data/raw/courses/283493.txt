ARCH 2700 - Structures I
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
The study of statically determinate structural elements and systems including load tracing through physical modeling and theoretical and analytical analysis, the interrelationship between stress and strain, stability and the implication of tension, compression, shear torsion and bending. Preq: Architecture major and PHYS 1220 and PHYS 1240; or PHYS 2070 and PHYS 2090.
