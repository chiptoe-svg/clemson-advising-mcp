DANC 3140 - Dance for Musical Theatre II
1 Credit (2 Contact Hours)
Expands and develops knowledge of dance elements for the stage. Provides opportunities for the development of dance choreography skills and explores concepts of musical theatre staging. Performing Arts fee is assessed. Preq: DANC 3120.
