AUD 3820 - Hip Hop Production Techniques
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course provides a comprehensive exploration of the history of Hip Hop and equips students with the skills necessary for proficient music writing, production, beat-making, mixing, and mastering within the realm of Hip Hop audio techniques and its subgenres. Preq: AUD 1850.
