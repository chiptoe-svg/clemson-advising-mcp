IS 1110 - English as an Additional Language
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Special course for students using English as an additional language (EAL). Intensive study and drill in American English pronunciation and listening comprehension. Required of all EAL students who do not make a satisfactory grade on the Verification of English Skills Test. Carries no credit for graduation. To be taken Pass/No Pass only. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits. Coreq: IS 1111.
