POSC 4800 - Gender and Politics
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Examination of the role of gender in politics in the United States and in other countries. Particular emphasis on the role of women in electoral politics, issues of gender, women’s rights as human rights, and feminist theory. Preq: Sophomore standing.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
