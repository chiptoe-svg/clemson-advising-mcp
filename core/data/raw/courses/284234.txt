CPSC 2910 - Seminar in Professional Issues I
1 Credit (1 Contact Hour)
Considers the impact of computer use on society. Discusses ethical use of software and protection of intellectual property rights. Profession is viewed historically; organizations important to the profession are discussed; the development process for standards is presented; and students are introduced to the professional literature. Preq: CPSC 1020 or CPSC 1070, with a grade of C or higher.
