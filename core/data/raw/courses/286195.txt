PHSC 1170 - Introduction to Chemistry and Earth Science
4 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Integrates topics in chemistry, earth science and environmental science. It emphasizes the interconnections among the various science disciplines and the practical application to experiments and activities. Coreq: PHSC 1171.
