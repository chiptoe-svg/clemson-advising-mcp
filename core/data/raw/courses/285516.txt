LARC 2550 - Community Design Studio
6 Credits (1 Contact Hours)
Studio focused on the study and design of communities and public spaces. Students explore multicultural, historical and ecological layers of community, as well as the role of landscape management and the creative design process to add new dimensions of meaning to these places. Preq: Sophomore standing. Coreq: LARC 2551.
