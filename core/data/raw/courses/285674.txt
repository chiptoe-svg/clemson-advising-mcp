MATH 1110 - Calculus II for Biologists
4 Credits (4 Contact Hours)
Selected topics from integral calculus, eigenvalues and eigenvectors of matrices and differential equations are used to encourage the use of mathematics, computational tool and biological science in the study of relevant biological models. Credit toward a degree will be given for only one of MATH 1080 or MATH 1110. Preq: MATH 1060 or MATH 1070.
