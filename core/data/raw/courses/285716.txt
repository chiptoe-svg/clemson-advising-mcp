MATH 4070 - Regression and Time-Series Analysis
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Theory and application of the regression and time series. Approaches to empirical model building and data analysis are treated. Computation and interpretation of results are facilitated through the use of interactive statistical packages. Written and oral reports are required for students using this course as part of their capstone. Preq: MATH 3110 and MATH 4000; and one of MATH 3020 or STAT 2300 or STAT 3090.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
