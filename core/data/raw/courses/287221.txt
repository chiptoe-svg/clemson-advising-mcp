ANTH 4310 - Historical Archaeology
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course covers the archaeology of the recent past, with an eye toward the role that the past has in the modern era. It ties together the present day with the conditions in the past through the archaeological record, addressing uneven access to things, places, and activities in the past that impact our lives today. Preq: Junior standing.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
