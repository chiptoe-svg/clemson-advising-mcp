ENGR 2100 - Computer-Aided Design and Engineering Applications
2 Credits (1 Contact Hour)
Introduction to graphics applications for engineering and related professions. Two dimensional drawings and CAD tools are used to visualize, communicate, and analyze engineering problems. Engineering applications include contour plots, hydrology, transportation, architectural, and site plan drawings. AutoCAD software is used. Coreq: ENGR 2101.
