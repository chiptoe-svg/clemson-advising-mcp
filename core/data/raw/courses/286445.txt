PRTM 4260 - Trends and Issues in Recreational Therapy
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Capstone course provides insight into the contemporary issues in the recreational therapy profession. The course requires students to synthesize previous course content and experiential learning and appraise personal and professional philosophies to elucidate their role as an entry-level practitioner. Preq: PRTM 3260 and PRTM 3270, each with a C or better.
