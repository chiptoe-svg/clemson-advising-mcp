REL 3990 - Research Colloquium in Religion
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Colloquium offered each spring for junior and senior Religious Studies majors. Students enroll individually with a faculty member of their choice and develop, in consultation with him or her, a research project suited to the student’s interests. All students meet four times as a group to present the discuss their research. Preq: Junior standing and Religious Studies major.
