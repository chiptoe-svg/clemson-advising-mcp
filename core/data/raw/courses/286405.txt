PRTM 3280 - Preceptorship in Recreational Therapy
3 Credits (5 Contact Hours)
Course facilitates experiential learning opportunities for recreational therapy students consisting of preceptor-supervised clinical experiences in health care and community-based agencies. Preq: PRTM-Recreational Therapy major and PRTM 3220 and PRTM 3230 and PRTM 3240, each with a grade of C or higher.
