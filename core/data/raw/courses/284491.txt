EDEC 2010 - Practicum in Early Childhood Settings I
1 Credit (3 Contact Hours)
Experiences in early childhood settings that provide opportunities for working with young children in a variety of settings. Practicum I focuses on five-year-old kindergarten and first grade classrooms in public school settings. This experience emphasizes the transition to formal school settings for young children. Practicum I builds on foundations of early childhood by making connections between theory and practice. Preq: ED 1050 and Sophomore standing.
