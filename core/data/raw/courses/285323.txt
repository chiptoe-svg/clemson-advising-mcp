HLTH 4990 - Independent Study
1-3 Credits (1-3 Contact Hours)
Study of selected problems in health under the direction of faculty member chosen by the student. Student and faculty member develop a course of study designed for the individual student and approved by the department chair prior to registration. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits. Preq: Junior standing and consent of instructor.
