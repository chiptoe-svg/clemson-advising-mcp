HLTH 4020 - Principles of Health Fitness
4 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Students apply current theories concerning physiological effects of exercise to select populations; understand the relationship between exercise and various chronic diseases; and design, execute, and evaluate exercise programs in terms of safety and effectiveness. Students must be certified in CPR to enroll in this course. Preq: HLTH 3980. Preq or concurrent enrollment: BIOL 2230. Coreq: HLTH 4021.
