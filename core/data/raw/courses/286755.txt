WCIN 4990 - World Cinema Practicum
3-6 Credits (6-12 Contact Hours)
Students apply their practical and theoretical knowledge to a substantive project involving their internship experience and/or creative option. Course provides elements of pre-professional training. To be taken Pass/No Pass only. May be repeated for a maximum of 12 credits. Preq: Junior standing in the World Cinema major.
