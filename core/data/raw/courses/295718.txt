ART 2330 - Beginning Industrial Design
3 Credits (6 Contact Hours)
This course introduces the process of industrial design by exploring the design, development, and improvement of three-dimensional products that holistically combine functionality, usability, manufacturability, user experience, sustainability, and aesthetics. Preq: ART 1510.
