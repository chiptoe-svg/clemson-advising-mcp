MUSC 3420 - Women’s Breakout Ensemble
1 Credit (2 Contact Hours)
Small ensembles: study of women’s a cappella/popular vocal music on an advanced level. Audition required. This course may be repeated for credit with a maximum of 16 hours ensemble credit allowable toward a degree. Preq or concurrent enrollment: MUSC 3700 or MUSC 3710.
