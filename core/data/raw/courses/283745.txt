BE 3200 - Principles and Practices of Geomatics
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Basic surveying measurements and computations for engineering project control, mapping, and construction layout. Leveling, earthwork, area, and topographic measurements using levels, total stations and GPS. Application of Mapping via GIS. Preq: CE 2010 with a grade of C or higher and ENGR 2100. Coreq: BE 3201.
