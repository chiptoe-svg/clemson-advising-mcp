ENT 4950 - Insect Biotechnology
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Considers many unique genetic features exhibited by insects and describes the applications of biotechnology to enhance useful products from insects and to affect the control of destructive insects. May also be offered as GEN 4950. Preq: ENT 3010 and GEN 3020.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
