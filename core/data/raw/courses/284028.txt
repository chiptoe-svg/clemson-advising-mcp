CE 4350 - Infrastructure Project Planning
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Covers concepts related to planning, executing, monitoring and controlling, and closing out infrastructure projects from the agency owner perspective. Advanced concepts of engineering economic analysis, risk analysis and data management software are addressed. Preq: CE 2310 and CE 3310, each with a grade of C or better.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
