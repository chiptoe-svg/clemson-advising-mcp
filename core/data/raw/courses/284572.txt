EDSC 4540 - Secondary English Capstone Seminar
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Seminar in conjunction with EDSC 4440. Interns reflect upon and solve problems regarding teaching events, share effective teaching practices, and devise ways to document dimensions of effective teaching. Taught spring semester only. Preq: EDSC 4240 and admission to the professional level.  Coreq: EDSC 4440 and EDSC 4541.
