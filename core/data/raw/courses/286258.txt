PKSC 3200 - Consumer Packaged Goods Development
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Students gain practical experience in developing a consumer packaged good (CPG) from its initial design to a functional prototype. They explore the technical aspects of CPG creation, define production processes, and analyze costs while iteratively developing a physical product and branded packaging system. This course equips students with the skills to produce CPGs that meet industry standards while navigating market constraints and user experience.  Preq: Both CH 1010 and MATH 1060; or GC 2070; or PKSC 2200. Coreq: PKSC 3201.
