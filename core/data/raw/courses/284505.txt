EDEC 4850 - Early Childhood Capstone
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Taken concurrently with student teaching. Students strengthen connections between theory and pedagogy; analyze and solve contemporary problems in early childhood education; and reflect upon their personal growth as educators. Preq: EDEC 4400 and admission to the professional level.  Coreq: EDEC 4840.
