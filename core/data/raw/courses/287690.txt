PRTM 2030 - Introduction to Campus Recreation
1 Credit (1 Contact Hour)
Exploratory course provides an overview of campus recreation as a profession, introducing students to the primary facilities, services, and programs offered through campus recreation. Students learn about the benefits of campus recreation participation, constraints that students may face in participation, and ways to negotiate those constraints.
