ART 2250 - Beginning Digital Media
3 Credits (6 Contact Hours)
Introductory level course that explores the theoretical and technical foundations of time-based mediums, including video, sound, and installation art. Studio projects cover video production with digital cameras and industry standard editing software, greenscreen production, keyframing and compositing, stop-motion animation, audio production, and video projection design. Preq: ART 1510 and ART 1550.
