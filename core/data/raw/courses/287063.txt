MATH 3130 - Algebra for Middle Level Teachers
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Study of elementary algebra solution of equations and inequalities; properties and applications of parent functions and models (linear, quadratic, polynomial, exponential, absolute value, logarithmic, square root); graphical analysis and curve fitting of data; systems of equations; and history of algebra. Students develop symbol sense and algebraic reasoning. Computational tools are used. Preq: Middle Level Education major.
