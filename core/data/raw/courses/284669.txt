ENGL 3860 - Adolescent Literature
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Reading and analysis of literature written for readers age 12-18. Emphasis is on historical context, chief themes and motifs, and censorship issues, as well as connections with classic literature. Preq: ENGL 2020 or ENGL 2120 or ENGL 2130 or ENGL 2140 or ENGL 2150.
