PKSC 4220 - Selected Topics in Packaging Science
1-3 Credits (1-3 Contact Hours)
Comprehensive study of selected topics in packaging science not covered in detail or contained in other courses. Contemporary developments in each area are stressed. May be repeated for a maximum of 15 credits, but only if different topics are covered. Preq: Consent of instructor.
