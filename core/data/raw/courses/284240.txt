CPSC 3520 - Programming Systems
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
An advanced course in programming languages and systems for computer engineering and computer science majors. The course objective is to enable a more complete understanding of programming topics and related supporting tools, including philosophy, methodology, formal syntax and semantics, and examples of programming paradigms, languages and development approaches. Preq: ECE 2230 with a grade of C or better; or CPSC 2120 and CPSC 2150, each with a grade of C or better. Preq or concurrent enrollment: CPSC 2070 or MATH 4190 with a grade of C or better.
