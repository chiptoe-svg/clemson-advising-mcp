PRTM 3970 - Program Plan Design in Recreational Therapy
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course explores the principles and practices of effective program planning and design within the field of recreational therapy, providing students with a solid foundation in the essential skills needed to create evidence-informed and theory-informed recreational therapy programs for diverse populations. Preq: PRTM 3220 and PRTM 3230 and PRTM 3260 and PRTM 3270, each with a grade of C or higher. Coreq: PRTM 3290 and PRTM 3310 and PRTM 4220.
