CSM 3990 - Creative Inquiry in Building Affordable Housing I
3 Credits (6 Contact Hours)
Students learn about affordable housing construction through classroom learning, research, and hands-on experience. They volunteer at Habitat for Humanity sites, gaining skills in site selection and planning. The course emphasizes project management, teamwork, and problem-solving to prepare students to positively impact communities in need. Preq: Sophomore standing.
