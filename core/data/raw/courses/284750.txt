ENGR 1050 - Engineering Discipline and Skills I
1 Credit (1 Contact Hour)
Provides foundational engineering problem solving skills. Students demonstrate problem solving techniques with an emphasis on dimensions and units. Includes exploration of various engineering disciplines. Introduces professional issues appropriate to engineering. Credit toward a degree will be given only for ENGR 1020 or the combination of ENGR 1050 and ENGR 1060. Includes Honors sections. Preq or concurrent enrollment: MATH 1040 or MATH 1060 or MATH 1080 or MATH 2060 or MATH 2080 with a C or better; or a score of 65 or higher on the Clemson Mathematics Placement Test (CMPT); or a score of 640 or higher on SAT Math section; or a score of 27 or higher on ACT Math section.
