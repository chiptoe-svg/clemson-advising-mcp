PSYC 3690 - Leadership in Organizational Settings
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Broad survey of theory and research on leadership in formal organizations. A detailed explanation and critical evaluation of major theories (including participative and charismatic leadership) are bridged with helpful remedies and prescriptions for effective leadership in organizations. Preq: PSYC 2010.
