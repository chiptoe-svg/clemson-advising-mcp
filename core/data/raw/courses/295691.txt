AUE 4300 - Automotive Conceptual Design
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course considers the early stage of engineering design with emphasis on automotive applications, and introduces relevant methods and tools that guide engineers through a systematic design process. The course is project-based and students work during the semester on applying the introduced principles, methods, and tools to formulate and solve an automotive engineering design problem. Design reviews are conducted and proof of concepts are developed, presented, and demonstrated. Project deliverables include a design notebook and a project report. Preq: Senior standing and AUE 2020 with a grade of C or higher.
