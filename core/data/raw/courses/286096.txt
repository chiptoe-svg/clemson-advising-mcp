PA 3980 - Special Topics in Performing Arts
1-3 Credits (1-3 Contact Hours)
Select areas of study in performing arts not addressed by other performing arts course offerings. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits, but only if different topics are covered. Preq: PA 1010 and consent of instructor.
