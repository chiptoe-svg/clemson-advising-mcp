GBS 4710 - Directed Studies on the Black Experience in Education
1-3 Credits (1-3 Contact Hours)
Students conduct research and produce scholarship on academic, social, and historical issues that impact the Black experience in educational settings. Students may also participate in service learning activities to broaden their understanding and apply their knowledge in the community. May be repeated for a maximum of nine credits. Preq or concurrent enrollment: GBS 1000 or GBS 1010.
