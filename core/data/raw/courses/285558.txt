LAW 3220 - Legal Environment of Business
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Examination of both state and national regulation of business. Attention is given to the constitution and limitations of power, specific areas in which governments have acted, and the regulations that have been imposed in these areas. Includes Honors sections. Preq: Junior standing.
