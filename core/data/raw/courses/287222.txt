EDHC 3200 - Generational Learning and Motivation in the Workplace
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to learning, development, and motivation across the lifespan. Emphasis is on the physical, social, emotional, and cognitive characteristics of each stage of the lifespan and the implications of those developmental characteristics as related to learning and motivation. Preq: Sophomore standing.
