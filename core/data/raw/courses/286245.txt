PHYS 4650 - Thermodynamics and Statistical Mechanics
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Study of temperature development of the laws of thermodynamics and their application to thermodynamic systems. Introduction to low temperature physics is given. Includes Honors sections. Preq: PHYS 3210.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
