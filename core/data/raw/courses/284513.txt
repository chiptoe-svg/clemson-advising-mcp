EDEL 4510 - Elementary Methods in Science Teaching
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Development of process skills, technical skills, and attitudes needed to foster increased confidence and commitment to the teaching of elementary science, with emphasis on teaching strategies and techniques and their implications for what we know of how children learn science. Includes Honors sections. Preq: BIOL 1090 and PHSC 1170 and PHSC 1180, or 12 hours of science content coursework (any combination of ASTR 1010/1030, ASTR 1020/1040, BIOL 1030/1050, BIOL 1040/1060, BIOL 1100, CH 1010, CH 1020, CH 1050, and CH 1060); and admission to the professional level. Coreq: EDEL 4511.
