MGT 4560 - Business Data Management
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course provides a comprehensive understanding of how to manage structured and unstructured data in facilitating managerial decision making. Students apply various data management tools to analyze real-world business data. Preq: MGT 3500; or ACCT 3220 and MGT 2010; or MGT 2010 and CPSC 2150 and CPSC 2310.
