THEA 4970 - Scene Painting
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Practical study of basic painting techniques for the theatre including layout, proper use of materials, painting styles, and texturing techniques. Coreq: THEA 4971.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
