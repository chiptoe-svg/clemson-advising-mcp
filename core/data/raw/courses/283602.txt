ASL 2010 - American Sign Language II
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Continuation of ASL 1020. Covers additional vocabulary, sentences, and grammar structures. Main focus is on conversational and receptive skills as well as a better understanding of Deaf culture. Preq: ASL 1020. Coreq: ASL 2011.
