NURS 3300 - Research in Nursing
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to conceptual frameworks, models, and theories related to nursing. Analysis of reported research in nursing and related disciplines. Ethical, moral, and legal issues are discussed in relation to nursing research. Includes Honors sections. Preq: Junior standing.
