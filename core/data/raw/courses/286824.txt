MKT 3240 - Introduction to Professional Selling
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduces contemporary theories about selling in the context of long-term relationships as a foundational framework for effectively influencing others. Role playing, video-taped presentations, and other techniques are employed to enhance interpersonal communication skills. Preq: Membership in the Sales Innovation Program.
