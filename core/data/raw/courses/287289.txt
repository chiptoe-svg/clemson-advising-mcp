EDHC 4310 - Leading Learning, Career, and Talent Development
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduces students to foundational concepts in learning and development (training), career development, and building a learning culture as a talent development strategy. This course focuses on preparing students to build the knowledge, skills, and abilities of others to help employees develop and achieve their potential to improve organizational success and performance. Preq: Sophomore standing.
