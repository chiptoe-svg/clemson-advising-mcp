HP 3040 - Conservation for Historic Preservation
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course focuses on the properties and performance of traditional and historic building materials. Through lectures, coordinating site visits and hands-on opportunities in the laboratory and field, a basic knowledge is provided to participants that allows better understanding of the mechanics of deterioration and the choices for treatments. Preq: HP 2010.
