MUSC 3390 - Mixed Brass Ensemble
1 Credit (2 Contact Hours)
Study of technique and repertoire for mixed brass chamber music ensembles with a focus on Renaissance, Baroque and modern music. Students participate in a weekly rehearsal, as well as occasional performances.
