CHE 1000 - Design of Coffee
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course explores the art and science of coffee through hands-on experiments and design challenges. Students learn how foundational principles such as heat transfer, fluid flow, and mass balance affect the roasting and brewing of coffee. Emphasis is placed on creative problem solving, data-driven thinking, and sensory analysis. No prior technical background is required, and all concepts are taught in an accessible, engaging way. This course is open to students of all majors and levels who are curious about how process design, experimentation, and flavor come together in the world’s favorite beverage.
