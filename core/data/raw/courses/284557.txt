EDSC 4170 - Teaching Internship in the Secondary School
6 Credits (16 Contact Hours)
Full-time, supervised teaching internship for one semester in cooperation with a participating South Carolina secondary school. Reserved for students seeking certification in critical-need teaching areas. To be taken Pass/No Pass only. May be repeated for a maximum of 12 credits. Preq: ED 3010 and EDF 3020 and EDF 3350 and EDLT 4980: and one of the following: EDSC 4240, EDSC 4250, EDSC 4260, EDSC 4270; and consent of School of Education by way of approving student’s application.
