PRTM 3660 - Campus Recreation Practicum
3 Credits (6 Contact Hours)
Students gain practical experience in an area or areas of a campus recreation program to aid in discovery and application of PRTM core and recreational sport management course content. To be taken Pass/No Pass only. Preq: PRTM 3600 and PRTM 3610 and PRTM 3630.
