EES 2010 - Environmental Engineering Fundamentals I
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Overview of topics and engineering application areas that comprise the environmental engineering profession. Significant emphasis is given to development of oral and written communication skills needed by the engineering professional and application of engineering fundamentals to environmental systems. Preq: CH 1010; and ENGR 1020 or ENGR 1060 or ENGR 1520; and MATH 1060 or MATH 1070; each with a grade of C or better. Preq or concurrent enrollment: CHE 1300 or ENGR 1410 or ENGR 1640.
