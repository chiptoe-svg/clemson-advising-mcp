HIST 3260 - History of American Transportation
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Examines the principal forms of transportation in the United States from colonial times to the present, including water, road, canal, railroad, internal combustion, and air. Emphasizes technological developments and economic, geographic, and social impact of specific transport forms.
