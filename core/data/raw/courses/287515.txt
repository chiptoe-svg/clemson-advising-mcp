JUST 4950 - Critical Issues in Policing
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course provides an analysis of contemporary issues and problems in policing. Topics include corruption, police use of deadly force, fairness and equity in policing, and police reform. Preq: JUST 4910.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
