MATH 1160 - Contemporary Mathematics for Elementary School Teachers II
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Continuation of MATH 1150. Manipulatives and concrete models are used for properties, operations, and problem solving for integers, elementary fractions, rational numbers, and real numbers. Selected topics in statistics and probability are introduced with a hands-on approach to learning. Restricted to Elementary, Early Childhood, and Special Education majors. Preq: MATH 1150 with a grade of C or higher.
