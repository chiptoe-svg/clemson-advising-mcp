HON 4020 - Honors Directed Studies
1-3 Credits (1-3 Contact Hours)
Supervised reading, research, and/or projects on selected multi-disciplinary topics at the advanced Honors level. Student(s) and faculty member develop a course of study designed for the student(s) and approved by the Honors College prior to registration. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits. Preq: Membership in the Clemson University Honors College and consent of instructor.
