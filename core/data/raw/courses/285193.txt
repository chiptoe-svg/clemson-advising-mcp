HIST 3000 - History of Colonial America
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Development of American institutions and customs in the period before 1776. Considerable emphasis is placed on the imperial relations between Great Britain and her colonies and upon the movement towards and the philosophy of the American Revolution.
