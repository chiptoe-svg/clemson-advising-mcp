HCC 3030 - Artificial Intelligence Integrations: Human-Centered AI in Disciplinary Contexts
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This interdisciplinary course explores the integration of artificial intelligence (AI) with diverse disciplinary fields through the lens of human-centered design ethics. Students learn core AI concepts while critically examining the global challenges of how AI impacts, reshapes, and can be shaped by different academic disciplines. Students explore core AI methods, ethical considerations, and real-world applications in the context of their own majors. The course emphasizes critical thinking, cross-disciplinary collaboration, and assignments to help students assess how AI is transforming their fields and how to engage with it responsibly and creatively. Preq: Junior standing.
