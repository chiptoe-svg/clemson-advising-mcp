IE 3610 - Industrial Applications of Probability and Statistics II
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduces central concept that apparent conflict between productivity and quality can be resolved through improvements in processes by introducing statistical thinking. Specific industrial applications, such as (static) simulation, quality control and reliability models, are emphasized. Preq: IE 3600.
