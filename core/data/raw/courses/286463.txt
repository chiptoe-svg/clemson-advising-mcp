PRTM 4410 - Commercial Recreation and Enterprise Development in Sport
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Components of offering sport, recreation, and leisure services and products to the public by individuals, partnerships, and corporations for the purpose of making a profit. Concepts related to strategic management and enterprise development in the sport and recreation context are discussed and applied. Preq or concurrent enrollment: PRTM 2540.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
