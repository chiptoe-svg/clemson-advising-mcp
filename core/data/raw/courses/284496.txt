EDEC 4000 - Observation and Assessment in Clinical Settings
3 Credits (9 Contact Hours)
Students engage in clinical experiences in early childhood settings by observing, guiding, and assessing young children, from birth to age eight, in various high-quality preschool and primary settings. To be taken Pass/No Pass only. Preq: EDEC 4500 with a grade of C or better and admission to the professional level.
