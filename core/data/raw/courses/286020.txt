NURS 3030 - Medical-Surgical II: Nursing of Adults
7 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Incorporates theoretical and empirical knowledge from physical and social sciences. Uses critical thinking to provide holistic, safe, individualized nursing care to adults, including health promotion, maintenance, restoration, and health teaching. Preq: NURS 3040 and NURS 3100 and NURS 3120 and NURS 3400, each with a C or better. Coreq: NURS 3031 and NURS 3050.
