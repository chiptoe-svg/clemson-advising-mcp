LS 2290 - Advanced Swing Dance
1 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Focuses on competition level and style of swing dance. Students learn to break down and teach a routine to beginners. Students also learn the skills necessary to create and choreograph a short routine. Preq: LS 2280.
