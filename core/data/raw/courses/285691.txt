MATH 2990 - Creative Inquiry in Mathematical Sciences
1-3 Credits (1-3 Contact Hours)
In consultation with and under the direction of a faculty member, students pursue scholarly activities individually or in teams. These creative inquiry projects may be interdisciplinary. Arrangements with mentors must be established prior to registration. May be repeated for a maximum of three credits. Preq: Consent of faculty member/mentor.
