AGM 4040 - Grain Handling and Storage
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
The course provides an introduction to post-harvest grain operations and explores the basic principles of grain handling and storage. Topics addressed include: grain drying principles, methods, and costs; psychrometric properties of air; grain handling equipment and conveyance; grain preservation quality standards and measurement; aeration and fan airflow; equilibrium moisture content relationships; and inventory estimation. Preq: AGM 2200. Coreq: AGM 4041.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
