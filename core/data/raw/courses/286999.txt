MGT 4050 - Leadership Development
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course covers leadership across many contexts. Students gain an understanding of what leadership is and what it means to be a good leader. Personal development is emphasized as students actively engage in exploring their own leadership abilities. Preq: Junior or senior standing.
