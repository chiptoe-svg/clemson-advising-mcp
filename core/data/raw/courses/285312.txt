HLTH 4600 - Health Information Systems
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Focuses on the application of information systems to patient care and management support systems. Provides a general understanding of how the information needs of health professionals and health service organizations can be met through the proper acquisition, storage, analysis, retrieval, and presentation of data. Preq: HLTH 2030.
