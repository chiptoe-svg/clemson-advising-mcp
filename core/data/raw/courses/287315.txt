JUST 4150 - Homeland Security and Intelligence
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Exposes students to the concepts and operational systems of homeland security and intelligence. This course addresses commonly used intelligence analysis techniques, as well as the broad range of homeland security threats currently facing the U.S., with a focus on terrorism. The relationship of homeland security and intelligence to civil rights, civil liberties, and privacy is also explored. Preq: JUST 2880 or SOC 2880.
