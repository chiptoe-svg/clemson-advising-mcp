GBS 4000 - Studies in Global Black Studies
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Study of selected topics or themes in Global Black Studies. Allows for individualized study of specific topics related to Global Black Studies such as music, dance, religion, colonization, slavery, or economic development. May be repeated for a maximum of nine credits, but only if different topics are covered. Preq: Sophomore standing.
