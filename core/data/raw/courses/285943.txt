MUSC 1430 - Aural Skills I
1 Credit (2 Contact Hours)
Beginning aural skills, which include Solfege, singing and identifying intervals and scales, identifying triads and seventh chords, sight singing simple melodies in major and minor keys, and taking dictation of simple melodies in major and minor keys. Coreq: MUSC 1420.
