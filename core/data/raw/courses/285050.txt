GEN 4930 - Senior Seminar
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Analysis and discussion of papers from the primary literature in the life sciences, specifically in genetics. Students find pertinent articles in the primary literature and present and analyze the selected reading. Students evaluate varying perspectives of stakeholders and discuss ethical concerns that arise from the peer-reviewed articles. Includes Honors sections. Preq: BCHM 3010 and GEN 3020, each with a grade of C or better; and one of the following with a grade of C or better: GEN 4100 or GEN 4200 or GEN 4500.
