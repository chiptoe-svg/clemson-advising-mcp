INNO 4950 - Undergraduate Research and Creative Inquiry Experience
0 Credits (0 Contact Hours)
Undergraduate students conduct mentored research under the direction of faculty and participate in professional development. Students submit a reflection that thoughtfully considers what they have learned and prepare a presentation of their research results. To be taken Pass/No Pass only. May be repeated three times for credit. Preq: Consent of instructor and acceptance into the Summer Research Program.
