CSM 2010 - Structures
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Study of statically determinate structural components and systems, including force applications and distributions in structural elements and the resulting stress-strain patterns in axial, shear, and bearing mechanisms. Preq: MATH 1020 or MATH 1060; and one of the following Physics combinations: PHYS 1220 and PHYS 1240; or PHYS 2070 and PHYS 2090; and Construction Science and Management major.
