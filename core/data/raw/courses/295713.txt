POSC 3780 - Human Security
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Examination and analysis of the concept of human security in a global context. Explores the shift away from traditional, state-centered approaches to a focus on the well being of individuals across state and non-state institutions. Focuses on both the causes of and solutions to issues like conflict, poverty, environmental degradation, human rights violations, and institution building.
