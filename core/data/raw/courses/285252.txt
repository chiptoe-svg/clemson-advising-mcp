HIST 3940 - Studies in Non-Western History
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Examines the important trends in world history since 1500, including capitalism, industrialization, nationalism, migration, and imperialism, with a focus on non-Western regions. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits. Preq: Sophomore standing.
