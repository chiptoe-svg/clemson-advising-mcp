EDEL 4820 - Capstone Seminar in Elementary Teaching
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Students strengthen connections between theory and pedagogy; analyze and solve contemporary problems in elementary education; and reflect upon their personal growth as educators within a social justice framework. Designed to meet Read to Succeed state teacher certification requirements. Preq: EDEL 3100 and EDEL 3210 and EDEL 4010 and EDEL 4510 and EDEL 4520 and EDEL 4870 and EDEL 4880 and EDLT 4610; and admission to the professional level. Coreq: EDEL 4821 and EDEL 4830.
