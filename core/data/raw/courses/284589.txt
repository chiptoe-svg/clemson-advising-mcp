EDSP 3740 - Characteristics and Strategies for Individuals with Emotional/Behavioral Disorders
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
In-depth coverage of characteristics and identification procedures for individuals with emotional or behavioral disorders. Effective instructional strategies and behavior management are addressed. Students participate in field experiences throughout the semester. Preq: EDSP 3700; and admission to professional level. Coreq: EDSP 3720 and EDSP 3741.
