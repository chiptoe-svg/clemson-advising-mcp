MATH 1070 - Differential and Integral Calculus
4 Credits (4 Contact Hours)
Continuation of MATH 1040. Successful completion of MATH 1040 and MATH 1070 is equivalent to the completion of MATH 1060. Continuation of differential calculus and an introduction to integral calculus. Not open to students who have received credit for MATH 1060. Preq: MATH 1040.
