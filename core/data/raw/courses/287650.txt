EDLT 4730 - Teaching Reading and Writing to PK-12 Multilingual Learners
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course provides preservice teachers with strategies and tools to effectively teach reading and writing to multilingual learners in PK-12 settings. Emphasizing culturally sustaining practices, it covers developmental literacy processes and instructional techniques to support students with diverse linguistic backgrounds. Preq: EDLT 4700.
