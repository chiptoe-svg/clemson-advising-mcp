MGT 4090 - Advanced Topics in Global Management
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
In-depth examination of advanced topics in global management based on the developments in the global manager profession and faculty interests. Emphasizes the strategic formulation and application of these topics to support organizational leadership. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits. Preq: MGT 3030.
