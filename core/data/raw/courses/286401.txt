PRTM 3240 - Assessment and Planning in Recreational Therapy
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course provides information and tools for students to develop the skills necessary to engage in the assessment and planning steps of the recreational therapy process. Students learn to safely conduct comprehensive clinical assessments, develop the ability to create individualized treatment plans based on client needs, and develop the skills to create evidence-informed program plans. Finally, students gain an understanding of current trends and best practices in the recreational therapy profession regarding assessment and planning. Preq: PRTM 2600 with a grade of C or higher. Coreq: PRTM 3220 and PRTM 3230.
