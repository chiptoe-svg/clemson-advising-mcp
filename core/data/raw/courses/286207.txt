PHYS 2090 - General Physics I Laboratory
1 Credit (2 Contact Hours)
Introductory laboratory course for students who are not majoring in physical science or engineering. Covers such topics as mechanics, waves, fluids, and heat. Credit for a degree will be given for only one of PHYS 1240 or PHYS 2090. Preq or concurrent enrollment: PHYS 2070.
