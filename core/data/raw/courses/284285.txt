CSM 4500 - Construction Internship and Close-Out Assessment
1 Credit (1 Contact Hour)
Documentation of 800 hours of approved experience in the construction industry with evaluation of student experiential learning. To be taken during the student’s final semester before graduation. Preq or concurrent enrollment: CSM 4110 and CSM 4530 and CSM 4610; and one of CSM 4220 or CSM 4320 or CSM 4540.
