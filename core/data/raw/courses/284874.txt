FIN 3040 - Risk and Insurance
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Studies the nature of risk and the role of insurance in risk management from individual and business viewpoints. Topics include probability, theory of the firm under uncertainty, insurance carriers and contracts, underwriting, and regulation. Preq: FIN 3060 or FIN 3110, each with a C or better.
