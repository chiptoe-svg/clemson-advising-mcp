WCIN 2100 - Film Appreciation
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Promotes understanding and appreciation of film through study of film as entertainment, cultural product, education, and political instrument. Surveys elements of formal film analysis and examines film history, film genres, and both theory and practice of filmmaking.
