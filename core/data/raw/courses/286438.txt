PRTM 4050 - Field Training II
6 Credits (18 Contact Hours)
Minimum ten weeks (400 hours) of uninterrupted, supervised work in a park, recreation or tourism management agency. Under agency supervision, students observe, organize, and implement activities, events, and programs. Students must have a 2.0 cumulative grade-point average to enroll in this course. To be taken Pass/No Pass only. Preq: PRTM 2060 and PRTM 2070; and senior standing in Parks, Recreation and Tourism Management; and consent of instructor.
