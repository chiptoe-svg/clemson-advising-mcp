EDHC 4950 - Human Capital Education and Development Honors Portfolio
1 Credit (1
Culminating experience for students in the Human Capital Education and Development departmental honors program. The course is designed to guide students in the deliberate and reflective process of compiling, refining, and synthesizing their projects and research from their honors coursework into a comprehensive, professional-quality honors portfolio.
  To be taken Pass/No Pass only. Preq: Enrollment in the Human Capital Education and Development departmental honors program.
