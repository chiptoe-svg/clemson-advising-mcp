POSC 3720 - Political Culture of East Asia
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to political culture that commonly characterizes East Asian countries, with emphasis on political subcultures of different nations, and on the analysis of the mutual influence between politics and culture. Preq: Sophomore standing.
