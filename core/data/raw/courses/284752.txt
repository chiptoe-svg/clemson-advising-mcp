ENGR 1070 - Programming and Problem Solving I
1 Credits (1 Contact Hours)
Students formulate and solve engineering problems using MATLAB; estimate answers for comparison to computed solutions; read, interpret and write programs, instructions and formatted output; evaluate and compose conditional statements; and debug. Includes Honors sections. Preq: ENGR 1020 with a C or better; or preq or concurrent enrollment in ENGR 1060 with a C or better.
