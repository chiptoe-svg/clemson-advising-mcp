PRTM 3290 - Recreational Therapy and Older Adults
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course examines concepts and theories related to aging and aging populations; common health conditions and health disparities experienced by older adults; and the role of recreational therapists working with aging populations in various service settings. Preq: PRTM 3220 and PRTM 3230 and PRTM 3260 and PRTM 3270, each with a grade of C or higher. Coreq: PPRTM 3310 and PRTM 3970 and PRTM 4220.
