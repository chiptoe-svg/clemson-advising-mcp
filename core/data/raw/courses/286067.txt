NURS 4500 - Adult Critical Care
3 Credits (6 Contact Hours)
Focuses on the biological, psychological, philosophical and sociocultural influences on complex health problems related to acute, critical and traumatic health conditions. Applications of the concepts in ventilator management, hemodynamic monitoring and implementation of practice alerts in the critical care population in an acute and critical care setting. Preq: Admission to RN/BS program.
