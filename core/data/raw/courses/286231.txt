PHYS 4010 - Senior Thesis
1-3 Credits (1-3 Contact Hours)
Semi-original theoretical, experimental, or computational research project performed under the direction of a faculty member. Fields available include astronomy, astrophysics, atmospheric physics, biophysics, high energy physics, relativity, solid state physics, and statistical mechanics. Includes Honors sections. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits. Preq: Nine credits of physics at the 3000 or 4000 level.
