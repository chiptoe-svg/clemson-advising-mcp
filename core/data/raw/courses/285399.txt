IE 3600 - Industrial Applications of Probability and Statistics I
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduces central concept that overall system performance can be improved by taking uncertainty into account, especially through the reduction of variability. Specific industrial applications, such as decision analysis, reliability and probabilistic inventory models, are emphasized. Preq: MATH 2060 with a C or higher.
