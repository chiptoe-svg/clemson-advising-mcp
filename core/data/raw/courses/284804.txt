ENT 4610 - Directed Research in Entomology
1-3 Credits (1-3 Contact Hours)
Development of a senior thesis based on a research problem in a selected entomological area. Emphasis is on integrating the knowledge gained in the student’s program with the results of the research project. May be repeated for a maximum of three credits. Preq: Senior standing and consent of instructor.
