LS 1610 - Turkey Hunting
1 Credit (3 Contact Hours)
Exposes students to the skills, techniques, and history of turkey hunting. Students learn gun and hunting safety; shotgun, muzzleloading, and archery hunting techniques; tracking; and basic calling techniques.
