AVS 3320 - Veterinary Terminology
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Students strengthen their competencies in veterinary terminology. Emphasis is placed on word construction, definition, spelling, pronunciation, and application of terms specific to the fields of veterinary medicine and sciences.
