PLPA 3100 - Principles of Plant Pathology
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Introduction to plant diseases caused by biotic agents and abiotic disorders, recognition of symptoms and signs, types of plant pathogens, diagnosis, disease development, economics, disease management, and effects of plant diseases on human welfare and the environment. Preq: BIOL 1110; or BIOL 1040 and BIOL 1060. Coreq: PLPA 3101.
