FNR 4520 - Environmental Justice in Natural Resources
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
In this class, it is assumed everyone has the right to live in a clean environment with resources managed with their best interests in mind. This course examines the history of social justice as it relates to natural resource management, the current state of the world in terms of environmental justice, and how these issues are approached from different perspectives. Areas of consideration include hunting, logging, recreation areas, and wildlife management areas, among others. Preq: Junior standing.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
