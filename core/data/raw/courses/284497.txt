EDEC 4200 - Early Childhood Science
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Students develop knowledge, skills, and attitudes needed to foster science education among young children. Emphasizes teaching strategies and techniques appropriate for young children (birth to age eight), understanding the unique learning needs of special populations, and integrating science across the curriculum. Preq: EDEC 3360 and admission to the professional level. Coreq: EDEC 4201.
