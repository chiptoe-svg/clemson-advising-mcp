AL 3580 - Introduction to Student Athlete Development
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Student-athlete development has emerged as a specialized field within the athletic professional landscape. This course is designed to educate students on the inner workings and day-to-day happenings of a student-athlete development staff. Preq: Sophomore standing.
