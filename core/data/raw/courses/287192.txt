ENGR 1100 - Engineering Success Skills
2 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Introduction to a variety of topics critical to students’ success in college and the engineering profession. Topics include time management, goal setting, test taking, campus resources and policies, teamwork, and study habits. Students are given opportunities to discover and practice many procedures, techniques, and tips. Preq: Membership in the General Engineering Learning Community.
