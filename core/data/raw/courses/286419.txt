PRTM 3610 - Sport and Recreation Facility Management
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
An overview of the management of sport and recreation facilities and venues. Topics discussed and applied include planning models, project management principles, financing capital projects, human resource considerations, risk management and legal liability, and current issues and trends in facility management. Preq or concurrent enrollment: PRTM 2540.
