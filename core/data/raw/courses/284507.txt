EDEL 3100 - Arts in the Elementary School
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduces skills, theories and practices used to integrate visual arts, drama, music and dance in the elementary classroom. Emphasizes philosophies associated with art education, content information, curriculum for diverse learners, and use of tools, media, materials and techniques. Preq: ED 1050 and sophomore standing and admission to the Early Childhood Education major, the Early Childhood and Special Education major, the Elementary Education major, or the Special Education major.
