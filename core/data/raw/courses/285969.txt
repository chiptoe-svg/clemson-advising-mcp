MUSC 3250 - CU Carillonneurs
1 Credit (2 Contact Hours)
Group study in playing the 47-bell University carillon. One two-hour meeting each week for which a minimum of two hours of individual practice is required. Participation in a recital is required. Students are expected to have musical keyboard experience.
