HLTH 3250 - Infectious Diseases and Exposure Management
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Introduction to communicable diseases commonly seen worldwide, and methods used to identify, treat, control and prevent these infections. Students examine how individuals with an infectious disease are a special population that requires a different level of care when being treated within a health system or within the community. Preq: Junior standing. Coreq: HLTH 3251.
