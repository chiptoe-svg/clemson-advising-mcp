AGRB 3020 - Economics of Farm Management
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Economic principles underlying the organization and operation of agricultural firms and related business enterprises. Particular emphasis is placed on management aspects of the farm as a production unit. Preq: AGRB 2020 with a grade of C or higher or ECON 2110 with a grade of C or higher.
