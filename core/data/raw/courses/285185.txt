HIST 1730 - The West and the World II
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Surveys the history of the West in modern times, from the late 17th century to the present. Particular emphasis is placed on Europe’s interaction with non-western societies. Through cross-cultural comparisons, European history is placed in global context. Includes Honors sections.
