NURS 1020 - Nursing Success Skills
2 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Introduction to a variety of topics critical to a student’s success as a Nursing major and future professional. Topics include time management, goal setting, test taking, campus and nursing department resources and policies, critical thinking, and diversity. Students have the opportunity to discover and practice many procedures, techniques, and tips, as well as apply principles learned to their future career in nursing. Preq: Nursing major.
