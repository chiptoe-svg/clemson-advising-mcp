PKSC 2040 - Plastics, Glass, and Metal Packaging Materials and Containers
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Course covers the manufacturing of plastics, glass, aluminum, and steel used by the packaging industry. It also covers all the packages and packaging components made using these materials. Preq: PKSC 1020 and PKSC 2020 and Packaging Science major. Coreq: PKSC 2060.
