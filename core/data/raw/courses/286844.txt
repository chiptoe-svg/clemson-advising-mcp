BIOE 3470 - Transport Processes in Bioengineering
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction of fundamental engineering principles to analyze energy, mass and momentum transport in biomedical systems. Emphasis is placed on the development and solution of mathematical equations to describe transport processes and their application to understand how various parameters influence biomedical system behavior. Preq: BIOE 3210 and BIOE 3460 and MSE 2100.
