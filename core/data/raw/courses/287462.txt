GEOG 4500 - Geographies of Food
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
An examination of the various challenges associated with the production and consumption of food. Topics include population growth and the current food supply, industrialized production systems, alternative production systems, trade in agriculture, labor in the food industry, food health and safety, food deserts, and the specific geographies of several commodities. Preq: Sophomore standing.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
