NURS 1400 - Fostering Innovation in Nursing
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Nursing students are empowered to create innovative solutions to local, national, or global healthcare problems. The course challenges students to think critically about the ever-changing field of healthcare while developing global solutions for the benefit of healthcare providers and patients alike. Students are introduced to design thinking and technology in the delivery of health care. The course covers existing healthcare applications, emerging trends, and forecasts future needs.
