HLTH 3800 - Epidemiology
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduces epidemiological principles and methods used in the study of the origin, distribution, and control of disease. Health majors are given enrollment priority. Includes Honors sections. Preq: STAT 2300 or STAT 3090; and at least one 2000-level HLTH course.
