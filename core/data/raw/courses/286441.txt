PRTM 4090 - Methods of Recreation Research I
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Analysis of the principal methods of recreation research, the application of descriptive statistics to recreation research, and the development of a research proposal. Students must have a 2.0 cumulative grade-point ratio to enroll in this course. Includes Honors sections. Preq: Junior standing.
