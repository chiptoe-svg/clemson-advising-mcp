PA 3990 - Internship
1-6 Credits (2-12 Contact Hours)
Provides performing Arts majors an opportunity to apply technical, managerial, and artistic concepts in a performing arts environment through preplanned, preapproved, faculty-supervised internships. Minimum of 45 hours of work per credit hour. To be taken Pass/No Pass only. May be repeated for a maximum of nine credits. Preq: PA 2790 and consent of instructor.
