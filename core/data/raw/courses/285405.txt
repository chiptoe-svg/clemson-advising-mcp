IE 4040 - Creative Inquiry Research
1-3 Credits (2-6 Contact Hours)
Research experience promoting reasoning, critical thinking, ethical judgment, communication skills, and an understanding of the scientific method and engineering design. These applied/basic research experiences are usually undertaken with a team under the mentorship of a faculty member or advanced graduate student. To be taken Pass/No Pass only. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits.
