YDP 3450 - Creative Activities for Youth
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course examines the use of various creative activities in youth programs. Students explore the cognitive strengths of various creative activities, how to integrate creativity into youth programs, the importance of creative activities in community identity, and the availability of community resources. Preq: Youth Development Studies minor.
