HON 3510 - Honors London Internship
1-6 Credits (3-18 Contact Hours)
Internship must be with an approved corporate, government, non-profit or other appropriate organization or setting in connection with the Honors College’s Semester Study Abroad Program in London, UK. Preq: Junior standing; and consent of instructor; and membership in the Clemson University Honors College.
