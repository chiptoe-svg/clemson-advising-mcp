ENGR 1640 - Engineering Programming
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Continuation of topics introduced in ENGR 1520 or ENGR 1530. Students formulate and solve engineering problems using computer applications. Coverage includes conditional statements, iteration, and recursion using looping structures. Various forms of technical communication are emphasized. Preq: ENGR 1520 or ENGR 1530 with a grade of C or higher; and membership in the General Engineering Learning Community. Coreq: ENGR 1641.
