ENGL 4230 - Topics in Writing Fiction
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Examines the craft, technique and stylistic choices of one or more contemporary fiction writers. Emphasizes the practice of writing fiction. Preq: ENGL 3450 or ENGL 3460 or ENGL 3470 or ENGL 3480.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
