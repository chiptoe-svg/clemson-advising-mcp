PCID 3190 - Professional Communication for Leadership
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course emphasizes professional communication as the foundation of effective leadership. Students learn to design and deliver workplace communication in scenarios such as team management, executive decision-making, and advocacy. Special attention is given to the challenges and opportunities of gendered communication. Assignments include scenario-based leadership projects, advocacy pieces, recommendation reports, and a professional branding portfolio to prepare students for leadership roles.
