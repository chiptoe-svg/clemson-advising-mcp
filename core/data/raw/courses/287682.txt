ENSP 2020 - Introduction to Experimental Environmental Science
4 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Fundamental principles of environmental science are considered with emphasis on data and analysis. Types and sources of data include scales from laboratory to repository. Experimental methods demonstrate intersections among resources, ecology, energy, and wastes. Coreq: ENSP 2021.
