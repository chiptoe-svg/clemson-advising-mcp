BIOE 4270 - Clinical Laboratory and Regulatory Management
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Explores the administrative and creative needs of a Clinical Diagnostic Laboratory, including regulation and compliance, logistical networks, needs assessment of the patient base, communicating with help care providers, potential clinical career paths and visits to institutions that participate in research and clinical activities. Preq: Sophomore standing in the College of Agriculture, Forestry and Life Sciences, or the College of Engineering, Computing and Applied Sciences, or the College of Science.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
