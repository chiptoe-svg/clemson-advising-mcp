ART 3290 - Digital Object Design
3 Credits (6 Contact Hours)
This course introduces students to the basics of Computer Aided Design and Computer Aided Manufacturing. Expanding on principles of 3D design and sculpture, this course expands student studio practice to creatively utilize Digital Fabrication. Preq: ARCH 2520 or ART 2190.
