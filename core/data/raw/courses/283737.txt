BE 2100 - Introduction to Biosystems Engineering
1 Credit (3 Contact Hours)
Overview of topics and engineering application areas that comprise the biosystems engineering profession. Significant emphasis is also given to development of oral and written communication skills needed by the engineering professional and application of engineering fundamentals to biological systems. Preq: ENGR 1020; and either MATH 1060 or MATH 1070.
