PRTM 3980 - Creative Inquiry in Parks, Recreation and Tourism Management III
1-4 Credits (1-3 Contact Hours)
In consultation with and under the direction of a faculty member, students pursue scholarly activities individually or in teams. These creative inquiry projects may be interdisciplinary. Arrangements with mentors must be established prior to registration. May be repeated for a maximum of eight credits. Coreq: PRTM 3981.
