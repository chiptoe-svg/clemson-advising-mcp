ENGR 1490 - Introduction to Engineering
2 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Introduction to the engineering profession and engineering disciplines, highlighting the industries based in South Carolina, for the purpose of assisting students in their selection of an engineering major. Professional, ethical and societal issues appropriate to engineering are introduced. Various forms of technical communication are emphasized.
