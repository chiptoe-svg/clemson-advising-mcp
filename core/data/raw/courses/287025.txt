PSYC 3770 - The Psychology of Group and Team Dynamics
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
The psychology of groups and team dynamics explores topics of group and team development, decision-making, communication, conflict resolution, and leadership. Knowledge of group and team dynamics developed from active participation in applied problem-solving activities results in students possessing teamwork skills highly sought by organizations today.
