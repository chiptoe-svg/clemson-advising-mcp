MATH 3140 - Foundations of Mathematics for Middle Level Teachers
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Study of number systems, with emphasis on whole number, rational number, integer, and real number operations and properties, and their links to algebra. Includes number theory, prime and composite numbers, decimals and percents, divisibility, common factors, multiples and applications. Students develop flexibility with multiple representations of numbers estimation and mental computations. Preq: Middle Level Education major.
