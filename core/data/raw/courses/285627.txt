LS 2120 - Belly Dance II
1 Credit (2 Contact Hours)
Designed to build on the dance and musicality skills developed during Introduction to Belly Dance, this course also introduces students to choreography, additional dance styles, improvisation, and the use of finger cymbals when they participate as a member of a percussion section. Preq: LS 2110.
