ENGL 3870 - Clemson Literary Festival
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Students prepare the annual Clemson Literary Festival. Activities include reading contemporary literature and planning the festival at every stage. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits. Preq: ENGL 2020 or ENGL 2120 or ENGL 2130 or ENGL 2140 or ENGL 2150; and Junior standing.
