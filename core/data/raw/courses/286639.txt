SPAN 3820 - Spanish-Language News
1 Credits (1 Contact Hours)
Weekly discussions of Spanish-language news articles in the foreign press with an emphasis on politics and on the connections among political, economic, social, and cultural trends. Emphasizes Spanish vocabulary as well as cross-cultural contrasts with the United States. May be repeated for a maximum of three credits. Preq: SPAN 2020.
