HORT 3100 - Growing Landscape Plants
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Principles, technologies, and techniques of landscape plant production and growth including environmental control and manipulation, water, nutrient and pest management, scheduling, propagation, and plant problem diagnostics. Emphasizes herbaceous ornamentals along with significant woody landscape plants. Preq: HORT 1010. Coreq: HORT 3101.
