MUSC 3720 - Men’s Chorus
1 Credit (3 Contact Hours)
Ensembles: devoted to the musical training of ensemble members through reading and rehearsal of appropriate music. Public performances are given in addition to the minimum rehearsal time. This course may be repeated for credit with a maximum of 16 hours ensemble credit allowable toward a degree.
