NUTR 4550 - Human Nutrition and Metabolism II
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Concepts of metabolism fundamental to understanding human nutrition are examined. Bioenergetics related to the metabolism of vitamins and minerals, physical activity, and hormonal responses are discussed. Preq: BIOL 2220 and NUTR 4510. Preq or concurrent enrollment: BIOL 2230.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
