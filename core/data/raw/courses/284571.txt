EDSC 4480 - Teaching Internship in Secondary Social Studies
9 Credits (27 Contact Hours)
Supervised observation and teaching in cooperation with a mentor teacher in a secondary public school. Students design, implement, and critically reflect upon curricular and instructional practices in public secondary social studies classrooms. Taught spring semester only. Preq: EDSC 4280 and admission to the professional level. Coreq: EDSC 4580.
