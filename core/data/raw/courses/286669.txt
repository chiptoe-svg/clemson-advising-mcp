SPAN 4990 - Special Topics
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Study of timely or special topics in Spanish. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits, but only if different topics are covered. Preq: At least six credits of 3000- or 4000-level SPAN coursework.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements
