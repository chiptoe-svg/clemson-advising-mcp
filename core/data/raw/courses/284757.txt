ENGR 1160 - Engineering Graphics and Computer-Aided Design
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Students use 2-D and 3-D drawings to visualize and analyze engineering problems. 2-D applications include site plans, contour maps, watershed, floodplains, road design, and architectural drawings. 3-D applications include models, orthographic views, and rapid prototype. Credit toward a degree will be given for only one of ENGR 1150, ENGR 1160, ENGR 2080, ENGR 2090, or ENGR 2100. Coreq: ENGR 1161.
