PKSC 4800 - Medical Packaging
3 Credits (6 Contact Hours)
This course explores medical device packaging in depth, focusing on regulatory requirements, sterilization methods, materials selection, and the design of functional, safe packaging solutions. Students learn about industry standards (FDA, ISO), packaging technologies, and emerging innovative and sustainable packaging trends. Through hands-on projects and case studies, students develop practical skills in designing medical packaging, considering factors like product protection, sterility, and compliance. The course includes a final project where students design and present their medical packaging solution. Preq: PKSC 2010.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
