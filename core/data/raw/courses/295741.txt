CPSC 4450 - Graph Representation Learning and AI Applications
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course presents fundamental concepts of graph representation learning and their application in AI systems. Topics include graph neural network theory, heterogeneous graphs, knowledge graph reasoning, graph foundation models and explainable AI methods for graph models. Preq: CPSC 2120; and MATH 2100 or MATH 3110.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
