BIOL 4060 - Introductory Plant Taxonomy
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to the principles of plant systematics, nomenclature, and classification. Includes Honors sections. Preq: BIOL 1040 and BIOL 1060; or BIOL 1110. Coreq: BIOL 4070.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
