NURS 3180 - Multidisciplinary Approach to End-of-Life Care
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Integrates principles of care to increase comfort at the end of life, presented within a framework that encompasses the physical, psychosocial, and spiritual dimensions of an individual. Coursework also includes ethical and legal issues related to advance directives, reimbursement, and regulatory topics. Preq: PSYC 2010 or SOC 2010.
