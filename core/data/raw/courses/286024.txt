NURS 3070 - Family Nursing in the Community
4 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Bridge course for registered nurse students that focuses on nursing care of families across the lifespan in the context of the community. Major emphasis is on practice activities to assist individuals in achieving or maintaining wellness in the family, home, and community environments. Coreq: NURS 3071 and NURS 4250.
