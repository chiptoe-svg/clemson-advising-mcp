NURS 4060 - Issues in Professionalism
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Analysis of the development of professional nursing. Consideration of educational issues, legal and economic issues, health policy, leadership, cultural variations, and the influence of values in ethical decisions and nursing practice. Preq: Admission to RN/BS program.
