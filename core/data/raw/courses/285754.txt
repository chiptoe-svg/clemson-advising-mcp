ME 4020 - Mechanical Engineering Capstone
3 Credits (1 Contact Hour)
Creative application of general engineering knowledge in solving an open-ended design problem provided by a sponsor typically external to the University. Progress is evaluated by a faculty jury. Students present results to the jury and sponsor through written reports and oral presentations addressing University written/oral competency goals. Preq: ME 3040 and ME 3330 and ME 4010, each with a grade of C or higher. Coreq: ME 4021.
