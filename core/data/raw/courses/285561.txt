LAW 4060 - Sports Law
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Provides awareness of sport-related legal issues. Topics include contracts, torts, arbitration, mediation, criminal liability, intellectual property, gender equity, disabilities, drug testing, and professional and amateur organizations. Preq: LAW 3220.
