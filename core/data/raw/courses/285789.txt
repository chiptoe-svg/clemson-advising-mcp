MGT 3100 - Applied Business Statistics
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Quantitative methods of the management scientist with applications to business problems. Topics include descriptive vs. inferential statistics, probability theory, distributions, sampling, regression analysis, correlation analysis, analysis of variance, and chi-square using business datasets. Includes Honors sections. Preq: STAT 2300.
