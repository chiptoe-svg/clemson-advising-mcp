NUTR 4510 - Human Nutrition and Metabolism I
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Concepts of metabolism fundamental to understanding human nutrition are examined. Bioenergetics and the metabolism of carbohydrates, lipids and amino acids are discussed. Includes Honors sections. Preq: Food Science and Human Nutrition major or Food Science minor; and NUTR 2030; and one of BCHM 3050 or BCHM 4060 or BCHM 4230.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
