EDSP 4970 - Secondary Methods for Individuals with Disabilities
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Preparation for working with students with mild/moderate disabilities in secondary schools. Focus is on literature, methods, and materials for providing instruction in transition, self-determination, knowledge within content areas, functional skills, and integration into the community. Offered fall semester only. Preq: EDSP 4910; and admission to the professional level. Coreq: EDSP 4920.
