EDSP 4810 - Directed Teaching in Special Education: PK-5
6 Credits (18 Contact Hours)
Comprehensive course providing a full-time semester-long experience for preservice teaching candidates who plan to teach in early childhood special education. This course provides teaching experience under the supervision of university and school district personnel. Preq or concurrent enrollment: EDSP 4920 and EDSP 4930 and EDSP 4940 and EDSP 4970. Coreq: EDSP 4950.
