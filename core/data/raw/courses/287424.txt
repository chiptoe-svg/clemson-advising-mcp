MSE 4170 - Innovations in Sustainable Energy Materials
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Explores science and engineering issues that underlie advanced energy systems for the transportation, industry/manufacturing, buildings and electrical sectors of the economy. Preq: MSE 2100.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
