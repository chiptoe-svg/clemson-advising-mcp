HORT 3080 - Sustainable Landscape Design, Installation and Maintenance
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Landscape planning of gardens using environmentally sensitive design, construction, and maintenance practices. Survey skills to obtain user perception and preference and environmental measurement skills are introduced. Offered fall semester only. Preq: HORT 1010.
