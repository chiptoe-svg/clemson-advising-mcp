EDSC 3270 - Practicum in Secondary Science
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Pre-service secondary science teachers gain both content and pedagogical knowledge by observing and reflecting upon the classroom practices of selected in-service high school science teachers. Preq: Admission to the professional level. Coreq: EDSC 3271.
