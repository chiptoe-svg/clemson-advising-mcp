BIOL 4490 - Field Ecology
4 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduces students to the practice of field ecology in terrestrial, aquatic, and/or marine habitats. Emphasis is placed on field techniques, organism adaptations, regional conservation issues, and effects of human disturbance. Includes Honors sections. May be repeated for a maximum of eight credits, but only if different topics are covered. Preq: Consent of instructor. Coreq: BIOL 4491.
