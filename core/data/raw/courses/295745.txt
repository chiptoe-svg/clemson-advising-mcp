CPSC 4750 - Introduction to Quantum Computing
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course offers a structured exploration of the foundational concepts and emerging applications of quantum computing, beginning with the principles of quantum mechanics relevant to computation, including quantum states, gates, circuits, and system modeling. Key quantum algorithms and strategies for error mitigation and correction are covered via hands-on projects. Preq: CPSC 2120 with a grade of C or higher and MATH 3110.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
