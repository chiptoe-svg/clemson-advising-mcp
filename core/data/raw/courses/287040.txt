EDML 3260 - Practicum in Middle Level Mathematics
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Intensive field-based practicum experience. Pre-service middle-level mathematics teachers gain content and pedagogical knowledge by observing and reflecting upon the classroom practices of selected in-service middle school mathematics teachers. Students collaborate with mentor teachers and university instructors in conducting focused observations, tutoring individual students, and leading instructional activities. Preq: Admission to the professional level. Coreq: EDML 3261.
