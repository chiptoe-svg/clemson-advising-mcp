LARC 2990 - Creative Inquiry in Landscape Architecture II
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
In consultation with and under the direction of a faculty member, students pursue scholarly activities individually or in teams. These creative inquiry projects may be interdisciplinary. Arrangements with mentors must be established prior to registration. Preq: LARC 1990 and consent of faculty member/mentor.
