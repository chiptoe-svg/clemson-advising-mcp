CSM 4540 - Construction Capstone
3 Credits (6 Contact Hours)
Students develop a capstone project that entails the knowledge obtained in all previous courses in the Construction Science and Management Program. Students must take the capstone course at Clemson University. Preq: Construction Science and Management major. Preq or concurrent enrollment: CSM 4530.
