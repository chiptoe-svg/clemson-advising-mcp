AGED 4820 - Advanced Educational Applications of Microcomputers
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Provides students with the knowledge and skills needed to apply microcomputer technology to the utilization and generation of educational software in accordance with sound educational principles. Preq: AGED 4800 or EDF 4800. Coreq: AGED 4821.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
