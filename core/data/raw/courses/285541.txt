LARC 4720 - South Carolina’s Landscapes: Then and Now
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course investigates South Carolina’s designed and cultural landscapes. It addresses the human impacts and settlement patterns, the state’s natural and physical environments, and focuses on South Carolina’s landscape legacy of the built environment. Preq: Junior standing.
