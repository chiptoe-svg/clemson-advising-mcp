LANG 4620 - Borders
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Cross-cultural inquiry into representations of physical and non-physical borders. Provides a theoretical framework in which various forms of borders, limits, and boundaries can be studied through literature and other artistic media. Preq: Junior standing.
