LANG 4200 - France and the Francophone World
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Selected masterpieces of French and Francophone Culture are considered within their historical and cultural context. All readings and instruction are in English. No knowledge of the foreign language is required. Includes Honors sections. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits. Preq: Sophomore standing.
