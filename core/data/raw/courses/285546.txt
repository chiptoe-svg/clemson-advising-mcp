LARC 4940 - Landscape Architecture Honors Research
2-3 Credits (2-3 Contact Hours)
Independent, student-generated research on a preapproved topic conducted under the supervision and weekly guidance of a faculty member. Second in a sequence of three required courses for students enrolled in Departmental Honors Program. Written interim report and presentation to faculty and honors students are required before the end of the semester. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits. Preq: LARC 4910 and membership in the Clemson University Honors College.
