EDEC 3040 - Practicum in Early Childhood Settings IV
1 Credit (3 Contact Hours)
Experiences in early childhood settings that provide opportunities for working with young children in a variety of settings. Practicum IV focuses on first-third grade classrooms in public school settings. This experience emphasizes the upper grade levels of early childhood education. Preq: EDEC 3030 and admission to the professional level.
