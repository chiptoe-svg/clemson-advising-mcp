WCIN 4720 - Introduction to Editing and Post-Production
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course focuses on the basics of post-production with an emphasis on the personnel and management processes associated with turning raw footage into finished film, and includes hands-on experience. Preq: Sophomore standing.
