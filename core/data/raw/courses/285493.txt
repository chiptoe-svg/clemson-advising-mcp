LANG 3420 - Sacred and Profane Bodies
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Cross-cultural inquiry into the ambivalence surrounding female sexuality implicit in images of women and, in particular, the division of women into “earthly” and “divine” categories. Preq: Junior standing.
