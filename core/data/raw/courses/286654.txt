SPAN 4160 - Spanish for International Business II
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Study of more complex business vocabulary, cultural concepts, and environment of Hispanic markets. Social, political, and economic issues related to Spanish-speaking countries and their current economies in global marketing. Economic geography of Hispanic countries, company organization, management, banking, investment, goods and services, and marketing. Preq: SPAN 3160.
