PRTM 2080 - Profession and Practice of Recreational Therapy
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course introduces students to the professional field of recreational therapy, including the foundational concepts of recreation and leisure, the history and evolution of the recreational therapy profession, and the role of recreational therapists working with diverse populations in health and human service settings.
