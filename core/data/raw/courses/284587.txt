EDSP 3730 - Characteristics and Instruction of Individuals with Intellectual Disabilities and Autism
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
In-depth study of the etiology, assessment procedures, learning and behavioral characteristics, and effective instructional strategies related to the education of individuals with intellectual disabilities and autism. Students participate in a field experience throughout the semester. Preq: Sophomore standing. Coreq: EDSP 3731.
