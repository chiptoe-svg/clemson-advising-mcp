ENR 4140 - Plant Identification and Systematics
4 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Study of taxonomy, phylogeny, and morphology of vegetation, with an emphasis on flowering plants. Students learn plant identification skills and evolutionary relationships among plant species. Preq: BIOL 1030 and BIOL 1040; or BIOL 1100 and BIOL 1110. Coreq: ENR 4141.
