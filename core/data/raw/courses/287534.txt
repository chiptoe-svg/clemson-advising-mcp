FIN 4070 - Analysis of Fixed Income Securities
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Analysis of fixed income securities. Topics include fixed income markets, types of fixed income securities, interest rate yield curves, fixed income valuation, credit analysis, and credit risk management. Preq: FIN 3050 with a grade of C or higher.
