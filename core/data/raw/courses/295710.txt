FOR 4280 - Wood Anatomy and Identification
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Study of macroscopic and microscopic anatomical features present in the woody tissue, and the influence of wood properties and use. Includes laboratory identification of commercial timber species. Preq: Junior standing. Coreq: FOR 4281.
