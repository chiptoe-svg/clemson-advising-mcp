HLTH 4780 - Health Policy Ethics and Law
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Critical examination of the legal and ethical dimensions of public health policy formation and change and how legal, ethical, and policy considerations influence health services administration and delivery. Preq: Junior standing and Health Science major or Language and International Health major.
