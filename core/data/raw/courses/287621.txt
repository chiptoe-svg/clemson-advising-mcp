PRTM 3310 - Recreational Therapy and Intellectual and Developmental Disabilities
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course examines concepts and theories related to intellectual and developmental disabilities. Key terminology, the underlying etiology of these disabilities, strategic intervention planning, and the role of recreational therapists working with individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities are covered. Preq: PRTM 3220 and PRTM 3230 and PRTM 3260 and PRTM 3270, each with a grade of C or higher. Coreq: PRTM 3290 and PRTM 3970 and PRTM 4220.
