HLTH 4790 - Financial Management and Budgeting for Health Service Organizations
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Overview of basic principles of budgeting and financial management and analysis for health services organizations. Techniques for financial management are provided with an emphasis on health services environments. Preq: HLTH 2030.
