PRTM 3210 - Recreation Administration
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Analysis of the internal organization of a recreation department dealing with finances and accounting, records and reports, publicity and public relations, state and federal legislation, staff organization, coordination of community resources. Students must have a 2.0 cumulative grade-point average to enroll in this course. Includes Honors sections.
