FR 3200 - Studies in French Theatre
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Explores representative texts of French theater, based on period, genre, or theme. Class materials consist of the primary texts of the plays as well as secondary theoretical readings. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits if topics vary. Preq: FR 3050.
