ED 2010 - Practicum in Early Childhood and Special Education Settings I
1 Credit (3 Contact Hours)
Experiences in early childhood and special education settings that provide opportunities for working with young children in a variety of settings. This course builds on foundations of early childhood coursework by making connections between theory and practice. Preq: ED 1050.
