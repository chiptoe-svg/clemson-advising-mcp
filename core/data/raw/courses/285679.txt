MATH 1180 - Mathematics for Elementary School Teachers II
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Simple probability and descriptive statistics are reviewed. Two- and three-dimensional geometry including polygons, polyhedra and their properties; congruence, similarity, and constructions; coordinate system; standard measurement, area, surface area, volume; and motion geometry are explored. Content, according to State standards, is taught with appropriate methodology for teaching K-6. Preq: MATH 1170. Coreq: MATH 1181.
