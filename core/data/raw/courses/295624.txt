ECE 2050 - Control and Automation Laboratory
1 Credit (2 Contact Hours)
Explores the use of industrial controllers for automation, measurement and instrumentation. Emphasizes laboratory skills, data analysis, communication skills and safety in the context of industrial processes. Preq: ECE 2020 and ECE 2110, each with a grade of C or higher.
