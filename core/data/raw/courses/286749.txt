WCIN 4620 - World Documentary
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduces students to the history, theory and form of documentary practice in international context. Through a selection of representative works, students examine the ethical, legal, aesthetic and social concerns inherent in the genre. Preq: ENGL 3570.
