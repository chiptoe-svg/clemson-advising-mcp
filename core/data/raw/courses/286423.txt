PRTM 3650 - Community Sport Practicum
3 Credits (6 Contact Hours)
Students gain practical experience in community-based recreational sport management to aid in discovery and application of PRTM core and recreational sport management course content within a municipal/community-based recreation agency or equivalent setting. To be taken Pass/No Pass only. Preq: PRTM 3600 and PRTM 3610 and PRTM 3620.
