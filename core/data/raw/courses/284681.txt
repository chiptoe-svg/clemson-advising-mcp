ENGL 4990 - Practicum in Writing
1-6 Credits (2-12 Contact Hours)
Students apply their knowledge of concepts and principles to a substantive project involving writing, editing and/or publication activities. Emphasis is on practical experience in print, digital and online forums for academic, private or public readership. To be taken Pass/No Pass only. May be repeated for a maximum of nine credits. Preq: ENGL 2020 or ENGL 2120 or ENGL 2130 or ENGL 2140 or ENGL 2150; and Junior standing.
