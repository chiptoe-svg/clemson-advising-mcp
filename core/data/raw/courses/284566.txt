EDSC 4280 - Teaching Secondary Social Studies
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Development of instructional practices and materials appropriate for secondary social studies; familiarization with curriculum materials; includes field experiences in local schools in preparation for student teaching. Includes Honors sections. Taught fall semester only. Preq: Second semester Junior standing; and admission to the professional level; and ED 1050; and ED 3010 or ED 3200; and EDF 3020; and EDF 3350; at least 18 hours of social studies coursework and a minimum grade-point average of 2.75. Preq or concurrent enrollment: EDLT 4980. Coreq: EDSC 4281.
