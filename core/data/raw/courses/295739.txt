BIOL 3170 - Barcoding Biodiversity
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to the basic principles and concepts of DNA barcodes, with an emphasis on using barcodes to infer taxonomy, evolutionary relationships and ecology among different groups of organisms. Preq: BIOL 1030 or BIOL 1100. Coreq: BIOL 3180.
