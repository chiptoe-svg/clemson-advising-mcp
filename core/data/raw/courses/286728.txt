THEA 4800 - Advanced Scene Study for Actors
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Students interpret and perform in complex plays written in heightened styles and integrating period movement into the various genres and styles of plays throughout major periods of theatre history. Styles include Elizabethan, Comedy of Manners, and early Realism. Preq: THEA 4790. Coreq: THEA 4801.
