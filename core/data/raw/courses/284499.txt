EDEC 4300 - Early Childhood Mathematics
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Examination of theories and methods of teaching mathematics in terms of how young children develop mathematical thinking. Topics include problem solving, current issues, diversity, current technologies, reflective teaching, and applications of math in everyday life. Preq: General Education mathematics requirement; and admission to the professional level; and MATH 1150 and MATH 2150 and EDEC 4500.
