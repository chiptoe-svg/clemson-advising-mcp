HIST 2140 - Introduction to Public History
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
An introduction to the study of public history and the role memory plays in shaping our understanding of the past, and an overview of the different ways historians engage and work with publics. This course covers some of the major debates and theoretical discussions occurring in the field of public history and teaches students how to apply their knowledge and research skills to work with and/or for publics. Topics of discussion may include: shared authority, exhibit design, historic preservation, archives, historic tours, mobile apps, podcasts, monuments, video games, and documentaries.
