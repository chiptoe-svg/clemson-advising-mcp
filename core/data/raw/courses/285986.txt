MUSC 3610 - Marching Band
1 Credit (3 Contact Hours)
Ensembles: devoted to musical training of ensemble members through reading and rehearsal of appropriate music. Public performances are given periodically in addition to the minimum rehearsal time. Offered fall semester only. This course may be repeated for credit with a maximum of 16 hours ensemble credit allowable toward a degree.
