ECAS 1900 - Global Challenges: Service Learning I
1-3 Credits (1-3 Contact Hours)
Individual or group projects in engineering, computing and/or applied science with a focus on service learning and humanitarian projects. Projects may be interdisciplinary and involve analysis, design and/or implementation. Instruction in methods, tools and equipment is included when appropriate. May be repeated for a maximum of nine credits.
