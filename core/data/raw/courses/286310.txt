POSC 3100 - Political Science Internship
1-3 Credits (1-3 Contact Hours)
Off-campus internship for at least one semester equivalent. No more than three hours from POSC 3050, POSC 3100, POSC 3110, POSC 3120, POSC 3130, POSC 4090, POSC 4100 may be applied toward a Political Science major, minor or a Global Politics minor. No more than six hours from POSC 3100, POSC 3110, POSC 3120, POSC 3130 may be applied toward any other degree. May be repeated for a maximum of three credits Preq: Consent of instructor.
