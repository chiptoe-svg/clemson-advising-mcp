EDEC 3030 - Practicum in Early Childhood Settings III
1 Credit (3 Contact Hours)
Experiences in early childhood settings that provide opportunities for working with young children in a variety of settings. Practicum III focuses on childcare settings for children from birth to age four. This experience allows students to understand the developmental needs and capabilities of very young children. Preq EDEC 2020 and admission to the professional level.
