SPAN 4420 - Translation Seminar
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Focuses on the theory and practice of English-Spanish translation. Students learn the multi-faceted skills of translators, essential concepts in translation, and the professionalization and ethics of translation in the English-Spanish language pair. Preq: At least three credits of 3000-level SPAN coursework.
