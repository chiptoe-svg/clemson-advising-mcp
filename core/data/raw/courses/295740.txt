BIOL 3180 - Barcoding Biodiversity Laboratory
1 Credit (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to the basic field and lab techniques necessary for sample collection, sequencing of DNA barcodes and barcode analysis, as well as comparison of barcode results to more traditional methods for taxonomic identification and construction of phylogenetic trees. Preq: BIOL 1030 or BIOL 1100. Coreq: BIOL 3170.
