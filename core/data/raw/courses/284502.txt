EDEC 4600 - Critical Issues in Early Childhood Education
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course offers an in-depth examination of current and critical issues in early childhood education. Topics include ethical decision-making, assessment in early childhood settings, professionalism, working in various educational contexts, and meeting the needs of all learners. Meets Read to Succeed requirements. Preq: EDEC 4500 and admission to the professional level.
