ECE 2090 - Logic and Computing Devices Laboratory
1 Credit (2 Contact Hours)
Introduction to designing, building, simulating and testing digital logic circuits. Topics include SSI and MSI ICs; general combinational circuits; adders, decoders and multiplexors; general sequential circuits; shift registers, counters and memory; logic simulation, timing diagrams, hardware description language (HDL), microarchitecture, computer organization, and assembly language programming. Includes Honors sections. Preq: ECE 2010 with a grade of C or higher. Preq or concurrent enrollment: ECE 2720 with a grade of C or higher.
