CPSC 4170 - 3D Game Programming: Algorithms and Techniques
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduces students to important concepts, algorithms, and techniques of 3D game programming. Topics include game engines, character controllers, motion graphs, and artificial intelligence for games. Preq: CPSC 4050 with a grade of C or higher or CPSC 4160 with a grade of C or higher or DPA 3070 with a grade of C or higher.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
