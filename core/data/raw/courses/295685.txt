GC 3630 - Brand Leadership and Experience
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course explores the intersection of brand management and organizational culture. Students learn how effective brand leadership aligns with and shapes organizational culture, and how culture, in turn, influences brand identity. The course covers various aspects of branding, leadership, and culture, and analyzes case studies from diverse industries. Preq: GC 3700.
