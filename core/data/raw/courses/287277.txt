CSM 4310 - Residential Construction Practices
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Exploration into key topics related to best practices of residential construction, including high performance design and construction standards, building green homes, universal design, and concepts of aging in place. Preq: CSM 2030.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
