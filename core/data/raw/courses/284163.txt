COMM 1800 - Introduction to Cross-Cultural Communication
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introductory course designed to provide an overview to intercultural communication questions stemming from the growing diversity and interconnectedness of the world. The United Nations Sustainable Development Goad of good health and wellbeing, gender equality, and reduced inequalities are addressed. Students are challenged to learn about the ways people from different cultural backgrounds think, communicate and behave based on the value systems and worldviews that ground them.
