MGT 4150 - Business Strategy
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Capstone course for seniors. Various methods are used in analyzing complex business problems, requiring students to integrate their knowledge of all areas of business. Student participation and written and oral communications are stressed. Students must take this course at Clemson University. Includes Honors sections. Preq: FIN 3060 or FIN 3110; and MGT 2010; and MKT 3010; and Senior standing.
