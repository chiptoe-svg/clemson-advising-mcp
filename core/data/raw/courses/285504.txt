LANG 3970 - Creative Inquiry in Language
1-4 Credits (1-4 Contact Hours)
Under the direction of one or more faculty mentors, students focus on a special research topic, individually or in teams. Creative Inquiry may include disciplinary, interdisciplinary, or community-engaged research projects. Arrangements with mentor must be established prior to registration. May be repeated for a maximum of nine credits to continue a project or to propose a new line of inquiry. Preq: Sophomore standing.
