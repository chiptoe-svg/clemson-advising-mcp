HIST 1220 - History, Technology, and Society
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Topics in the history of technology with emphasis on how technology affects society and how society shapes technology. Emphasis is on 19th and 20th century America, but some material from other periods of Western Civilization and other world regions may be discussed. Includes Honors sections.
