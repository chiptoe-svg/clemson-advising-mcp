HIST 3360 - The Indian Ocean World
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to the connected histories of Africa, the Middle East, and Asia from the ancient era to the present using the Indian Ocean as a historical unit of analysis. Themes include trade, religious and cultural exchange, slavery, and empire. Preq: Sophomore standing.
