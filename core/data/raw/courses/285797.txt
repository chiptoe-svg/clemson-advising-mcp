MGT 4000 - Management of Organizational Behavior
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Provides management students with a framework for understanding how behavior within business organizations is managed. Particular emphasis is on integrating management theory with recent developments in the behavioral sciences with distinct management applications. Theory, research, and business applications are considered. Preq: MGT 2010.
