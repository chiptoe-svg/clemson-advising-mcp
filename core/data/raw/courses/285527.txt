LARC 3620 - Design Implementation II
3 Credits (1 Contact Hours)
Advanced landscape architecture construction methods and construction documents, including site information gathering, analysis, site grading and drainage, cut and fill, principles of stormwater management, sustainable land management related to implementation, materials research and use, sustainable planting strategies, site demolition and construction management. Includes explorations in appropriate graphic communication techniques. Preq: Consent of instructor. Coreq: LARC 3621.
