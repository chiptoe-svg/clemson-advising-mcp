ECE 2120 - Electric Circuits II Laboratory
1 Credit (2 Contact Hours)
Emphasizes measurement techniques in AC steady-state circuits and comparison to theoretical predictions. Two-port network methodology and transfer functions are studied experimentally and related to analysis using transform techniques. Preq: ECE 2020 and ECE 2110, each with a grade of C or higher. Preq or concurrent enrollment: ECE 2620 with a grade of C or higher.
