CH 1010 - General Chemistry
4 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to the elementary concepts of chemistry through classroom and laboratory experience. Emphasizes chemical reactions and the use of symbolic representation, the mole concept and its applications and molecular structure. Credit toward a degree will be given for only one of CH 1010 and CH 1050. Includes Honors sections. Preq: A score of 60 or higher on the Clemson Mathematics Placement Test (CMPT). Preq or concurrent enrollment: MATH 1020 or MATH 1040 or MATH 1060 or MATH 1080 or MATH 2060 or MATH 2080 or STAT 2300. Coreq: CH 1011.
