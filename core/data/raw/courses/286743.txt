WCIN 4550 - History of Cinemas
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Close study of the development of a specific national or regional cinema (e.g., Hollywood, European, Chinese, African, Middle Eastern) in terms of its aesthetic, theoretical and sociopolitical dimensions. Cinematographic and storytelling techniques in Western and non-Western filmmaking forms are compared. Preq: ENGL 3570 or WCIN 3570.
