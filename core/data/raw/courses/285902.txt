ML 3010 - Advanced Leadership I
4 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Study of leadership focused on decision making, planning, communicating, and executing. Addresses motivational techniques, the role of a leader, and performance assessment. Provides students with leadership management tools and methodology. Students are responsible for training, developing, and mentoring Basic Course students. Students apply learned techniques in leadership laboratory. Preq: ML 2020 or ML 2100. Coreq: ML 3011.
