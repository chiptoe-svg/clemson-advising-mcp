PHYS 2900 - Physics Research
1-3 Credits (1-3 Contact Hours)
Individual research project in any area of experimental or theoretical physics or astronomy supervised by a physics or astronomy faculty member. Project need not be original but must add to students’ ability to carry out research. Students must have a 3.0 minimum grade-point average to enroll in this course. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits. Preq: Consent of instructor.
