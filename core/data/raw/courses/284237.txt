CPSC 3220 - Introduction to Operating Systems
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Detailed study of management techniques for the control of computer hardware resources. Topics include interrupt systems, primitive level characteristics of hardware and the management of memory, processor, devices, and data. Preq: CPSC 2120 and CPSC 2310, each with a grade of C or better; or ECE 2230 and ECE 2720, each with a grade of C or better.
