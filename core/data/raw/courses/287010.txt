WFB 4610 - Quantitative Ecology
4 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Students are introduced to the theory and techniques of building statistical models to evaluate community- and population-level responses by animals to environmental conditions and human exploitation. A secondary goal is to improve student writing and communication of these tools to managers and the public. Preq: STAT 2300 and WFB 3000. Coreq: WFB 4611.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
