LARC 2620 - Design Implementation I
3 Credits (1 Contact Hours)
Basics of landscape architecture construction methods and construction documents, including site information gathering and analysis, basic site grading and drainage, cut and fill, principles of stormwater management, and sustainable land management related to implementation. Includes explorations in hand and computer graphic techniques used in construction drawings. Preq: Consent of instructor. Coreq: LARC 2621.
