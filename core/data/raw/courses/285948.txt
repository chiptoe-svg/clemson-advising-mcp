MUSC 1530 - Applied Music for Majors
1 Credit (1 Contact Hour)
Individual study in vocal or instrumental performance (voice, woodwinds, brass, strings, percussion or keyboards). One 45-minute lesson each week. Jury required. Applied music fee is assessed. May be repeated for credit with departmental approval of differing performance media. Preq: Performing Arts major (Music Concentration) and consent of instructor, based upon qualifying audition.
