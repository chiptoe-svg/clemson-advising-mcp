HLTH 2500 - Population Health and Fitness
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course provides an overview of the interrelationship that exists between the holistic health status of populations, health-related fitness, physical activity behavior, and psychosocial determinants of behavior change. An emphasis is placed on assessing and promoting these concepts among individuals and populations at risk for health inequities/disparities.
