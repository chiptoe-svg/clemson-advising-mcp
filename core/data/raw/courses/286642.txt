SPAN 3930 - The Hispanic World: Latin America (Honors)
1 Credits (1 Contact Hours)
One-hour independent study to allow honors students to pursue supervised research on a topic related to Hispanic American history, politics, geography, economics, social institutions, or artistic movements. Preq: Membership in Calhoun Honors College. Preq or concurrent enrollment: SPAN 3080.
