SPAN 4010 - New Spanish Fiction
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Study of selected readings by popular emerging and established authors of Spain, with emphasis on current cultural trends. Readings include, but are not limited to, detective novels, regional fiction, and fiction from marginalized groups in Spain. Preq: At least six credits of 3000- or 4000-level SPAN coursework.
