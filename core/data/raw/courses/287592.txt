PA 1500 - Performing Arts Seminar
1 Credit (1 Contact Hour)
Provides opportunities for students to experience performance showcases by fellow students, faculty, and guest artists. A diverse roster of guest speakers provides insight into the performing arts industry and gives students a head start on professional networking. To be taken Pass/No Pass only. May be repeated for a maximum of eight credits.
