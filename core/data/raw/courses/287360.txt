JUST 4480 - Moot Court
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course simulates the appellate court process. Students learn how to analyze law and cases, construct effective arguments about the law in the context of case problems, engage in oral argument, and construct legal briefs. Preq: Junior standing.
