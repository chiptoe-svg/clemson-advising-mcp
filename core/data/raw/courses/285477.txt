JAPN 4900 - Classical Japanese
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Examination and analysis of premodern Japanese texts. Special emphasis is on the grammar and syntax of the classical language, its divergence from and influence upon the modern idiom. All coursework is conducted in Japanese. Preq: JAPN 3060.
