HIST 3050 - The United States in the Jazz Age, Depression, and War: 1918-1945
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Starting at Armistice Day, 1918, course analyzes the dawn of the age of mass consumption and mass communication, the Crash of 1929, Franklin Roosevelt and the New Deal, the gathering war clouds in Europe and Asia, and the Great Crusade of World War II. Preq: Sophomore standing.
