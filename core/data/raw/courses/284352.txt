ECE 3110 - Electronics I Laboratory
1 Credit (2 Contact Hours)
Measurements and characteristics of electronic devices and circuits; use of manual and automated instruments to acquire data; oral and written engineering reports. Preq: ECE 2120 and ECE 2620 and MATH 2080 and PHYS 2210, each with a grade of C or higher. Preq or concurrent enrollment: ECE 3200 with a grade of C or higher.
