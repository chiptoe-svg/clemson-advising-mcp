NURS 4160 - Concepts in Transcultural Nursing
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Focuses on transcultural nursing concepts, theory and practices in order to provide culturally congruent nursing care. Culture care beliefs, values and practices of specific cultures are analyzed based on Leininger’s Culture Care Theory, using the ethnonursing method and research findings.
