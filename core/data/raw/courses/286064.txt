NURS 4250 - Community Nursing
4 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Consideration of health promotion activities for groups within the community with emphasis on community assessment, screening, and health teaching/counseling. Practice activities are related to health promotion in population groups and nursing care of homebound clients. Laboratory settings include homes, schools, industries, public health department, and other community agencies. Preq: Admission to RN/BS program. Coreq: NURS 3070 and NURS 4251.
