POSC 4710 - Russian Politics
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Comprehensive examination of the Russian Federation since the fall of the Soviet Union. The successes and failures of democratic transition are analyzed, with topics covering political participation, organized crime and corruption, center-periphery conflict, and ethnic/religious unrest. Preq: Sophomore standing.
