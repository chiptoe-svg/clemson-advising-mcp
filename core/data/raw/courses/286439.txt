PRTM 4070 - Personnel Administration in Parks, Recreation and Tourism Management
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Study of personnel administration practices in recreation agencies, including employee selection, training, motivation, rewards, evaluation, and legal issues related to personnel and supervision. Students must have a 2.0 cumulative grade-point average to enroll in this course.
