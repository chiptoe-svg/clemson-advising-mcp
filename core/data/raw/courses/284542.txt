EDLT 4630 - Teaching Reading and Writing to Elementary Multilingual Learners
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Within a framework of the dimensions of cross-cultural knowledge and knowledge of theories and principles related to second language acquisition, participants develop understanding of the reading and writing processes and instructional models, strategies and tools that offer supportive learning for multilingual learners (MLs). Designed to meet Read to Succeed state requirements.  Preq: EDLT 4600 and admission to the professional level.
