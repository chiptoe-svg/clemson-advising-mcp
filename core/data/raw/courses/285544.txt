LARC 4910 - Honors Research Methods for Landscape Architecture
1-3 Credits (1-3 Contact Hours)
Students investigate various research methodologies in landscape architectural design or related areas and apply to student generated project(s). Students generate a proposal for Landscape Architecture Honors Research. Preq: Junior standing and membership in the Clemson University Honors College and consent of Department Honors Program Advisor.
