IE 4870 - Industrial Safety
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Recognition and prevention of hazards; recognition and control of hazardous materials; developing and managing a safety program; designing inherently safe equipment and workplaces. Preq: MATH 1020 or MATH 1060 or MATH 1070; and junior standing.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
