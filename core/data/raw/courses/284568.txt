EDSC 4440 - Teaching Internship in Secondary English
9 Credits (27 Contact Hours)
Interns design, implement, and critically reflect upon instructional units and teaching practices in supervised secondary English classes. Interns must provide evidence of performance that meets national and state teaching standards for secondary English. Taught spring semester only. Preq: EDSC 4240 and admission to the professional level.  Coreq: EDSC 4540.
