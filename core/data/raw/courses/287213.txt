ECE 4580 - Algorithms for VLSI Design Automation
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to computer-aided design (CAD) algorithms for designing very large scale integrated (VLSI) circuits. Emphases include Application Specific Integrated Circuits (ASICs) and VLSI design flow. Preq: ECE 3200 with a grade of C or better.
