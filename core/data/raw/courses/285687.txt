MATH 2080 - Introduction to Ordinary Differential Equations
4 Credits (4 Contact Hours)
Introduction to the study of differential equations and their application to physical problems. Topics include exact, series, and numerical solutions; solutions by means of Laplace transforms; and solutions of systems of differential equations. Includes Honors sections. Preq: MATH 2060 with a C or better.
