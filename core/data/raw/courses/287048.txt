EDML 4260 - Teaching Middle Level Mathematics
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Development of instructional practices and materials for teaching middle level school mathematics and familiarization with middle level mathematics curriculum materials. Includes field experiences in local schools. Preq: EDML 3260 or EDML 3270; and admission to the professional level.  Coreq: EDML 4261.
