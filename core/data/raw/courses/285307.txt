HLTH 4110 - Health of At-Risk Children and Families
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Analysis and evaluation of multiple determinants and contexts related to the health of families and children. Elevated risks related to poverty, food insecurity, foster care, incarceration, homelessness, emotional and/or physical trauma or abuse, drug or alcohol misuse, mental health, behavioral issues, disability, medical fragility, and multiple special needs are addressed. Health maintenance and early intervention strategies are emphasized.
