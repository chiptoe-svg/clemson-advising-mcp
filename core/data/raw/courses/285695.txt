MATH 3150 - Advanced Topics in Mathematics for Elementary Teachers
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Course builds and expands upon content from previous elementary mathematics courses. Covers investigation of two- and three-dimensional shapes; scale and scale factor; ratio and proportional reasoning; relationships between perimeter, area, surface area and volume; relationships between fractions, decimals, and percents. Open to Elementary, Early Childhood, and Special Education majors only. Preq: MATH 2160.
