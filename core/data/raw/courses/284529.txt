EDF 3340 - Child Growth and Development
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to child development, birth to age 12. Heavy emphasis is placed on the physical, social, emotional, and cognitive characteristics. Includes a minimum of six observation-participation visits to an elementary/middle/high school. Includes Honors sections. Preq: Sophomore standing and a 2.0 minimum grade-point average.
