FDSC 4080 - Food Process Engineering
4 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Study of basic engineering principles and their application in food processing operations. Emphasizes the relation between engineering principles and fundamentals of food processing. Preq: Food Science and Human Nutrition major or Food Science minor; and CH 1020 and FNPS 2140; and one of MATH 1020 or MATH 1060; and one of PHYS 1220 or PHYS 2000 or PHYS 2070. Coreq: FDSC 4081.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
