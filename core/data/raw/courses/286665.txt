SPAN 4910 - Hispanic Narrative Fiction (Honors)
1 Credits (1 Contact Hours)
One-hour independent study to allow honors students to pursue supervised research on the socio-political climate under Franco’s dictatorship, with emphasis on contemporary literary theory. Preq: Membership in Calhoun Honors College. Preq or concurrent enrollment: SPAN 4060.
