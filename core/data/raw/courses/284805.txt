ENT 4900 - Practicum
1-4 Credits (1-4 Contact Hours)
Supervised entomological learning opportunity providing highly individualized experiences to complement other programs and courses. Must be prearranged at least two months in advance. Students must file written reports midway during enrollment period and at its conclusion and must appear for oral evaluation at the end of the period. Preq: Junior standing and consent of instructor.
