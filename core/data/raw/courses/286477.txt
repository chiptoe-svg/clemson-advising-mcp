PSYC 3300 - Motivation
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Various aspects of motivation are considered by studying physiological, emotional, and environmental influences on behavior. Orientation is empirical rather than theoretical with emphasis on pertinent research, applications, and measurement of motives. Preq: PSYC 2010.
