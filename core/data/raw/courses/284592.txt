EDSP 4900 - Teaching Writing to Students with Disabilities
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course prepares candidates to use data-based decision making to provide effective instruction and strategies in writing to students with or at risk of disabilities. Meets Read to Succeed state requirements. Preq: Admission to the professional level. Preq or concurrent enrollment: EDSP 3730 and EDSP 4910.
