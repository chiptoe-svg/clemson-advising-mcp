EDML 3240 - Practicum in Middle Level Language Arts
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Students explore theoretical ideas in a field-based practicum. Working both individually and in collaboration with selected mentor teachers, students practice planning instruction, observing students and teachers in the classroom, and reflecting on the experience. Preq: Admission to the professional level. Coreq: EDML 3241.
