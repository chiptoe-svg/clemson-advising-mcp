PSYC 4880 - Theories of Psychotherapy
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Survey of alternative theories of psychological treatment for behavioral and emotional disorders. Various theoretical assumptions, techniques, and applications of each approach are examined and compared, and case examples are considered. Preq: PSYC 3100 and PSYC 3830.
