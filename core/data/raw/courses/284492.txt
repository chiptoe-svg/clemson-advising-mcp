EDEC 2020 - Practicum in Early Childhood Settings II
1 Credit (3 Contact Hours)
Experiences in early childhood settings that provide opportunities for working with young children in a variety of settings. Practicum II focuses on second and third grade classrooms in public school settings. Practicum II emphasizes building relationships with families of young children. Preq: EDEC 2010.
