EDEC 2000 - Foundations of Early Childhood Education
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Philosophical and historical foundations of early childhood education, societal changes and influences, needs of young children and families, program differentiation, and future trends are examined through coursework and experiential activities. Meets Read to Succeed requirements. Preq: ED 1050 and Sophomore standing.
