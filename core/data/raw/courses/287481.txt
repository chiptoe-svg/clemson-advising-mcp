SPAN 4410 - Medical Interpreting, Ethics and Intercultural Communication
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Students learn Spanish-English medical interpretation and translation, as well as roles of healthcare interpreters and ethics. Students participate in experiential learning and serve as cross-cultural liaisons. The course qualifies as one of the requirements to take the CCHI or NBCMI national exam. Preq: SPAN 3150.
