ECE 3120 - Electronics II Laboratory
1 Credit (2 Contact Hours)
Design and characterization of functional circuits using solid-state devices; use of manual and automated instruments for measurements; statistical analysis of data; preparation of engineering reports. Preq: ECE 3110 and ECE 3200, each with a grade of C or higher. Preq or concurrent enrollment: ECE 3210 with a grade of C or higher.
