HON 1920 - First-Year Seminar: Social Science
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
First-year honors students study the foundations of human thought and behavior and use social science concepts and evidence to explain human actions or behaviors in the past, the present, and/or the future. Topics vary. Preq: Membership in the Clemson University Honors College.
