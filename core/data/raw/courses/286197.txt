PHSC 1180 - Introduction to Physics, Astronomy, and Earth Science
4 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Integrates topics in physics, astronomy, and earth science. It emphasizes the interconnections among the various science disciplines and the practical application to experiments and activities. Preq: PHSC 1170. Coreq: PHSC 1181.
