LANG 4550 - Hispanic Film: Documentary and Feature
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Overview of theory and discourse on Hispanic film. Through lectures, discussions, and films, students become acquainted with film as a vehicle for understanding the Hispanic World. Taught in English. Films are in Spanish with English subtitles. Preq: Sophomore standing.
