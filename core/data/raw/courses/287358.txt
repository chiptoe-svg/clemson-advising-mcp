HON 1950 - First-Year Seminar: Oral Communication
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
First-year honors students learn practical skills for oral communication and exploration on the ethics of speaking. Practice in the preparation, delivery, and criticism of short speeches, in addition to a seminar style exploration of how and why we speak; what makes a good speaker; and what our obligations to speech are. Preq: Membership in the Clemson University Honors College.
