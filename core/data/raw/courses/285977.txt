MUSC 3370 - Steel Drum Band
1 Credit (2 Contact Hours)
Ensembles: devoted to the musical training of ensemble members through reading and rehearsal of appropriate music. Public performances are given in addition to the minimum rehearsal time. Rehearsals also include discussions of steel band history and performance practice. Audition required.
