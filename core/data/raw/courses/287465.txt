INT 3600 - Global Challenges Internship Full-Time
3 Credits (20 Contact Hours)
This full-time internship provides students with a professional internship experience, interacting with professionals working within a specific field or discipline. All internship settings are designed to provide meaningful and intentional experiential educational opportunities. Students are challenged with questions about career readiness, professional aspirations, and how global exchanges take place in international and stateside business and industry settings. Additionally, students explore how concepts of professional and cultural differences and customs are formed. By acquiring these skills, students learn to make independent and ethical judgements about different business settings, cultures, perspectives, and values in different parts of their professional and personal worlds. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits.
