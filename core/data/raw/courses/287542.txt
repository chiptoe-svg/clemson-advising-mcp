MGT 3090 - Special Topics in Global Management
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Examination of topics in Global Management based on the developments in the Global Manager profession and faculty interests. Emphasizes the formulation and application of these topics to support organizational leadership. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits if different topics are covered. Preq: MGT 2010.
