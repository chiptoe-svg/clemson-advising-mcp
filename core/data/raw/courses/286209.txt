PHYS 2210 - Physics with Calculus II
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Continuation of PHYS 1220. Topics include thermodynamics, kinetic theory of gases, electric and magnetic fields, electric currents and circuits, and motions of charged particles in fields. Credit for a degree will be given for only one of PHYS 2080 or PHYS 2210. Includes Honors sections. Preq: PHYS 1220. Preq or concurrent enrollment: MATH 1080 or MATH 1110.
