AL 3560 - History of Athletics in Education
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course examines the historical development of athletic programs within educational settings. Students examine the growth and popularity of athletic programs over time, as well as the barriers experienced as athletic programs developed into core aspects of educational settings. Topics include historical growth of athletics at the high school and collegiate levels; the introduction and impact of Title IX; and the evolution of coach and athletic director roles within educational settings. Preq: Sophomore standing.
