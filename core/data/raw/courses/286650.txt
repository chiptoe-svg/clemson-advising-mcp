SPAN 4070 - Hispanic Film
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Films are “read” as texts that mirror Hispanic society. Besides learning about cinematographic techniques in Spanish, topics include comparative analysis of film and literature, film as propaganda, film as “blockbuster,” and the cinematic depiction of social, cultural, and historical realities of Hispanic nations. Preq: At least three credits of 3000-level SPAN coursework.
