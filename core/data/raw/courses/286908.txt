INNO 3990 - Cross-Disciplinary Creative Inquiry
1-4 Credits (1-4 Contact Hours)
Cross-disciplinary teams engage in projects with the goal of developing each student’s ability to investigate issues; think critically; collect, analyze and interpret data; and communicate results relative to real-world applications. Arrangements with mentors must be established before registration. May be repeated for a maximum of nine credits.
