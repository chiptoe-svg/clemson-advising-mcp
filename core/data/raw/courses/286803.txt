YDP 3150 - Community and Youth Development Systems
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course focuses on organizations and systems that offer opportunities for youth to reach their potential and develop competencies and assets. These approaches include studying educational systems that foster success, community organizations that engage youth in becoming leaders and contributing members, and environments that are conducive to youth well being. Preq: Youth Development Studies minor.
