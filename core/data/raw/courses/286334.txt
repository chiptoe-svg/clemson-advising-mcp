POSC 4490 - Political Theory of Capitalism
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Examines the ethical foundations of capitalism. Focuses primarily on the major ethical theories that have supported or criticized capitalism throughout history. Topics include justification of private property, role of corporations, the profit motive, and the source of wealth creation. Includes Honors sections. Preq: Sophomore standing.
