NURS 4110 - Nursing Care of Children
5 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Focuses on child health problems and health maintenance. Emphasizes biological, pathophysiological, psychological, and sociocultural concepts related to nursing care of children with acute, critical, and chronic illnesses. Includes strategies for alleviation of illnesses, restoration of wellness, promotion and maintenance of health, growth, and development. Preq: NURS 3030 and NURS 3050 and NURS 3110 and NURS 3230 and NURS 3300, each with a C or better. Coreq: NURS 4010 and NURS 4111 and NURS 4120.
