PRTM 3950 - Professional Golf Management Seminar III
2 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Covers advanced teaching methods, golf club fitting, and player development programs. This course is designed to assist students in gaining the knowledge and skills necessary to successfully complete the PGA/PGM Training Program Level II. Students must have a 2.0 cumulative grade-point average to enroll in this course. Preq: PRTM 2830.
