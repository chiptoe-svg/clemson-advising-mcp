EES 3050 - Water and Wastewater Treatment Laboratory
1 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Laboratory exercises to accompany EES 3030 and EES 3040 in selected water and wastewater treatment operations and processes. Emphasis is on understanding of fundamental principles and operational procedures, experimental design, data analysis, use of experimental data in engineering design applications, and writing of engineering reports. Preq: EES 2020 with a C or better. Coreq: EES 3030 and EES 3040.
