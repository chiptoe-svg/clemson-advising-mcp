PRTM 4240 - Evidence-Based Lifestyle Interventions for Older Adults
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Students are equipped with foundational knowledge, skills and abilities to identify and utilize meaningful evidence-based lifestyle interventions with older adults seeking to improve overall health, social support networks, and community engagement. Preq: Junior or senior standing and consent of instructor.
