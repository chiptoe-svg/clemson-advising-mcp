PRTM 4160 - Leisure and Aging
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Examines the role of leisure services in later life, the needs of community-based and institutionalized elderly, and the development of service-delivery systems to meet those needs. Students must have a 2.0 cumulative grade-point average to enroll in this course.
