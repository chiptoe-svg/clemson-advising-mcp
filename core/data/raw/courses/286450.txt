PRTM 4550 - Advanced Program Planning
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Advanced recreation programming techniques with an emphasis on funding, outcome measurement, customer service, program development, marketing, specialized populations, and current trends and issues impacting the delivery of recreation programs. Students must have a 2.0 cumulative grade-point average to enroll in this course. Preq: PRTM 2410.
