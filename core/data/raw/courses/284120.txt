CHE 4330 - Process Design II
3 Credits (1 Contact Hour)
Continuation of CHE 4310. Principles of process development, design, and optimization are applied in a comprehensive open-ended problem carried from a general statement of the problem to detailed design and economic evaluations. Communication skills are developed through presentations, correspondence, and project reports. Preq: CHE 3300 and CHE 4070 and CHE 4310 and CHE 4430 and CHE 4500. Coreq: CHE 4331.
