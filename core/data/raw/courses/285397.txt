IE 3010 - Systems Design I
4 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to the design of industrial engineering systems. Design methodologies are introduced in the context of a design process that includes identifying user needs; developing a design specification; generating, evaluating, refining, and selecting design concepts; detail design; constructing, testing, and refining prototypes; and delivering the product to the customer. Preq: ENGL 1030 with a C or better. Coreq: IE 3011.
