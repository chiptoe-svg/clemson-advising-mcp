HIST 3350 - India in the World
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Examination of the major socio-cultural changes, political traditions, and institutions of India or the Indian subcontinent from 1500 to the present. Topics include India and South Asia’s historic role in the global economy, the Mughal empire and its place in the wider Islamic world, and British colonialism and decolonization.
