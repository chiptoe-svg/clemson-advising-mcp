ENT 2000 - Six-Legged Science
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to insects, their various relationships with humans, other animals, and plants. The general nature of this course makes it beneficial to all students regardless of specialty. Not open to students who have received credit for ENT 3010 or equivalent.
