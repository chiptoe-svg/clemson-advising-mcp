EM 2020 - Engineering Mechanics: Dynamics
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Continuation of CE 2010. Principal topics are kinematics and kinetics of particles and rigid bodies of finite size. Techniques of vector mathematics are employed. Includes Honors sections. Preq: CE 2010 and MATH 2060.
