WCIN 4740 - Film as Industry
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introductory study of the basic roles necessary to create and disseminate films in traditional and emerging media formats. Course considers business strategies and industry practices across production, distribution and exhibition. Students learn the professional and organizational structures and standards in the film industry.
