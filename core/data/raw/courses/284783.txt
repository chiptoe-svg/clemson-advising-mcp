ENR 3020 - Natural Resources Measurements
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Introduction to measurements of natural resources including land, vegetation, animal habitat, water quality and quantity, climate, and recreation. Remote sensing techniques are also introduced. May not be taken for credit by Forest Resource Management majors. Preq or concurrent enrollment: STAT 2300. Coreq: ENR 3021.
