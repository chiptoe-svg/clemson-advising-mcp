MUSC 4510 - Applied Music
1 Credit (1 Contact Hour)
Continuation of MUSC 3520, guiding students in interpretation of advanced solo and ensemble literature. Students are required to perform an appropriate solo in a student recital. Applied music fee is assessed. May be repeated for credit with departmental approval of differing performance media. Preq: MUSC 3520 and consent of instructor.
