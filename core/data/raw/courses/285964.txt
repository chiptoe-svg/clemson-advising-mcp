MUSC 3130 - History of Rock and Roll
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Comprehensive survey of rock elements, styles, and artists. Emphasizes the evolution of rock and roll including a broad examination of musical influences. Course content examines how rock and roll both reflected and influenced social issues.
