PRTM 3300 - Visitor Services and Interpretation
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduces the philosophy and principles of the art of environmental interpretation. Comprehensive survey of interpretive theory as it applies to the recreation and parks practitioner and the varying settings within the profession. Students must have a 2.0 cumulative grade-point average to enroll in this course. Includes Honors sections.
