AGM 2200 - Calculations for Mechanized Agriculture
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Enhances students’ ability to analyze and solve a wide range of problems requiring engineering technology. Laboratory periods introduce students to microcomputer hardware. Basic programming and typical applications to agricultural mechanization problems are included. Preq or concurrent enrollment: MATH 1020; and PHYS 2000 or PHYS 2070. Coreq: AGM 2201.
