EDLT 2000 - Exploring Contemporary Social Issues Through Children’s Literature
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Critical exploration of contemporary social issues to meet the global challenge of promoting inclusive societies through the use of children’s literature. Participants explore different viewpoints, identities, and life experiences through close analysis and discussion of picture books and children’s novels. Competencies with facilitating difficult dialogue among children and adults are developed.
