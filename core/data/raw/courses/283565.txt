ART 4150 - Advanced Graphic Design
3 Credits (6 Contact Hours)
Advanced studies of visual communication, with a focus on the latest theories and techniques in the field. Students engage in multimedia approaches to graphic design, including print, web, motion graphics, and environmental design. Design research and individual projects are emphasized. May be repeated for a maximum of nine credits. May be repeated for a maximum of nine credits. Preq: ART 3150.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
