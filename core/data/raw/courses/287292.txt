BIOL 4210 - Neurobiology of Disease
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Diseases of the nervous system provide unprecedented insight into how the nervous system functions under physiological as well as pathophysiological conditions. This course provides students unprecedented insight into how the brain functions by examining processes related to normal physiological states and how processes go awry in a wide range of diseases and disorders of the nervous system. Neurobiology of Disease integrates the underlying principles of development, organization, and behaviors of cells with pathophysiological states. Preq: BIOL 1040 and BIOL 1060, or BIOL 1110; and BCHM 3010 or BCHM 3050.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
