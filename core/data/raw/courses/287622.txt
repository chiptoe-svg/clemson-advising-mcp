PRTM 4710 - Understanding Professional Sport
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Students gain an understanding professional sport by applying theories, concepts, and best practices for the strategic management of organizations within the industry. Preq or concurrent enrollment: PRTM 2540.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
