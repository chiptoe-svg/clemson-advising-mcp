CRP 4180 - Innovative Planning and Partnerships: The Greenville Living Case
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Planning as an approach to urban innovation lies at the intersection of professional planning practice, government capacity, and community/public partnerships. This class looks at urban change as a function of innovative planning and supportive partnerships. Each class session is grounded in Greenville, SC, as a living case study to better understand innovate planning and community-based partnerships. Preq: Junior standing.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
