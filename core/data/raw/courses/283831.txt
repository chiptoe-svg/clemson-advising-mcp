BIOL 1230 - Keys to Human Biology
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to scientific inquiry through human biology for non-science majors. Considers biological processes occurring within humans and human impact on global biological processes. Interrelationships ultimately affecting evolution and diversity are explored. High school biology or its equivalent is highly encouraged. Credit toward a degree will be given for only one of BIOL 1220 or BIOL 1230.
