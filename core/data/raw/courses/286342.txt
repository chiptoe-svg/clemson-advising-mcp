POSC 4760 - Middle East Politics
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Comprehensive thematic and empirical analysis of the Middle East region. Issues covered include democratization, political and religious freedom, oil, the role of women, and terrorism. States analyzed include Syria, Jordan, Iran, Iraq, Saudi Arabia, Turkey, and the Gulf States. Preq: Sophomore standing.
