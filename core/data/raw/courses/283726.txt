BCHM 4930 - Senior Seminar
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Analysis and discussion of papers from the primary literature in the life sciences, particularly in biochemistry and molecular biology. Students find pertinent articles in the primary literature and present and analyze the selected reading. Students evaluate varying perspectives of stakeholders and discuss ethical concerns that arise from the peer-reviewed articles. Includes Honors sections. Preq: BCHM 3010 and GEN 3020, each with a grade of C or better; and one of BCHM 4310 or BCHM 4320 or BCHM 4360 with a grade of C or better.
