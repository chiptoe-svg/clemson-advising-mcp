CSM 4420 - Roofing Business
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
A descriptive study of the roofing industry, focusing on the business aspects of managing a roofing company, specifically, starting and sustaining a roofing construction business, leadership and communication, negotiation, sales and marketing, procurement and sourcing, and technology innovations. Preq: CSM 2030.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
