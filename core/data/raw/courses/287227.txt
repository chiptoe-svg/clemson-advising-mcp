EDHC 4220 - Effective Online Learning and Development
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course covers the creation and delivery of effective online training/instruction, with an emphasis on participant engagement, instructor presence, course facilitation, best practices, and emerging tools and methods used in online learning and development. Preq: Sophomore standing.
