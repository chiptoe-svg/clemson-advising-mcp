AGM 4000 - Senior Seminar in Agricultural Mechanization and Business
1 Credit (1 Contact Hour)
This seminar-based course focuses on developing skills in critical thinking, problem solving, professional ethics, and oral and written communication. Students develop a proposal for a capstone project that is completed in AGM 4720 the following semester. Topics in the this seminar are related to agricultural production, agribusiness and resource management. Preq: AGM 2060 and AGM 2200 and AGM 3010 and consent of instructor.
