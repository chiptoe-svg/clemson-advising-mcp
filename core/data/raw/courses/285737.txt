ME 2030 - Foundations of Thermal and Fluid Systems
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to control volumes, conservation laws of mass, momentum, and energy. Concepts of work and heat are introduced, including rate forms. Properties of pure substances. Preq: MATH 2060 and PHYS 2210, each with grade of a C or better.
