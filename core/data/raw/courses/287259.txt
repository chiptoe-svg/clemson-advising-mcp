NPL 3910 - Selected Topics in Nonprofit Leadership
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
In depth examination of topic areas and trends in the field of nonprofit leadership that warrant timely study and that are not covered in other NPL courses. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits if different topics are covered.
