PRTM 3070 - Facility Planning and Operations
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to recreation facility planning and operations processes. Design, planning, financing, construction, budgeting, personnel, operating policies and procedures, maintenance, and equipment considerations are covered. Students must have a 2.0 cumulative grade-point average to enroll in this course.
