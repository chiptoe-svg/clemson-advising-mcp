COMM 3430 - Podcasting and Audio Storytelling
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course covers the production of podcasts and other forms of audio storytelling for online distribution. Students learn to write, record, edit, and distribute audio content. Preq: Sophomore standing.
