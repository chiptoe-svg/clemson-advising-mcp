IE 4560 - Supply Chain Design and Control
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Industrial engineering aspects of supply chains, including design and control of material and information systems. Preq: IE 3600 and IE 3800.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
