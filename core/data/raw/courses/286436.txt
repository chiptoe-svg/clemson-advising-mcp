PRTM 4030 - Elements of Recreation and Park Planning
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Basic recreation and park planning principles, processes, and trends in area and facility development combine to form the basis for formulation of a relevant knowledge of planning. Students must have a 2.0 cumulative grade-point ratio to enroll in this course. Preq: Junior standing.
