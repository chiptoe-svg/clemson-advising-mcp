GEOL 2300 - The Global Ocean: A Survey of Oceanography
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Provides an overview of the fundamentals of oceanography. Topics include the biological, chemical, geological, and physical features of the global ocean. The course also addresses the role of the ocean in Earth’s climate system, discussion of the current challenges facing the ocean-climate change, overfishing, pollution-and how these changes are connected to people.
