GEN 4210 - Molecular Genetics and Gene Regulation Laboratory
2 Credits (4 Contact Hours)
Explores molecular genetics techniques (transformation, cloning, PCR, gel electrophoresis, Southern Blotting, reporter genes, gene mapping) using prokaryotic and eukaryotic organisms. Preq: BCHM 3040 or GEN 3040 with a grade of C or higher. Preq or concurrent enrollment: GEN 4200.
