GER 3160 - German for International Business I
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Spoken and written German common to the German-speaking world of business and industry emphasizing business practices and writing and translating business letters and professional reports. Cross-cultural references provide opportunity for comparative and contrastive analysis of American and German cultural patterns in a business setting. Preq: GER 2020 or GER 3100. Preq or concurrent enrollment: GER 3050 or GER 3060.
