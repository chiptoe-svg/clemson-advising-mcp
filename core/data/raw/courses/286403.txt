PRTM 3260 - Recreational Therapy and Physical Health Conditions
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course examines physical health conditions and the role of recreational therapy in treatment, planning, and intervention(s) across care settings. Preq: PRTM 3220 and PRTM 3230 and PRTM 3240, each with a grade of C or higher. Coreq: PRTM 3270.
