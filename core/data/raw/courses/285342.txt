HON 2220 - Studies in Arts and Humanities
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Exploration of music, literature, film, philosophy or another area in humanities by examining a discipline specific topic. Focus may be on a particular scholar, era or culture idea or concept specific to a particular arts/humanities discipline. May be repeated for a maximum of 12 credits, but only if different disciplines are covered. Preq: Membership in the Clemson University Honors College.
