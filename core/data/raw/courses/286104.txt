GBS 1010 - Africa and the Atlantic World
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Study of Africa and its influence on the culture and life of the people in the Americas. Traces the impact Africans have had on shaping the music, languages, dress, art, religion, and culture of contemporary Western societies.
