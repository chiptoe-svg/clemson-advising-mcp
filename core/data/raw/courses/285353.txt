HORT 2080 - Landscape Appreciation
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Deepens students’ appreciation of natural and built environments through a study of landscape elements, styles, and professions. Landscapes ranging in scale from residential to regional are critiqued, and design principles and landscape ethics are discussed.
