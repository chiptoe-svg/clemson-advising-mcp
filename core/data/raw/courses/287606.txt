BIOE 4280 - Advances in Biomolecules and Biosystems for Medical Applications
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course introduces recent discoveries in biomolecules and biosystems being used for or on the path to clinical applications. Students have opportunities to lead in-depth discussions on relevant biomolecules and biosystems, the available tools, and databases for clinical diagnostics and therapeutics needs. Preq: BCHM 3010 or BCHM 3050 or GEN 3020.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
