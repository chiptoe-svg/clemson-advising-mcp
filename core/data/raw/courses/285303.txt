HLTH 3950 - Honors Research Seminar
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Students review basic steps in the development of an honors research proposal and develop a draft of the proposal under the supervision of a faculty mentor. Students are also required to attend research presentations of senior departmental honors students. Preq: HLTH 3800 and Junior standing.
