PKSC 4210 - Special Problems in Packaging Science
1-4 Credits (1-4 Contact Hours)
Independent research investigations in packaging science related to packaging materials, machinery, design, and applications. Special emphasis is placed on organizing a research proposal, conducting research, and reporting results. May be repeated for a maximum of 15 credits. Preq: Consent of instructor.
