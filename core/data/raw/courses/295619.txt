PRTM 3340 - Backcountry Expedition and Leadership
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This intensive course is centered on a multi-day backcountry expedition trip where students apply outdoor leadership, group management, technical skills, and environmental ethics in real expedition settings. The experience emphasizes making decisions in dynamic contexts, resilience under uncertainty, and the integration of theory into practice while leading groups in wilderness environments.
