MATH 4410 - Introduction to Stochastic Models
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introductory treatment of stochastic processes, finite-state Markov chains, queueing, dynamic programming, Markov decision processes, reliability, decision analysis, and simulation. Both theory and applications are stressed. Includes Honors sections. Preq: ECE 3170 or MATH 4000.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
