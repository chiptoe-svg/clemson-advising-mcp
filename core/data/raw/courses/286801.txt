YDP 3050 - Theory and Philosophy of Youth Development Work
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course examines the philosophical, conceptual, and theoretical frameworks of positive youth development from the perspective of real-world application within developmental systems. Students explore both the distinctiveness and complementarity between problem-focused and youth development approaches to youth work, and work on building a common language for the field. Preq: Youth Development Studies minor.
