EDEC 4400 - Early Childhood English Language Arts
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Examination of language arts across the early childhood curriculum. Focus is on early literacy skills, oral language development, writing development, and process-oriented writing strategies. Writing assessment and future professional development opportunities in writing instruction are also discussed. Meets Read to Succeed requirements. Preq: Sophomore standing and admission to the professional level.
