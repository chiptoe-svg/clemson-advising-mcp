HON 3900 - Honors Leadership and Student Development Theory
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to theoretical and practical applications of student leadership on the Clemson University campus and the Honors College. Focus is on student development theory and support for specific student populations such as Honors students and Living-Learning Communities. Students develop skills that assist with leadership development, problem solving, conflict resolution, confrontation, and referral. Issues for practitioners and the effective utilization of resources available on campus are explored. Preq: Membership in the Clemson University Honors College.
