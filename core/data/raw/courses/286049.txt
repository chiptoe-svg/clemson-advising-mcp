NURS 4050 - Leadership and Management in Nursing
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Focuses on the role of the professional nurse in managing nursing care. Theories and research related to leadership, power, management, organizations, regulation, and ethics are discussed. Directed laboratory experiences are provided. Preq: Admission to RN/BS program. Coreq: NURS 4051.
