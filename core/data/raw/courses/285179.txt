HIST 1000 - History of Clemson University
1 Credit (1 Contact Hour)
Introduction to the history of Clemson University and its surroundings, emphasizing the community’s origins in a colonial and African-American context during the nineteenth century, and its foundation and modern development as a diverse community of higher education into the present. May be repeated for a maximum of four credits.
