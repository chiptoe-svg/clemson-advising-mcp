FIN 2110 - Explorations in Finance
1 Credit (1 Contact Hour)
Introduction to finance terminology, concepts, and basic problems. Provides an overview of the different areas of finance and the terminology and job opportunities related to each area. Focus is on career development and exploration. Course is geared toward students early in the financial management major. Preq: ECON 2110. Preq or concurrent enrollment: ACCT 2010.
