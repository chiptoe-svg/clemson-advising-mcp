HORT 2130 - Turfgrass Culture Laboratory
1 Credit (2 Contact Hours)
Provides hands-on activities and understanding of basic principles and techniques in turfgrass culture. Students learn all phases of turfgrass management including identification, turfgrass culture, common turfgrass pest identification and control. Preq or concurrent enrollment: HORT 2120.
