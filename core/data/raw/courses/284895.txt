FIN 4060 - Derivatives and Financial Risk Management
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Students explore key derivative contracts, such as forwards, futures, swaps, and options, and learn their role in managing financial risk across various sectors, including investment banking, corporate finance, and asset management. The course covers hedging and arbitrage strategies, derivative pricing theory, and contract structures, blending theory with practical applications that help professionals protect assets and manage market exposure. Includes Honors sections. Preq: FIN 3050 with a grade of C or better.
