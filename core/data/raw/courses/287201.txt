THEA 3780 - Acting Theorists and Approaches
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Familiarizes theatre artists with the ideas, work and approach of various influential acting theorists, such as Stanislavski, Meisner, Adler, Hagen, Bogart and others. Students analyze and apply these theories to their work. Preq: THEA 2780.
