MATH 2100 - Applied Matrix Algebra
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to the basic principles of matrix algebra with applications to the behavioral and managerial sciences. Major areas of application include linear programming, directed graphs, and game theory. Preq: MATH 1020 or MATH 1060 or MATH 1070.
