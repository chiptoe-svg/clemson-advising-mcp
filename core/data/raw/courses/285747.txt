ME 3080 - Fluid Mechanics
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Behavior of fluids at rest or in motion, including the study of fluid properties. Emphasizes a rational, analytical approach from which are developed basic principles of broad applicability to all fields of engineering. Includes Honors sections. Preq: ME 2030, and one of ME 2010 or ME 2060, each with a grade of C or better. Preq or concurrent enrollment: MATH 2080.
