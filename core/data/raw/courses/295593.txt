ECAS 1200 - Artificial Intelligence for All
1 Credit (1 Contact Hour)
An overview of the foundational concepts, practical applications, and societal implications of artificial intelligence. The course examines how AI technologies function, their influence across diverse domains, and the ethical considerations they raise. Designed to be accessible to students from all academic backgrounds, the course emphasizes critical analysis and informed engagement rather than technical proficiency.
