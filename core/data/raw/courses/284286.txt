CSM 4530 - Construction Project Management
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Study of construction business organization, methods of project delivery, field organization, policy, ethics, project management, control systems, labor management relations, and productivity. Preq: CSM 3520 and CSM 3530, and Construction Science and Management major. Preq or concurrent enrollment: CSM 4110 and LAW 3220 and MGT 3070.
