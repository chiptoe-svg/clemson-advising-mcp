GEOL 4300 - Paleoclimatology
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to paleoclimatology and Earth’s climate system. This course draws on current themes in topics that include past temperatures, ocean and atmospheric circulation, seawater and atmospheric chemistry, glaciations and ice sheets, radiative forcing, extreme events, and modes of climate variability (e.g., ENSO). This course covers the methods used in paleoclimatology, including proxies, paleoclimate modeling, and dating techniques. Material presented in this course contextualizes anthropogenic climate change. Preq: GEOL 1010.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
