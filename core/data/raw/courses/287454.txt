HP 3020 - Preserving American Architectural History
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course investigates architectural developments in the United States from the mid-fifteenth century to the present day and exposes students to the growth and change of the American architectural landscape, both geographically and aesthetically. Focusing on the basic form, construction, and ornamental attributes of buildings is important to understanding buildings as products of particular times and places. Preq: HP 2010.
