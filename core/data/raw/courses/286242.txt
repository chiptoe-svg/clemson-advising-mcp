PHYS 4520 - Nuclear and Particle Physics
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Study of our present knowledge concerning subatomic matter. Experimental results are stressed. Topics include particle spectra, detection techniques, Regge pole analysis, quark models, proton structure, nuclear structure, scattering and reactions. Includes Honors sections. Preq: PHYS 2210. 
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
