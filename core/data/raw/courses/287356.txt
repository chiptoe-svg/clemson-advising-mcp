DANC 3130 - Dance Composition II
1 Credit (2 Contact Hours)
Examines dance composition and the creative process at an intermediate/advanced level. Examines choreographic concepts as applied to a variety of dance styles. Performing Arts fee is assessed. Preq: DANC 3110.
