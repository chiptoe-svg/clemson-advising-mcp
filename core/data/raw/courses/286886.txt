ENTR 4080 - Family Business
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course provides an in-depth examination of the unique aspects of a family business, including the lifecycles and development stages, distinctive advantages, unique challenges, and specific tensions that family firms face that non-family firms typically do not. Preq: ENTR 3010.
