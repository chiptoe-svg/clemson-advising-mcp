SPAN 4030 - Spanish American Women Writers
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
In-depth study of selected literary works by Spanish American women. Representative authors are studied within their philosophical and sociopolitical contexts. Preq: At least six credits of 3000- or 4000-level SPAN coursework.
