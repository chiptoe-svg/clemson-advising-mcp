LARC 3630 - Landscape Aesthetics
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
The course introduces the landscape from the aesthetic and ethical perspectives on everyday landscapes, connecting aesthetic sensibility with commonsense morality. It also explores the poetic experiences of landscapes that transcend the limitations of image and words through expressive media. Preq: Sophomore standing.
