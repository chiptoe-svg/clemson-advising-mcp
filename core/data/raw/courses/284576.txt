EDSC 4570 - Secondary Science Capstone Seminar
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Capstone seminar accompanying supervised high school science teaching internship. Satisfies part of requirement for South Carolina secondary science certification. Offered spring semester only. Preq: EDSC 4270 and admission to the professional level.  Coreq: EDSC 4470 and EDSC 4571.
