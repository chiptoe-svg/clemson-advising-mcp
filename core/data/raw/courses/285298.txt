HLTH 3150 - Social Epidemiology
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Exploration of the current problems and issues associated with the health of population groups. The interrelationships of biological, sociocultural, behavioral, environmental, political, and economic risk factors and the health and illness patterns of those in population groups are examined. Preq: HLTH 2980.
