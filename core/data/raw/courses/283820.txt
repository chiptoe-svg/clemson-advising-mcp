BIOL 1040 - General Biology II
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Continuation of BIOL 1030 for allied science majors [science and life science majors (nursing, animal and veterinary sciences, nutrition, agriculture, etc.) OTHER than biological sciences, microbiology, biochemistry, and genetics] or those students interested in medicine. Includes an evolutionary approach to human anatomy and physiology, plant diversity, morphology, and physiology and principles of ecology. Credit toward a degree will be given for BIOL 1040 or BIOL 1110 only. Includes Honors sections. Preq: BIOL 1030 and BIOL 1050; or BIOL 1100.
