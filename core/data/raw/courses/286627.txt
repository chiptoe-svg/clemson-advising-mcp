SPAN 3040 - Introduction to Hispanic Literary Forms
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to the basic structures and elements of fiction, poetry, drama, and essay, including literary and critical theory, with readings in 19th- and 20th-century Spanish and Spanish-American literature. Preq: At least three credits of 3000-level SPAN coursework.
