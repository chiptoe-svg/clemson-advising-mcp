WFB 4620 - Wetland Wildlife Biology
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Study of wetland wildlife habitats, emphasizing classification by physical, chemical, and biological characteristics; importance of wetland habitat for management and production of wetland wildlife species. Includes Honors sections. Offered spring semester only. Preq: BIOL 4410 or BIOL 4430 or FNR 4660 or WFB 3000.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
