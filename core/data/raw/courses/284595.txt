EDSP 4920 - Mathematics Instruction for Individuals with Mild Disabilities
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Prepares students to provide explicit instruction in mathematics for individuals with mild disabilities. Students learn to assess, analyze and teach math skills systematically. Offered fall semester only. Preq: EDSP 4910; and admission to the professional level. Coreq: EDSP 4970.
