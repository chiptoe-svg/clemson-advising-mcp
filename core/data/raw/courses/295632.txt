PCID 3170 - Data Communication and Information Design
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Prepares students to perform effective visual information design with a focus on ethical data storytelling. Through research, they define audience and purpose and then design accessible visual forms that fit the task. Work progresses from critique and redesign to a client-ready applied project through iteration and testing.
