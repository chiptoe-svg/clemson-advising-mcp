GC 1050 - Application of Digital Graphics
2 Credits (6 Contact Hours)
Graphic Communications industries make extensive use of software and best practices from concept through production. This course provides a solid foundation in drawing, imaging, and layout software; packaging structure and 3D CAD; and design principles and problem solving relative to audience, need, typography, color, materials, printing, and end use. Preq or concurrent enrollment: GC 1020.
