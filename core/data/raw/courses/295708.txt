PRTM 1890 - Recreation for All Abilities
0 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
This introductory course familiarizes students with adaptive sports, recreation, and various aspects of health and wellness, and introduces recreation as a means for social skills development and a lifetime of participation in leisure activities. Students improve their physical skills necessary for employment and increase their participation in activities related to work and daily living. This course is taught by a recreational professional trained to increase participant well being through intentionally designed recreational programming. To be taken Pass/No Pass only. May be repeated six times.
