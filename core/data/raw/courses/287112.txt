MUSC 3500 - Jazz Combos
1 Credit (2 Contact Hours)
Study of the techniques and repertoire of the contemporary professional musician, enabling students to perform and function in various live events and studio recording settings. Instrumentalists and vocalists are placed in the beginning, intermediate or advanced combos, where they learn musical and interpersonal skills necessary for success. Performance opportunities depend on skill and achievement. Genres include Jazz, Pop, R&B, Rock, Folk, Singer-Songwriter, Blues, Should, Funk, and Latin.
