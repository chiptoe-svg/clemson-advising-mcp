JUST 4290 - Justice Administration
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Comprehensive evaluation of the administration of criminal justice. The course seeks to develop an understanding of how criminal justice components operate, interact with one another, and administer justice, and explores key theoretical approaches and organizational principles, models and typologies. Preq: JUST 2880 or SOC 2880.
