PRTM 2020 - Foundations of Wellbeing in Parks, Recreation, and Tourism Management
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
This course introduces concepts related to wellbeing and wellness and their intersection with parks, recreation, sport, and leisure. Dimensions of wellness and wellbeing, such as physical, emotional, social, spiritual, and intellectual, are explored. Tools and resources are identified for maintaining and improving wellbeing. Particular attention is paid to the importance of sport, recreation, and/or leisure to improving or maintaining wellbeing.
