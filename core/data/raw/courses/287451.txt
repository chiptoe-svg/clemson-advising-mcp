GLCH 3520 - The Health Impact of Abuse
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Examines abuse and its impact on society locally, nationally, and globally, applied directly to health care. Exploration of the role of healthcare professionals and researchers builds upon the emphasis on holistic care. A primary outcome of this course is a greater understanding of abuse as it relates to health care and health outcomes. Preq: Junior standing.
