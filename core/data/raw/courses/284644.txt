ENGL 2150 - Literature in 20th- and 21st-Century Contexts
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to major contemporary cultural movements via selected authors in 20th- and 21st-century literature, primarily American and British, with attention to poetry, fiction, and drama since World War II. Includes Honors sections. Preq: ENGL 1030.
