BE 4750 - Biosystems Engineering Capstone Design
3 Credits (6 Contact Hours)
Students apply engineering knowledge to develop solutions to open-ended biosystems engineering problems. Designs are evaluated by faculty and practicing engineers based on appropriate use of engineering principles, consideration of ecological, economic and societal impacts, and clear communication. A final presentation and report are required. Preq: CE 3410 with a grade of C or higher; and BE 3220, BE 4100, BE 4120, and BE 4380. Preq or concurrent enrollment: BE 4150. Coreq: BE 4740.
