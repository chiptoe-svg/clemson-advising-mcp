HLTH 3300 - International Health Perspectives and Comparative Systems
3 Credits (9 Contact Hours)
This course examines the differences in health cultures, health services administration and policy, the role of NGOs in public health, and related public health challenges and successes of various European countries in comparison to the United States healthcare system and practices. Students gain a broadened perspective on global health practices as well as cross-cultural competencies and understanding. Students spend time exploring systems in Aix-En-Provence, France, and Madrid, Spain, with an additional visit to Geneva, Switzerland, to visit the World Health Organization, the Red Cross International, and United Nations. Preq: Sophomore standing.
