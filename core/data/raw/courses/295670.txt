ART 3390 - Game Design
3 Credits (6 Contact Hours)
Foundation of game design with an emphasis on concept, planning and creation of game prototypes. Topics include history of games from tabletop to tablet, markets, mechanics, prototyping, play testing, and analysis. Students play and critically analyze modern games and create their own prototypes. Students create two physical prototypes using industry-standard tools and design concepts, and acquire a working knowledge of game engine software through the development of a digital prototype. Preq: ART 1550 and ART 2250.
