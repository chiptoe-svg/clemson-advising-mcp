ML 3020 - Advanced Leadership II
4 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Continuation of leadership study focusing on collective skills training, tactics, and small group instruction. Synthesizes various components of training, leadership, and team-building learned during the Basic Course and ML 3010. Final step in students’ progression prior to the Leader’s Development and Assessment Course. Preq: ML 3010. Coreq: ML 3021.
