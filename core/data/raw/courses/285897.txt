ML 2010 - Leadership Development I
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Study of leadership focused at the team level. Students develop leadership skills through public speaking, managing small groups, and mentoring first-year students. Skills are applied in a variety of challenging training events during leadership laboratory, including rappelling, water survival, land navigation, and team-building exercises. Coreq: ML 2011.
