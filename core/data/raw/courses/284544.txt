EDLT 4980 - Content Area Reading and Writing for Middle and Secondary Teachers
3 Credits (2 Contact Hours)
Designed for preservice teachers who are involved with field experiences prior to student teaching full time. Prepares content area teachers to teach the reading skills necessary for effective teaching of content area material. Designed to meet Read to Succeed state requirements. Preq: EDLT 4800 and admission to professional level. Coreq: EDLT 4981.
