PKSC 2060 - Analytical Techniques in Packaging
1 Credit (3 Contact Hours)
This course emphasizes data analysis of laboratory practices in sample and prototyping, designing and constructing various containers from materials, and converting practices. Data analysis best practices are also discussed. Preq: PKSC 1020 and Packaging Science major. Coreq: PKSC 2040.
