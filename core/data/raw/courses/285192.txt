HIST 2990 - Seminar: The Historian’s Craft
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Writing and speaking intensive course to introduce History majors to study of what history is and what a historian does, including historiography, research techniques, ethics of the historical profession, and forms of presentation. Preq: History major or Secondary Education: Social Studies major.
