NUTR 4210 - Special Problems in Nutrition
1-4 Credits (1-4 Contact Hours)
Independent research investigation in nutrition. Special emphasis is on developing a research proposal, conducting the research, and reporting the findings. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits, but only if different topics are covered. Preq: Food Science and Human Nutrition major and senior standing.
