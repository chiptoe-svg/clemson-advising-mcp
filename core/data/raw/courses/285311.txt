HLTH 4400 - Managing Health Service Organizations
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Provides the conceptual and theoretical foundation of management and organizational theory of health service organizations. Focuses on the role of health services managers and how they modify and maintain organizations. Preq: HLTH 2030.
