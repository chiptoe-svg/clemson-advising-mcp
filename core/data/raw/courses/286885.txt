ENTR 4040 - Venture Funding and Formation
3 Credits (3 Contact Hours)
Introduction to core topics of venture funding and formation, including funding sources, timing considerations, deal structures, and business structures. The focal perspective is that of an entrepreneur looking to secure financing and form a company for a venture pursuit. Preq: ENTR 3010.
