CSM 4220 - Construction Management Studio I
3 Credits (6 Contact Hours)
Students apply concepts of construction management in completion of an industry-sponsored project. Projects are presented in a multi-university competition to the industry judges. May be repeated for a maximum of six credits. Preq: CSM 2060.
