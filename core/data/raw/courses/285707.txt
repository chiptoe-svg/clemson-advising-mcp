MATH 4810 - Seminar in Mathematics
1-3 Credits (1-3 Contact Hours)
Attention is focused on mathematical areas in which nonroutine problems can be posed with comparative ease. Emphasis is on independent study and student use of previously acquired mathematical skills. Open to students by invitation only for a maximum of three credits. Preq: Consent of instructor.
