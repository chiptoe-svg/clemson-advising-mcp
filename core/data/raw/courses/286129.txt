PES 4030 - Soil Genesis and Classification
2 Credits (1 Contact Hours)
Study of soil morphology and characterization, pedogenic processes, soil-forming factors, and classification of soils. Offered fall semester only. Preq: PES 2020. Coreq: PES 4031.
This 4000-level course has a 6000-level counterpart. Students should refer to the Graduate Catalog for the 6000-level description and requirements.
