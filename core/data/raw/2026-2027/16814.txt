Japanese Studies Minor

Minor Description

(18 credits minimum)

A minor in Japanese Studies requires six credits of JAPN 2010 and JAPN 2020, and 12 credits of 3000- and 4000-level JAPN courses, of which at least six credits must be taught in Japanese.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Language and International Business majors whose target language is Japanese or to Modern Languages majors whose emphasis area is Japanese.
