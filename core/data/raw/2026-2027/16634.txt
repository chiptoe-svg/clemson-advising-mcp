East Asian Studies Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in East Asian Studies requires three credits selected from CHIN 3050, CHIN 3060, CHIN 3160, CHIN 3170, CHIN 3980, CHIN 4110, CHIN 4120, CHIN 4160, CHIN 4170, JAPN 3030, JAPN 3050, JAPN 3060, JAPN 3070, JAPN 3080, JAPN 3160, JAPN 3980, JAPN 4040, JAPN 4060, JAPN 4110, JAPN 4120, JAPN 4160, JAPN 4900, or JAPN 4910; and 12 additional credits selected from ANTH 4170, ANTH 4180, CHIN 3120, CHIN 3130, CHIN 4010, CHIN 4020, CHIN 4140, CHIN 4180, CHIN 4990, EAS 1230, HIST 3300, HIST 3330, HIST 3340, JAPN 4010, JAPN 4060, JAPN 4170, JAPN 4300, JAPN 4560, JAPN 4990, LANG 3100, PHIL 3120, PHIL 3130, PHIL 4140, POSC 3720, POSC 4770, REL 3130, REL 3140, and WCIN 4560. These additional 12 credits must include courses focused on both China and Japan, and at least six of these credits must be at the 4000 level.
