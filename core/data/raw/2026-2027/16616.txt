American Sign Language Studies Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in American Sign Language Studies requires ASL 3010 and ASL 3020, plus nine credits of ASL courses at the 3000 or 4000 level.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Modern Languages majors whose emphasis area is American Sign Language.
