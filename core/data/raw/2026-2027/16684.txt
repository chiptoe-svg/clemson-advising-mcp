Spanish-American Area Studies Minor

 

Minor Description

(18 credits minimum)

A minor in Spanish-American Area Studies requires the equivalent of SPAN 2020; and ECON 4100 and 12 additional credits distributed as follows:

Six credits selected from GEOG 3400, HIST 3400, HIST 3410, HIST 3420 and HIST 4400 
Six credits selected from SPAN 3080, SPAN 3110, SPAN 3820, SPAN 4030, SPAN 4220 and SPAN 4350
