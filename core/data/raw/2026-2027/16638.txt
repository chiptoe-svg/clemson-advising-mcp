Entrepreneurship Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Entrepreneurship requires ENTR 3010 and 12 additional credits selected from ECON 3210, ELE 3210, ENTR 3990, ENTR 4010, ENTR 4020, ENTR 4030, ENTR 4040, ENTR 4060, ENTR 4080, ENTR 4440, MGT 4220, MGT 4400, MGT 4440, and MGT 4970.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Management majors.
