Human Capital Education and Leadership Development Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

The minor in Human Capital Education and Development Leadership is an interdisciplinary, comprehensive degree focused on organizational learning, assessment, and development that prepares students for diverse settings across a variety of industries. The minor requires EDHC 3300 and EDHC 4320; and nine additional credits selected from EDHC 3100, EDHC 3200, EDHC 3210, EDHC 4100, EDHC 4110, EDHC 4200, EDHC 4210, EDHC 4220, EDHC 4300, EDHC 4310, and EDHC 4330.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Human Capital Education and Development majors.
