Cybersecurity Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

The Cybersecurity minor provides students the opportunity to complete a deeper examination of cybersecurity topics than is typical for a conventional degree. The minor requires a minimum of 15 credits. Students select one of two paths of study and complete all courses from that path, as well as an additional course selected from the approved cybersecurity course list. Please note that prerequisites for courses on Path I include CPSC 2120, CPSC 2150, CPSC 3220, and CPSC 3600. Prerequisites for courses on Path II include ECE 2230, ECE 2720, ECE 3170, and ECE 3220. Some courses on the cybersecurity course list may also have prerequisites. Please check course descriptions for detailed prerequisite information.

Select one of the paths below.

Path I: CPSC 2920, CPSC 4200, CPSC 4240, CPSC 4620, and one additional course selected from the cybersecurity course list.

Path II: CPSC 4200, CPSC 4620, and ECE 4490; either ECE 4380 or ECE 4400; and one additional course selected from the cybersecurity course list.

Cybersecurity Course List: CPSC 2920, CPSC 4140, CPSC 4180, CPSC 4200, CPSC 4240, CPSC 4620, ECE 4270, ECE 4380, ECE 4400, ECE 4490, ECE 4550, ECON 4220, MATH 2190, MATH 4100, MGT 3180, MGT 4360, MGT 4540, and MGT 4550 

For additional information about the Cybersecurity Minor, please visit the Cybersecurity Center website or email Harlan Russell at advising-contact@example.invalid.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Computer Science BA majors.
