Management Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Management requires 15 credits as follows: MGT 2010, MGT 2180, MGT 3180, MGT 4000, and one additional course selected from ENTR 3010, MGT 3100, or MGT 3900.

NOTES:

STAT 3090 is a prerequisite for both MGT 3100 and MGT 3900.
This minor is not open to Management majors.
