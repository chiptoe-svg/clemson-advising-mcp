Psychology Minor

Minor Description

(18 credits minimum)

A minor in Psychology requires PSYC 2010 and 15 credits from 3000- or 4000-level psychology courses. At least nine credits from courses other than PSYC 4970 and PSYC 4980 must be taken.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Psychology majors.
