Physics Minor

Minor Description

(18 credits minimum)

A minor in Physics requires PHYS 1220, PHYS 2210 and PHYS 2220 and nine additional credits selected from any 3000- or 4000-level ASTR courses and any 3000- or 4000-level PHYS courses, excluding PHYS 3990 and PHYS 4990. 

NOTE: This minor is not open to the following majors: Physics; Science Teaching - Physical Sciences Teaching Area; or Science Teaching - Physics Teaching Area.
