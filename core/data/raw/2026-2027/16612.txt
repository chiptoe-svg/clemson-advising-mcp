Adult/Extension Education Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Adult/Extension Education requires AGED 4030 and AGED 4150, and nine additional credits selected from AGED 2010, AGED 2030, AGED 4160, AGED 4230, and AGED 4800.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Agricultural Education majors.
