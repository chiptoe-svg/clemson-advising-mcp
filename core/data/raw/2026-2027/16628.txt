Cluster Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

The Cluster minor allows students a somewhat wider choice of course materials than is possible with the conventional subject-matter minor. The general requirement for the Cluster minor is 15 credits in courses at the 3000-level or higher, except where noted differently, chosen according to one of the plans below. Courses within the student’s major course rubric may not be included in the Cluster minor.

Group I - Social Sciences: anthropology, criminal justice, economics, geography, history, political science, psychology, rural sociology, sociology
Group II - Life Sciences1: biochemistry, biological sciences, genetics, microbiology
Group III - Physical Sciences1: chemistry, geology, physics (NOTE: This cluster is not open to Chemistry majors or Physics majors.)
Group IV - Engineering1: courses in all engineering majors, plus engineering mechanics and engineering graphics
 

1 No course in the 1000 series is acceptable toward the minor and not more than six hours in the 2000 series are acceptable.
