Race, Ethnicity and Migration Minor

 

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Race, Ethnicity and Migration requires 15 credits as follows:

Literature and the Arts: Three credits selected from ENGL 3530, ENGL 3540, ENGL 4030, ENGL 4190, ENGL 4340, ENGL 4430, ENGL 4560, ENGL 4820, ENGL 4830, THEA 3170, THEA 3180, or WCIN 4550.
Society: Three credits selected from ANTH 3200, GBS 1000, GBS 4300, GBS 4680, POSC 3810, RS 3010, SOC 4330, SOC 4590, SOC 4600, or SOC 4710.
History and Geography: Three credits selected from GEOG 3050, GEOG 3400, GEOG 4100, HIST 3000, HIST 3030, HIST 3110, HIST 3120, HIST 3170, HIST 3350, HIST 3370, HIST 3380, HIST 3390, HIST 3400, HIST 3940, HIST 3950, HIST 3970, or POSC 4230.
Electives: Six additional credits selected from any of the courses listed above.

Students interested in pursuing this minor are encouraged to contact the director of the Race, Ethnicity and Migration minor in the department of English.
