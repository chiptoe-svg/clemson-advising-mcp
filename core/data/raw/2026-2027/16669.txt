Packaging Science Minor

Minor Description

(18 credits minimum)

A minor in Packaging Sciences requires PKSC 1020, PKSC 2020, PKSC 2040, PKSC 3200, and PKSC 4400; and either FNPS 3680 or PKSC 4050. 

NOTES:

This minor is not open to Packaging Science majors.
Students should review the prerequisite course requirements for all courses listed above. For questions or assistance in planning a minor, students should contact the Packaging Science Minor advisor.
