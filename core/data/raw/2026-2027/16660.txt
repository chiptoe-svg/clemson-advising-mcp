Management Information Systems Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Management Information Systems requires 15 credits as follows:

ACCT 3220 or MGT 3180 
MGT 4110 
MGT 4520 
MGT 4540 
MGT 4560 

NOTE: This minor is not open to Management majors. However, a student may declare a second emphasis area, as long as no classes are double counted. Students should meet with their advisor to discuss this option.
