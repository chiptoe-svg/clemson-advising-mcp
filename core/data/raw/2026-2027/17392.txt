Neuroscience Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Neuroscience requires BIOL 4200, BIOL 4210, and BIOL 4400; and an additional six credits selected from BCHM 4400, BIOL 3150, BIOL 4080, BIOL 4090, BIOL 4700, BIOL 4750, GEN 4400, PSYC 3220, PSYC 3240, and PSYC 3330.

NOTE: Courses in this minor may have required prerequisite courses that must be completed prior to enrollment in courses that fulfill requirements for the minor.
