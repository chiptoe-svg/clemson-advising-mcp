Geology Minor

Minor Description

(16 credits minimum)

A minor in Geology requires GEOL 1010, GEOL 1030, and 12 additional credits of GEOL courses, at least nine of which must be at the 3000 level or higher.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Geology majors.
