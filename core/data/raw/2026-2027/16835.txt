Educational Interpreting Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

The Educational Interpreting minor prepares students as entry-level interpreters to work with students who are Deaf in K-12 classrooms. Coursework focuses on interpreter processing skills, continued sign vocabulary development, the transfer of meaning between languages, as well as, a specialized focus on interpreting in the educational setting. The minor requires ASL 3150, ASL 3200, ASL 3250, ASL 4200, and ASL 4250.
