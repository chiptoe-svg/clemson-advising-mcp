Park and Protected Area Management Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Park and Protected Area Management requires PRTM 2700 and PRTM 4740 and nine additional credits selected from PRTM 3200, PRTM 3210, PRTM 3300, PRTM 4030, PRTM 4300 and PRTM 4310.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Parks, Recreation and Tourism majors.
