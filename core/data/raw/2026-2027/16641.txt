Financial Management Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Financial Management requires FIN 3050, FIN 3070, FIN 3080 and FIN 3120, and either FIN 3060 or FIN 3110. Please note that both FIN 3060 and FIN 3110 have prerequisites of ACCT 2010 and a statistics course. See course descriptions for courses that currently fulfill the statistics requirement. Additionally, these statistics courses may have prerequisites of their own. All of the other FIN courses required for the minor require either FIN 3060 or FIN 3110 with a C or better as a prerequisite.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Financial Management majors.
