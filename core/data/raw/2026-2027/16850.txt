Dance Minor

Minor Description

(16 credits minimum)

A minor in Dance requires DANC 3100, DANC 3110, DANC 3120, DANC 3130, DANC 3140; three additional credits selected from MUSC 2100, MUSC 3080, MUSC 3090, THEA 3080, THEA 3090, THEA 4670, and THEA 4870; and at least six credits1 selected from DANC 2300, DANC 2400, DANC 2500, DANC 2600, and DANC 3300.

1DANC 3300 is an auditioned performance ensemble for advanced dance students, in which a variety of dance styles are studied and performed. If a student is not enrolled in DANC 3300, these six credits must be fulfilled by enrollment in courses focusing on at least three different dance styles. All 2000-level DANC classes are repeatable. Students should consult with the departmental coordinator to plan course selections.

NOTES:

Students must be enrolled in at least one 2000-level DANC class or DANC 3300 prior to declaring a minor in Dance.
Nine credits at the 3000 or 4000 level are not required for a minor in Dance, as approved by the University Undergraduate Curriculum Committee 2021.
