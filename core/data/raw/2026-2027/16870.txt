Pathways in the Humanities and Social Sciences Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

The Pathways in the Humanities and Social Sciences Minor requires 15 credits selected from the categories below.

Gateway Coursework (three credits) - Select HIST 1010, HON 1910, HUM 1010, PHIL 1010, POSC 1010, POSC 1030, or REL 1010.
Core Coursework (nine credits) - Select from ANTH 2010, ART 2100, CHIN 4010, CHIN 4990, ENGL 2120, ENGL 2130, ENGL 2140, ENGL 2150, ENGL 2160, HIST 1720, HIST 1730, HON 2030, HON 2210, HUM 3010, HUM 3020, HUM 3090, FR 2600, FR 3000, GER 2600, GER 3600, ITAL 3020, JAPN 4010, PHIL 3260, POSC 3510, POSC 3530, REL 3010, REL 3020, REL 3030, RUSS 3600, SOC 2010, SPAN 3040, THEA 3150, THEA 3160, THEA 3170, and THEA 3180. At least two of the courses must be at or above the 3000 level.
Capstone Coursework (three credits) - Select ANTH 3010, ANTH 3250, ENGL 3490, ENGL 3560, ENGL 4110, ENGL 4260, GER 4760, HIST 4370, HON 3960, HUM 3060, LANG 3480, LANG 4600, LANG 4620, PHIL 3480, PHIL 4010, POSC 4500, REL 3350, or REL 4010.

NOTE: Alternate coursework requires a course substitution.
