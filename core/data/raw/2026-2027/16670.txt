Global Black Studies Minor

 

Minor Description

(18 credits minimum)

A minor in Global Black Studies requires 18 credits as follows:

GBS 1000 
HIST 3110 or HIST 3120 
Three credits in the social sciences selected from AL 3340, COMM 3240, GBS 4550, GEOG 3030, GEOG 3400, GLCH 3510, JUST 4130, POSC 3810, POSC 4660, RS 3010, SOC 3010, SOC 3310, SOC 4440, SOC 4550, SOC 4600, and YDP 3250.
Nine credits in the humanities selected from ENGL 3330, ENGL 3530, ENGL 3540, ENGL 4190, ENGL 4820, ENGL 4830, FR 3270, GBS 1010, GBS 4300, GBS 4980, HIST 3370, HIST 3380, HIST 3390, HIST 3520, HIST 3930, HIST 3950, HIST 4370, HIST 4380, LANG 4200, THEA 3170, and THEA 3180.

Courses should be scheduled in consultation with the appropriate advisors. Students may submit a course substitution request to petition for relevant courses not listed above to count toward the minor requirements.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Global Black Studies majors.
