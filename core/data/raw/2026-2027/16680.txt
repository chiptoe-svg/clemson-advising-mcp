Religious Studies Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Religious Studies requires 15 credits as follows: REL 3000 and 12 additional credits, nine of which must be at the 3000 level or higher. Select from any REL course or ANTH 4270, ENGL 3500, ENGL 4140, HIST 3960, HIST 4720, PHIL 3030, POSC 4070, POSC 4080, POSC 4390, and SOC 4320.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Religious Studies majors.
