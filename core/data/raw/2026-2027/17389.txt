Health Services Administration Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Health Services Administration requires HLTH 2020, HLTH 2030, and HLTH 3610; and an additional six credits selected from HLTH 4400, HLTH 4600, HLTH 4780, and HLTH 4790.
