Thomas F. Chapman Leadership Certificate

Program Description

The Thomas F. Chapman Leadership Certificate program is a nine-credit program open to all Thomas F. Chapman Leadership Scholars. Courses focused on developing intellect, making courageous and ethical decisions, and leading from the heart serve as the basic requirements for the certificate. Completion of the certificate requires the following:

A total of six credits of creative inquiry courses in business-one credit each semester of BUS 2990, BUS 3990, or BUS 4990 in the sophomore, junior, and senior years.
Three additional credits selected from MGT 4050, MKT 4950, both ML 1010 and ML 1020, NPL 3000, and WS 2300. Another leadership course may be approved for substitution by the program director.
