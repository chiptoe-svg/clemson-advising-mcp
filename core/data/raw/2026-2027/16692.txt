Professional Writing Minor

 

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Professional Writing requires ENGL 3040 or ENGL 3120 or ENGL 3140 or PCID 3040 or PCID 3140; and 12 additional credits selected from the following courses, at least six of which must be at the 4000 level: ENGL 3100, ENGL 3150, ENGL 3310, ENGL 3320, ENGL 3330, ENGL 4000, ENGL 4010, ENGL 4410, ENGL 4600, ENGL 4750, ENGL 4850, ENGL 4890, ENGL 4910, ENGL 4920, ENGL 4940, and ENGL 4950.
