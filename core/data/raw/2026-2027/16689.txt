Urban Forestry Minor

Minor Description

(19 credits minimum)

A minor in Urban Forestry requires a minimum of 19 credits, six credits of which must be at the 3000 or 4000 level, selected from the following courses. Students must have a minor GPA or 2.0 or higher.

Required Courses:

BIOL 3040 or FOR 2210 
FOR 2050 or HORT 3030 
FOR 2060 
FOR 4270/HORT 4270 
FOR 4800 

Additional Courses: Select an additional four to six credits selected from the course list below.

BIOL 3080 
CRP 4010 
FOR 2270 
FOR 3080 
FOR 4330 

Note: Most of these courses have prerequisite and/or corequisite requirements. Students should refer to the course descriptions for requirements.
