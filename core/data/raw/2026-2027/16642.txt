Food Science Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Food Science requires FNPS 2140 and FDSC 4010, and nine additional credits in FDSC or NUTR courses numbered 3000 or higher.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Food Science and Human Nutrition majors.
