Industrial Design Studies Minor

Minor Description

(18 credits minimum)

A minor in Industrial Design Studies requires:

AAH 1010 or ART 2100 
ART 1520 or ART 1550 
ART 2330
nine additional credits selected from the following:
ARCH 4240, ARCH 4280, ARCH 4870. Other courses may be eligible with department approval.*
ART 2060, ART 2090, ART 2150, ART 2170, ART 2230, ART 3230, ART 3270, ART 3290, ART 4310. Special topics courses or other courses may be substituted with department approval.*
CRP 2010, CRP 4150. Other courses may be eligible with department approval.*
CSM 3990. Other courses may be eligible with department approval.*

*Any alternative course approved by the department to fulfill a requirement will require the student to submit a course substitution form.

NOTES:

Some courses are offered during summer sessions.

Majors in the College of Architecture, Art and Construction are given priority for registration in courses. Non-major students may request enrollment via waitlist.
