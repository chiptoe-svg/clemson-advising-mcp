Agricultural Business Management Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Agricultural Business Management requires AGRB 2080, AGRB 3020, and AGRB 3190, and at least two courses selected from AGRB 3090, AGRB 3510, AGRB 4020, AGRB 4080, AGRB 4090, AGRB 4520, AGRB 4560 and AGRB 4600.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Agribusiness majors.
