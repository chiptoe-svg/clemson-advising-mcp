Forest Resource Management Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Forest Resource Management requires FOR 2050 and FOR 2060, and at least ten additional credits of forestry courses at the 3000 level or higher, excluding FOR 4190 and FOR 4470.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Forest Resource Management majors or Forest Resource Management majors with an Emphasis Area in Land Surveying.
