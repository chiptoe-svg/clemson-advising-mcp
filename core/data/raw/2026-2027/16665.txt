Music Minor

Minor Description

(17 credits minimum)

A minor in Music requires the following:

MUSC 1510, MUSC 1520, MUSC 2510 and MUSC 2520 
MUSC 4150 or MUSC 4160 
Four semesters of ensemble, totaling four credits, selected from MUSC 3230, MUSC 3610, MUSC 3620, MUSC 3630, MUSC 3640, MUSC 3690, MUSC 3700, MUSC 3710 and MUSC 3720 1 
One three-credit MUSC course at the 3000 or 4000 level
One three-credit music theory course selected from MUSC 1410, MUSC 1420, MUSC 1440, or MUSC 2420 based on the placement exam.

1 Keyboard students may substitute secondary keyboard courses (MUSC 1510, MUSC 1520, MUSC 2510, MUSC 2520, MUSC 3510, MUSC 3520, MUSC 4510, MUSC 4520) or piano accompanying (MUSC 3230) for large ensemble courses. 

NOTES:

All four semesters of applied music and large ensemble must be on the student’s primary instrument.
MUSC 1420, MUSC 1440, and MUSC 2420 must be taken with their corequisite one-credit aural skills labs (MUSC 1430, MUSC 1450, and MUSC 2430 respectively).
Due to the private lesson requirement, students who have fewer than four semesters remaining before graduation will not be admitted to the Music minor.
This major is not open to Performing Arts majors whose concentration area is Music.
