Travel and Tourism Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Travel and Tourism requires PRTM 3420 and PRTM 3450; three credits selected from PRTM 4460 or PRTM 4470; and six additional credits selected from PRTM 3090, PRTM 3300, PRTM 3430, PRTM 3440, PRTM 3460, PRTM 3470, PRTM 3480, PRTM 3490, PRTM 3910, PRTM 3920, PRTM 4030, PRTM 4300, PRTM 4410, PRTM 4440, PRTM 4450, PRTM 4460, and PRTM 4470.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Parks, Recreation and Tourism majors.
