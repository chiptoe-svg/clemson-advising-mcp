Nonprofit Leadership Minor

Minor Description

(18 credits minimum)

A minor in Nonprofit Leadership requires completion of 18 credits as follows: NPL 3000, either NPL 3010 or NPL 3050, NPL 3020, NPL 3030, NPL 3040 and NPL 4900.
