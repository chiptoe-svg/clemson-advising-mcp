Astronomy Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in Astronomy requires one of ASTR 1010, ASTR 1020, or ASTR 2200; and ASTR 3020, ASTR 3030, PHYS 2210, and three additional credits selected from any ASTR course numbered 3040-4990, PHYS 3550, PHYS 4200, PHYS 4320, or PHYS 4520.

NOTE: This minor is not open to BS Physics majors with an Interdisciplinary Emphasis Area.
