Nonprofit Leadership Certificate

Program Description

The Nonprofit Leadership Certificate requires the following six courses, all of which are taught online:

NPL 3000 - Foundations in Nonprofit Leadership 3 Credits
NPL 3010 - Understanding Stakeholders for Non-Profit Organizations 3 Credits
NPL 3020 - Funding and Accountability in Non-Profit Organizations 3 Credits
NPL 3030 - Personnel Leadership in Non-Profit Organizations 3 Credits
NPL 3040 - Risk Management of Non-Profit Organizations 3 Credits
NPL 4900 - Non-Profit Leadership Preceptorship 3 Credits
