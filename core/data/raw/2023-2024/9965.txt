History Minor

Minor Description

(15 credits minimum)

A minor in History requires 15 credits in history at the 3000 and 4000 levels. Three credits at the 4000 level must be included.

NOTE: This minor is not open to History majors or Secondary Education - Social Studies (History) majors.
