Automotive Engineering Certificate

Program Description

The Automotive Engineering Certificate is a 12-credit program. Students select four courses from any 4000-level AUE courses offered. Multiple sections of AUE 4930 may be used to satisfy the certificate requirements as long as different topics are covered in each of the sections and the courses have unique titles.

Occasionally, a 6000-level course may also be approved to count towards the undergraduate Automotive Engineering certificate, provided it aligns with student learning capabilities and objectives.

For more information, please visit http://www.clemson.edu/cecas/departments/automotive-engineering/academic-programs/ae-certificate/ae-certificate-undergrad.html.

AUE (ME) 4600 - Dynamic Performance of Vehicles 3 Credits
AUE (ME) 4610 - Advanced and Electrified Powertrains 3 Credits
AUE 4030 - Automotive Project Tools and Prototyping 3 Credits
AUE 4080 - Vehicle Testing and Characterization 3 Credits
AUE (ME) 4620 - Digital Automotive Manufacturing 3 Credits
