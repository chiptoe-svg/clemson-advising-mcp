Global Politics Minor

Minor Description

(18 credits minimum)

A minor in Global Politics requires POSC 1020 and POSC 1040, and 12 additional credits selected from the lists below. At least three of these credits must be from Group I and at least three credits must be from Group II.

Group I - Comparative Politics: POSC 3710, POSC 3720, POSC 4080, POSC 4660, POSC 4710, POSC 4760, POSC 4770, POSC 4780 
Group II - International Relations: POSC 3610, POSC 3620, POSC 3630, POSC 3750, POSC 4470, POSC 4480, POSC 4560, POSC 4570, POSC 4590, POSC 4610 

With the approval of the Political Science department, a maximum of three credits from POSC 3050, POSC 3110, POSC 3130 or POSC 4100 may also be applied toward a Global Politics minor.

NOTE: This minor is not open to Political Science majors.
