Great Works Minor

 

Minor Description

(18 credits minimum)

The Great Works minor requires GW 3010, plus one course from each of the following groups. A minimum of nine credits must be at the 4000 level.

Group I - Classical Civilization: Three credits selected from ENGL 4030, ENGL 4910, ENGL 4290, HIST 3540, HIST 3550, HIST 4500 or PHIL 3110 
Group II - Post-Classical Literature: Three credits selected from ENGL 4080, ENGL 4110, ENGL 4140, ENGL 4160, FR 4000, GW 4030, SPAN 3130 or SPAN 4010 
Group III - Philosophy, Religion, and Social Thought: Three credits selected from ENGL 3500, HIST 4950, PHIL 3160, PHIL 3170, POSC 4500, REL 3010, REL 3020 or REL 4010 
Group IV - The Arts: Three credits selected from AAH 4230, AAH 4240, HUM 3010, HUM 3020, MUSC 4150, MUSC 4160, THEA 3150 or THEA 3160 
Group V - The Sciences: Three credits selected from BIOL 4860, ENGL 4340, GW 4020 or GW 4050
